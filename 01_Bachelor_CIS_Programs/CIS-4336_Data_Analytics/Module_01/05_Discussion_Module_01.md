## Scenario A — Retail Data Classification

A regional grocery chain collects the following data each day: customer loyalty card scans, point-of-sale transaction records, in-store security camera footage, and open-ended customer comment cards submitted at the service desk.

In 175–225 words, address all three of the following:

1. Classify each of the four data sources as structured, semi-structured, or unstructured. Justify each classification using specific characteristics of the source.
2. Identify one data source that would require the most preprocessing effort before analysis, and explain what preprocessing steps would be needed.
3. A store manager wants to know which product categories drove the highest revenue last quarter. Which analytics type does this question represent, and why?

## Scenario B — Healthcare Analytics Types

A regional hospital is building an analytics capability and has assigned three projects to different teams.

- Team 1 is building a dashboard that displays current bed occupancy rates, average length of stay, and 30-day readmission rates updated hourly.
- Team 2 is using historical patient data to build a model that flags patients at high risk of readmission before discharge.
- Team 3 is investigating a recent spike in hospital-acquired infections to identify the root cause.

In 175–225 words, address all three of the following:

1. Identify the analytics type for each team's project and justify your classification.
2. Explain the correct sequence of the three analytics types in the analytics maturity model — why must organizations typically develop them in a specific order?
3. Identify one data quality challenge this hospital is likely to face and explain how it could affect the analysis.

## Scenario C — Measurement Scale in Practice

A marketing analyst at an e-commerce company is reviewing a customer survey. The survey includes the following fields: customer age in years, preferred shipping speed selected from "Standard, Expedited, Overnight," product rating on a scale of 1 to 5, annual household income in dollars, and geographic region.

In 175–225 words, address all three of the following:

1. Classify each survey field by its scale of measurement (nominal, ordinal, interval, or ratio). Justify each classification.
2. The analyst proposes computing the average product rating across all customers and reporting it to leadership as "mean customer satisfaction." Evaluate this proposal using your knowledge of measurement scales.
3. For the `preferred shipping speed` field, explain what statistical operations are valid and what operations would produce misleading results. Provide a specific example of a misleading operation.
