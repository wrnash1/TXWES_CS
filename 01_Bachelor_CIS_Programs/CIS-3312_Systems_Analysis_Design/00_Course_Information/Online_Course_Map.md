# Online Course Map: CIS-3312 Systems Analysis & Design
**Target Certification:** IIBA Entry Certificate in Business Analysis (ECBA)

## 16-Week Breakdown

*   **Module 01:** Introduction to Systems Analysis and the SDLC — roles, phases, and the business analyst's position in project lifecycles.
*   **Module 02:** Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring — BA planning approach, stakeholder engagement, governance, and performance measurement (BABOK KA 2).
*   **Module 03:** Requirements Elicitation Techniques — interviews, workshops, surveys, observation, and document analysis (BABOK KA 4).
*   **Module 04:** Requirements Analysis and Documentation — functional vs. non-functional requirements, requirements attributes, traceability (BABOK KA 5).
*   **Module 05:** Use Case Modeling and User Stories — actor/use-case diagrams, user story format, acceptance criteria, and backlog management.
*   **Module 06:** Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs) and Entity-Relationship Diagrams (ERDs) — context diagrams, leveled DFDs, data modeling, normalization concepts.
*   **Module 07:** Process Modeling with BPMN — pools, lanes, events, gateways, and flow objects in BPMN 2.0 notation.
*   **Module 08:** Feasibility Analysis and Cost-Benefit Analysis — technical, financial, organizational feasibility; NPV, ROI, payback period.
*   **Module 09:** System Design — Logical vs Physical Design — design decisions, architecture options, transition from requirements to design specifications.
*   **Module 10:** User Interface and UX Design Principles — usability heuristics, wireframing, prototyping, accessibility, and user-centered design.
*   **Module 11:** Database Design and Normalization — relational model, 1NF–3NF, keys, referential integrity, and physical data model.
*   **Module 12:** Software Testing and Quality Assurance — test types, test planning, defect management, acceptance testing, and quality standards.
*   **Module 13:** Implementation and Change Management — deployment strategies, training, organizational change management, and transition planning.
*   **Module 14:** Agile BA — Scrum and Kanban in BA Context — product backlog, sprint planning, BA role in Agile, Kanban flow management.
*   **Module 15:** Enterprise Architecture and Business Strategy Alignment — EA frameworks, capability mapping, solution evaluation (BABOK KA 7).
*   **Module 16:** Final Exam Prep & IIBA ECBA Certification — comprehensive review, ECBA exam strategies, practice questions, and registration guidance.

## Weekly Rhythm
Each module follows the same structure:
1. Video Lecture (watch first)
2. Reading Guide with glossary and certification tips (read before lab)
3. Hands-on Lab or Diagram Activity (submit via Canvas)
4. Module Quiz (10 questions, timed, one attempt)
5. Discussion Board Post (due by Thursday; reply to peers by Sunday)
