## Question 1

Which of the following correctly states an Agile Manifesto value?

- A) Processes and tools over individuals and interactions
- B) Comprehensive documentation over working software
- C) Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
- D) Following a plan over responding to change

Correct Answer: C — "Customer collaboration over contract negotiation" is one of the four Agile Manifesto values exactly as written.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: This reverses Value 1. The Manifesto values individuals and interactions over processes and tools, not the reverse.
- Why B is incorrect: This reverses Value 2. The Manifesto values working software over comprehensive documentation.
- Why D is incorrect: This reverses Value 4. The Manifesto values responding to change over following a plan.

## Question 2

The Agile Manifesto states "while there is value in the items on the right, we value the items on the left more." What does this mean for documentation in an Agile project?

- A) Documentation is prohibited and teams must work from verbal agreements only
- B) Documentation has value but is secondary to working, tested software when trade-offs arise
- C) Teams must write complete documentation before any software development begins
- D) Documentation is required only for regulated industries; Agile teams in other industries skip it

Correct Answer: B — The Manifesto explicitly acknowledges the right-side items have value; the "over" language means the left-side item takes priority when trade-offs arise, not that the right side is eliminated.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: The Manifesto does not prohibit documentation. Scrum requires written artifacts including the Product Backlog.
- Why C is incorrect: This describes "big design up front," a Waterfall practice that Agile explicitly reacts against.
- Why D is incorrect: The Manifesto makes no such industry-specific distinction.

## Question 3

Agile Manifesto Principle 12 states that teams should "at regular intervals reflect on how to become more effective, then tune and adjust their behavior accordingly." Which Scrum event directly operationalizes this principle?

- A) Sprint Planning
- B) Daily Scrum
- C) Sprint Review
- D) Sprint Retrospective

Correct Answer: D — The Sprint Retrospective is the formal event where the Scrum Team inspects itself and creates a plan for improvements, directly embodying Principle 12.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Sprint Planning focuses on what work to accomplish during the Sprint, not on team process improvement.
- Why B is incorrect: The Daily Scrum is a short synchronization event for Developers, not a process reflection.
- Why C is incorrect: The Sprint Review is an inspection of the product Increment with stakeholders, not an internal process improvement event.

## Question 4

Agile Manifesto Principle 1 states the highest priority is to satisfy the customer through "early and continuous delivery of valuable software." How does Scrum honor this principle?

- A) By writing a complete requirements document before development begins
- B) By delivering a potentially releasable product Increment at the end of every Sprint
- C) By assigning customer satisfaction metrics to the Scrum Master
- D) By scheduling a customer demonstration once at the end of the project

Correct Answer: B — Scrum's Sprint cadence produces a potentially releasable Increment at the end of every Sprint, honoring the "early and continuous" delivery commitment in Principle 1.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Writing a complete requirements document upfront delays delivery, contradicting "early" delivery.
- Why C is incorrect: Customer satisfaction is the Product Owner's primary accountability, not the Scrum Master's.
- Why D is incorrect: A single end-of-project demonstration is not "continuous" delivery and violates Principle 1.

## Question 5

A Product Owner receives a request for a significant new feature after Sprint 4 has already begun. What does the Agile Manifesto say about this situation?

- A) The change must be rejected because Agile teams never change scope after planning
- B) The change should be welcomed and added to the current Sprint immediately
- C) The change should be welcomed, added to the Product Backlog, and considered for a future Sprint
- D) The change must go through a formal change control board before it can be considered

Correct Answer: C — Agile Manifesto Principle 2 says to welcome changing requirements even late in development. In Scrum, this is handled by adding the item to the Product Backlog and having the Product Owner prioritize it for a future Sprint, protecting the current Sprint's integrity.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Refusing legitimate changes contradicts Principle 2 and Value 4.
- Why B is incorrect: Adding work to a running Sprint without considering the Sprint Goal or team capacity disrupts Sprint integrity.
- Why D is incorrect: Formal change control boards are associated with Waterfall; Agile uses the Product Backlog to manage change.

## Question 6

A Scrum team has been working 70-hour weeks for three consecutive Sprints. What should the Scrum Master do, according to Agile Manifesto Principle 8?

- A) Congratulate the team for their commitment and ask them to maintain the pace through release
- B) Raise the unsustainable pace as an impediment and work with the team and management to address it
- C) Add more resources to the team so the same work can be done in normal hours
- D) Shorten the Sprint length so less work is expected per Sprint

Correct Answer: B — Principle 8 calls for sustainable development at a constant pace indefinitely. The Scrum Master's accountability includes coaching the team and organization on this principle. An unsustainable pace is an impediment to long-term team health.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Celebrating unsustainable pace violates Principle 8 and will degrade team capacity and quality over time.
- Why C is incorrect: Adding people to a stressed project typically slows it down (Brooks's Law) and does not address the root cause.
- Why D is incorrect: Shortening Sprint length does not address the underlying issue of too much work being committed.

## Question 7

Which team behavior best demonstrates Agile Manifesto Value 1: "Individuals and interactions over processes and tools"?

- A) The team adopts a new tool that automatically generates status reports for management
- B) Two developers resolve a design disagreement by meeting in person for 20 minutes
- C) The team creates a 50-step onboarding process so new members can ramp up without asking questions
- D) The Scrum Master enforces a rule that all communication must go through the team's ticketing system

Correct Answer: B — Resolving a disagreement through direct conversation is a direct expression of valuing human-to-human interaction over process formalities.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Adopting a tool to generate automated reports prioritizes tool output over human interaction.
- Why C is incorrect: A rigid 50-step process is process-over-people, contradicting Value 1.
- Why D is incorrect: Enforcing all communication through a ticketing system privileges the tool over direct interaction.

## Question 8

Agile Manifesto Principle 11 states "the best architectures, requirements, and designs emerge from self-organizing teams." How does the Scrum Guide reflect this principle?

- A) The Scrum Master assigns architecture tasks to the most senior developer
- B) The Product Owner approves all technical design decisions before implementation
- C) Developers self-manage their own work and collectively decide how to fulfill the Sprint Goal
- D) An external architect reviews and approves all design decisions before each Sprint begins

Correct Answer: C — The Scrum Guide states Developers self-manage their own work, directly embodying Principle 11.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: The Scrum Master does not assign technical tasks; they serve through coaching and facilitation.
- Why B is incorrect: The Product Owner is accountable for product value, not technical design decisions.
- Why D is incorrect: External architecture approval introduces dependency that slows the team and undermines self-organization.

## Question 9

Which of the following was NOT one of the development methods represented by the Agile Manifesto signatories at Snowbird in 2001?

- A) Extreme Programming (XP)
- B) Scrum
- C) Waterfall
- D) Crystal

Correct Answer: C — Waterfall is a plan-driven, heavyweight method that the Manifesto was written in response to. Its practitioners were not part of the Snowbird meeting.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Kent Beck, creator of Extreme Programming, was a Manifesto signatory.
- Why B is incorrect: Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland, co-creators of Scrum, were Manifesto signatories.
- Why D is incorrect: Alistair Cockburn, creator of the Crystal method family, was a Manifesto signatory.

## Question 10

A project manager reports that the team has completed 80% of the design documents and 100% of the requirements specification. According to Agile Manifesto Principle 7, is the team making meaningful progress?

- A) Yes — completing design documents and requirements specifications constitutes significant progress
- B) Yes — 80% design completion shows the team is on track for on-time delivery
- C) No — the primary measure of progress is working software, not documents
- D) It depends on whether the project is following Waterfall or Agile methodology

Correct Answer: C — Principle 7 is unambiguous: working software is the primary measure of progress. Completed documentation does not constitute measurable Agile progress when no working software has been demonstrated.

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: This applies a Waterfall phase-completion measure rather than the Agile measure of working software.
- Why B is incorrect: Design document completion percentage does not predict delivery timeline and is not a measure of working software.
- Why D is incorrect: Principle 7 applies specifically to Agile contexts — if the team claims Agile, working software is the measure.

### Question 11 (5 points)

Agile Manifesto Principle 4 states that "business people and developers must work together daily throughout the project." Which Scrum role is specifically designed to fulfill this principle by acting as the bridge between business stakeholders and the development team?

- A) Scrum Master
- B) Product Owner
- C) Release Manager
- D) Technical Lead

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — The Scrum Master coaches the team and organization on Scrum but does not serve as the business-to-developer bridge for product decisions.
  - C) Incorrect — Release Manager is not a Scrum role; release decisions in Scrum are owned by the Scrum Team.
  - D) Incorrect — Technical Lead is not a Scrum role; Scrum uses self-managing Developers without hierarchical technical roles.

### Question 12 (5 points)

Agile Manifesto Principle 9 states that "continuous attention to technical excellence and good design enhances agility." Which practice most directly embodies this principle?

- A) Scheduling a code review only before the final Sprint release
- B) Incorporating refactoring and automated testing as ongoing activities within every Sprint
- C) Assigning a dedicated quality assurance team to test after each Sprint
- D) Delaying architectural improvements until a designated "hardening Sprint"

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — A single pre-release code review is not "continuous" attention; it is a one-time gate inconsistent with Principle 9.
  - C) Incorrect — Delegating quality to a separate team is a handoff that reduces agility and is inconsistent with self-managing teams producing "done" increments each Sprint.
  - D) Incorrect — A hardening Sprint defers technical excellence to a batch activity, contradicting the "continuous" nature of Principle 9.

### Question 13 (5 points)

Which of the following statements accurately describes all seventeen signatories of the Agile Manifesto?

- A) They were academics who had never worked in commercial software development
- B) They were experienced practitioners from diverse lightweight development methods
- C) They were all employees of a single software consultancy that commissioned the document
- D) They were government contractors mandated to create a new software standard by the DoD

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — The signatories were experienced practitioners (Beck, Fowler, Schwaber, Sutherland, etc.), not academics; many had decades of industry experience.
  - C) Incorrect — The signatories came from different organizations and methods; no single company commissioned the Manifesto.
  - D) Incorrect — The Manifesto was a voluntary effort by independent practitioners, not a government-commissioned standard.

### Question 14 (5 points)

A Scrum Master notices that their team never raises impediments at the Daily Scrum, even though Sprint delivery consistently falls short. Which Manifesto value is most likely being violated?

- A) Working software over comprehensive documentation
- B) Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
- C) Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
- D) Responding to change over following a plan

- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — The problem is not about documentation vs. software; it is about communication and interaction quality within the team.
  - B) Incorrect — The issue is internal team dynamics, not the relationship between the team and the customer.
  - D) Incorrect — Failing to raise impediments is not about changing plans; it is about the team suppressing honest interaction in favor of appearing compliant with process.

### Question 15 (5 points)

Agile Manifesto Principle 6 states that "face-to-face conversation is the most efficient and effective method of conveying information." How should a Scrum Team interpret this principle when working fully remotely?

- A) Remote teams cannot be Agile because Principle 6 forbids distributed work
- B) The principle's intent is rich, synchronous communication; remote teams should use video calls and real-time collaboration tools to honor the spirit of the principle
- C) Remote teams should switch to Waterfall because Agile requires physical co-location
- D) The principle only applies to Sprint Reviews; all other communication can be asynchronous

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — The Manifesto does not forbid distributed work; the principle expresses a preference for richness of communication that can be approximated remotely.
  - C) Incorrect — Nothing in the Manifesto or Scrum Guide restricts Agile to co-located teams; the Scrum Guide explicitly addresses distributed teams.
  - D) Incorrect — The principle applies to all team communication, not only Sprint Reviews.

Which Manifesto principle directly warns against over-engineering or adding unrequested features to a product?

### Question 17 (5 points)

A development team using Scrum completes 85% of their Sprint Backlog items every Sprint but never achieves the Sprint Goal. According to the Agile Manifesto, what is the most significant problem with this pattern?

- A) The team is not tracking velocity correctly
- B) Sprint Backlog completion percentage is not the primary measure of progress; delivering working software toward the Sprint Goal is
- C) The team needs to commit to fewer items per Sprint to improve completion rate
- D) The Scrum Master should escalate to management for more resources

- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — Velocity tracking is a tool; the fundamental issue is the Sprint Goal not being achieved, which means the Increment does not deliver value.
  - C) Incorrect — Committing to fewer items might improve the percentage but does not address why the Sprint Goal is consistently missed.
  - D) Incorrect — Adding resources (Brooks's Law) typically slows teams; the problem here is goal alignment, not capacity.

### Question 18 (5 points)

The Agile Manifesto's opening statement says practitioners are "uncovering better ways of developing software by doing it." Which word in that statement most directly reflects the empirical philosophy of Scrum?

- A) "Better" — because Agile always produces better outcomes than Waterfall
- B) "Ways" — because Agile has multiple valid methods and frameworks
- C) "Uncovering" — because knowledge is discovered through experience, not prescribed in advance
- D) "Developing" — because Agile is only applicable to software development, not other work

- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — "Better" is a comparative adjective, not the word that captures epistemological stance; also, context-dependent outcomes are not guaranteed to be better.
  - B) Incorrect — "Ways" acknowledges plurality of methods but does not capture the empirical learning philosophy.
  - D) Incorrect — "Developing" describes the domain; the Scrum Guide itself notes Scrum is used beyond software.

### Question 19 (5 points)

A Product Owner tells the team: "I know the Retrospective found three process problems, but we don't have time to fix them — let's just keep shipping." Which Manifesto principle is being most directly violated?

- A) Principle 1 — customer satisfaction through early delivery
- B) Principle 5 — build around motivated individuals and trust them
- C) Principle 12 — teams reflect regularly and tune their behavior
- D) Principle 9 — continuous attention to technical excellence

- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — Principle 1 is about delivering valuable software to customers; this scenario is about ignoring process improvement identified by the team.
  - B) Incorrect — While dismissing the team's findings could undermine trust, Principle 5 is specifically about motivated individuals having the environment they need, not about retrospective action items.
  - D) Incorrect — The Retrospective findings described are process problems, not necessarily technical excellence issues.

### Question 20 (5 points)

According to the Agile Manifesto, what is the correct relationship between an Agile team and a contract with a customer?

- A) Contracts have no place in Agile; all agreements should be verbal
- B) Contracts should lock in the complete feature list before work begins to protect both parties
- C) Contracts have value, but the team prioritizes ongoing collaboration over strict adherence to contract terms when they conflict with delivering value
- D) The Product Owner's primary job is to enforce the original contract scope against scope creep from stakeholders

- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - A) Incorrect — The Manifesto does not prohibit contracts; Value 3 says "customer collaboration over contract negotiation," not "no contracts."
  - B) Incorrect — Locking a complete feature list is a Waterfall practice that conflicts with welcoming change (Value 4 and Principle 2).
  - D) Incorrect — The Product Owner maximizes product value, which means continuously re-prioritizing based on new information, not enforcing a fixed original scope.
