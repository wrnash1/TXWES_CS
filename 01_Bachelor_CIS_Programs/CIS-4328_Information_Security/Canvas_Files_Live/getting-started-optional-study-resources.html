CIS-4328 Information Security | Texas Wesleyan University

This course is Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC). Every module's Reading Guide is a complete, self-contained study document — you do not need to purchase, read, or watch anything outside of Canvas to learn the material tested in the labs, quizzes, and certification exam blueprint.

The resources below are entirely optional. Some students like a second explanation of a tricky concept, extra practice problems, or a different format (video vs. text). If that's you, these free resources are a good place to look — but nothing here is assigned or required.

Additional Free Resources

  1. Supplementary Study Notes: Professor Messer's CompTIA Security+ Study Notes — free written notes organized by Security+ exam domain, if you want a different explanation of a topic.

  2. Video Walkthroughs: Professor Messer's CompTIA Security+ Course Playlist — free videos organized by Security+ exam objective, useful for hearing a concept explained a different way or for additional exam-style review.

  3. Official Certification Reference: CompTIA Security+ Exam Blueprint Objectives — the official exam blueprint and vendor documentation, useful if you want to see exactly how CompTIA phrases an exam objective.

How to Use These (If You Choose To)

If a concept in a Reading Guide isn't clicking, try searching for that specific term in Professor Messer's playlist or study notes rather than working through either resource cover to cover — you'll get a faster, more targeted second explanation that way.
