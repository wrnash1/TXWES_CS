## Scenario 1: Identifying Your Strongest and Weakest CCNA Domains

Reflect on your experience across all 16 modules of this course. The CCNA 200-301 exam covers six domains: Network Fundamentals (20%), Network Access (20%), IP Connectivity (25%), IP Services (10%), Security Fundamentals (15%), and Automation and Programmability (15%).

In your post, address the following:

* Which domain do you feel most confident about and what experiences in this course built that confidence?
* Which domain presents the greatest challenge for you and why?
* What specific study strategies will you use in the final days before your exam to address your weakest domain?
* If you had to identify the single most important concept from the entire course for the CCNA exam, what would it be and why?

Be specific about your personal learning experience. Referencing particular lab activities, quiz questions that surprised you, or topics that required multiple attempts to master will make your post more meaningful and more useful for your own reflection.

## Scenario 2: Designing an Enterprise Network from Scratch

You have been hired as a network engineer for a 200-person startup company that is moving into a new three-story office building. You have complete design freedom and a reasonable budget. The company needs wired and wireless connectivity for employees, a secure guest network, Internet access through a single ISP, and basic security controls.

In your post, address the following:

* What VLAN structure would you design and why — how many VLANs and what does each serve?
* Which dynamic routing protocol would you choose for the campus core and why?
* How would you approach wireless security — WPA2-Enterprise or WPA3 — given that the company is starting fresh?
* Which CCNA 200-301 topic from this course would most directly apply to this real-world design challenge?

Approach this as a genuine design problem. There is no single correct answer — the goal is to demonstrate that you can apply technical knowledge from this course to a realistic business requirement.

## Scenario 3: Preparing Others for the CCNA Exam

You have just completed CIS-3322 Advanced Networking and are preparing to sit for the CCNA 200-301 exam. A junior colleague has just enrolled in the same program and asks you for advice. She has basic networking knowledge but has never taken a certification exam.

In your post, address the following:

* What are the three most important study habits or strategies you would share based on your experience in this course?
* Which three CCNA topics should she prioritize most heavily, and why did you choose those three?
* What is the single biggest exam-day mistake that well-prepared candidates make, and how can she avoid it?
* What resources beyond this course would you recommend, and what makes each one valuable?

Write your response as genuine advice from a peer — not as a summary of course content. Focus on what you learned about how to learn and test, not just what the content covered.
