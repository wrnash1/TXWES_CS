# Module 01 Graduate Colloquium: Distributed Systems Foundations & Architectural Models
**Course:** CSC-6362 | Texas Wesleyan University

### Colloquium Prompt
Critically analyze the trade-offs inherent in RPC, gRPC, Client-Server vs. Peer-to-Peer, Microservices, and Event-Driven Architectures. Consider an enterprise operating under strict latency SLA and five-nines (99.999%) availability mandates.
1. Formulate a technical design proposal defending your chosen architectural pattern.
2. Evaluate potential edge cases and failure domains.
3. Critique the proposals of at least two peers using empirical performance arguments.
