## 4. Grading Policy & Rubrics
**Grade Breakdown:**
*   **Lab Assignments (Modules 01–06):** 30%
*   **Weekly Quizzes (Modules 01–06):** 20%
*   **Discussion Boards (Modules 01–06):** 20%
*   **Graduate Research Paper (Module 06):** 10%
*   **Final Capstone Lab & Exam (Module 07):** 20%

**Standard Graduate Lab Grading Rubric (100 Points):**
*   **Configuration Accuracy (40 pts):** All required protocols/features are correctly configured and operational as verified by Packet Tracer output.
*   **Professional Documentation (30 pts):** Lab report includes annotated screenshots, topology diagrams, command outputs, and written analysis explaining design rationale.
*   **Technical Analysis (20 pts):** Student explains *why* the configuration works, what could go wrong, and how it would be monitored in production.
*   **Troubleshooting Narrative (10 pts):** If errors occurred, the student documented the error, their diagnostic process, and resolution steps.

**Graduate Discussion Grading Rubric (100 Points):**
*   **Substantive Content (40 pts):** Post is 400+ words, addresses the prompt thoroughly, and demonstrates understanding of graduate-level concepts.
*   **External Citations (30 pts):** At least one credible technical source (RFC, Cisco documentation, or peer-reviewed article) is cited and integrated.
*   **Peer Engagement (30 pts):** Meaningful responses to at least two classmates that advance the discussion beyond simple agreement.

**Graduate Research Paper Rubric (100 Points):**
*   **Technical Accuracy (35 pts):** Content is technically correct and reflects CCNP-level depth of understanding.
*   **Research Quality (25 pts):** Minimum 5 credible technical references; properly cited.
*   **Writing Quality (25 pts):** Professional, clear, and well-organized graduate-level writing (APA or IEEE format).
*   **Original Analysis (15 pts):** Paper demonstrates the student's own analytical perspective, not a summary of sources.

## 5. Final Exam / Capstone Instructions
**Module:** Module 07 (Week 7: November 30 – December 11)
**Format:** 100% Online — Comprehensive Lab + Written Exam via Canvas

The Module 07 Final consists of two components:

**Component A — Capstone Lab (60%):**
Students will receive a complete enterprise network topology brief and must configure it from scratch in Cisco Packet Tracer within a specified time window. The topology will incorporate: multi-area OSPF, BGP peering, VLAN segmentation, HA with HSRP, and a basic QoS policy. A complete professional lab report is required.

**Component B — Written Exam (40%):**
A timed Canvas quiz with CCNP Enterprise-style scenario questions covering all 7 modules. Questions will be a mix of multiple choice, drag-and-drop topology identification, and short-answer analysis questions.

*   **Passing Standard:** A combined score of 70% or higher on the Capstone Final is required to pass the course (in addition to meeting the overall weighted grade requirement).
*   **Academic Integrity:** The Final is an individual, proctored-equivalent assignment. Canvas submission timestamps, browser lock settings, and plagiarism detection are active. All work must be your own.
