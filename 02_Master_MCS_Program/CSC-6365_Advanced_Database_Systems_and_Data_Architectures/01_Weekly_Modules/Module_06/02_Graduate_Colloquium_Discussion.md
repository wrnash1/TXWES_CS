# Module 06 Graduate Colloquium: Modern Analytical Architectures & Data Lakehouses
**Course:** CSC-6365 | Texas Wesleyan University

### Colloquium Prompt
Critically analyze the trade-offs inherent in OLAP vs. OLTP, Columnar Storage (Parquet), Snowflake, Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg, and Lakehouse Patterns. Consider an enterprise operating under strict latency SLA and five-nines (99.999%) availability mandates.
1. Formulate a technical design proposal defending your chosen architectural pattern.
2. Evaluate potential edge cases and failure domains.
3. Critique the proposals of at least two peers using empirical performance arguments.
