## Lab Overview

In this lab you will simulate a two-week Scrum sprint for a realistic IT project. You
will write user stories, run a sprint planning session, manage a sprint board, calculate
velocity, and conduct a sprint retrospective. This lab builds hands-on Agile fluency
that directly supports Project+ exam readiness and real-world team participation.

Estimated completion time: 60–75 minutes

---

## Scenario: Fort Worth Parks and Recreation Mobile App

The City of Fort Worth Parks and Recreation Department is building a mobile app that
allows residents to browse park facilities, register for classes, reserve picnic
pavilions, and report maintenance issues. You are the Scrum Master for the development
team.

The project has a 16-week timeline (8 two-week sprints). This lab simulates Sprint 3.

The team has completed Sprints 1 and 2 with the following velocity:

- Sprint 1: 24 story points completed
- Sprint 2: 28 story points completed

The Product Owner has provided the following prioritized product backlog items (PBIs)
entering Sprint 3.

---
