## Question 1

Which statement best reflects the intent of the Agile Manifesto value "responding
to change over following a plan"?

A. Agile projects do not require a plan
B. Plans should be abandoned as soon as any change request is received
C. Adapting to change is prioritized over rigid adherence to an original plan
D. Only the Product Owner may approve changes to the project plan

### Correct Answer: C

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — Agile projects absolutely require planning; sprint planning, release
planning, and roadmaps are all planning activities. B is incorrect — the manifesto does
not say plans are abandoned; it says responding to change is valued more than following
a plan. D is incorrect — while the Product Owner manages the backlog, the Manifesto value
is about the team's mindset toward change, not an approval process. C is correct — the
Manifesto explicitly states it values the left side more than the right, meaning adaptability
is prized over plan adherence, not that plans are worthless.

## Question 2

In Scrum, who is solely responsible for ordering the product backlog and determining
which features deliver the most value?

A. Scrum Master
B. Development Team
C. Project Sponsor
D. Product Owner

### Correct Answer: D

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — the Scrum Master facilitates events and removes impediments but does
not own scope or prioritization decisions. B is incorrect — the development team owns
the sprint backlog and execution decisions but does not prioritize the product backlog.
C is incorrect — the sponsor may provide strategic direction but does not manage the
backlog in the Scrum framework. D is correct — the Product Owner is the single authority
responsible for product backlog ordering and maximizing product value.

## Question 3

A Scrum team completed the following story points across four sprints: 22, 30, 26, 26.
What is the team's average velocity, and how many story points should they plan for
Sprint 5?

A. Velocity 26; plan 26 story points
B. Velocity 26; plan 30 story points to stretch the team
C. Velocity 28; plan 26 story points
D. Velocity 26; velocity should not be used for sprint planning

### Correct Answer: A

### Distractor Analysis

B is incorrect — planning beyond velocity to "stretch" the team contradicts Agile
sustainable pace principles and corrupts velocity data. C is incorrect — the average
of 22, 30, 26, and 26 is 104 divided by 4 = 26, not 28. D is incorrect — velocity is
explicitly used as a sprint planning input. A is correct — average velocity is 26
story points; the team should plan approximately 26 story points for Sprint 5.

## Question 4

Which Scrum event is specifically designed to inspect the team's process and
create an improvement plan for the next sprint?

A. Sprint Review
B. Sprint Retrospective
C. Daily Scrum
D. Sprint Planning

### Correct Answer: B

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — the Sprint Review inspects the product increment and gathers
stakeholder feedback; it focuses on the product, not the process. C is incorrect —
the Daily Scrum is a 15-minute coordination meeting about the current day's work. D
is incorrect — Sprint Planning selects and decomposes backlog items for the upcoming
sprint. B is correct — the Sprint Retrospective is explicitly designed for the team
to reflect on how they work together and commit to process improvements.

## Question 5

A development team's sprint board shows five items in the In Progress column and the
WIP limit for that column is three. According to Kanban principles, what should happen?

A. The team should increase the WIP limit to accommodate the additional work
B. No new items should move into In Progress until at least two items are completed
C. The Scrum Master should assign the two excess items to specific developers
D. The sprint should be extended to allow the team to complete all five items

### Correct Answer: B

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — increasing WIP limits to avoid the discipline defeats the entire
purpose of WIP constraints and allows multitasking waste to continue. C is incorrect —
task assignment by a manager contradicts self-organizing team principles. D is incorrect —
extending sprints is not a Kanban mechanism and Scrum sprints are fixed-length. B is
correct — WIP limits are enforced by stopping new work from entering the column until
capacity is freed, which forces the team to finish work already started.

## Question 6

A user story reads: "The system should be fast and easy to use." Which INVEST criterion
does this story most clearly violate?

A. Independent
B. Negotiable
C. Testable
D. Small

### Correct Answer: C

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — the story does not have an obvious dependency on other stories, so
independence is not the primary issue. B is incorrect — the story could be negotiated
to be more specific, but that is not what it currently violates. D is incorrect — the
story is not unusually large in scope. C is correct — "fast and easy to use" cannot be
tested because there is no measurable threshold defined. A testable story would specify
"loads in under 2 seconds" and "achieves a System Usability Scale score of 75 or above."

## Question 7

An organization is running a large infrastructure migration project with fixed
regulatory compliance milestones. The infrastructure team uses a formal change control
process and staged approvals. The software development team delivers new features in
two-week sprints. This project is best described as which type of approach?

A. Pure Scrum
B. Waterfall
C. Kanban
D. Hybrid

### Correct Answer: D

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — pure Scrum would apply to the entire project, not just the software
team. B is incorrect — the software development team is explicitly using sprints, which
is not waterfall. C is incorrect — Kanban does not describe the staged approval and
compliance milestone structure. D is correct — the project blends formal waterfall
governance for infrastructure and compliance with Agile sprint-based delivery for
software, which is the definition of a hybrid approach.

## Question 8

During a Daily Scrum, a developer says she is blocked because she is waiting for
database credentials from the IT security team. What is the Scrum Master's most
appropriate response?

A. Document the impediment in the risk register and schedule a change control meeting
B. Reassign the developer to a different task and remove the blocked story from the sprint
C. Take ownership of resolving the impediment by following up with the IT security team
D. Wait until the Sprint Review to report the impediment to the Product Owner

### Correct Answer: C

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — logging in the risk register and scheduling a change control meeting
are waterfall mechanisms; the Daily Scrum impediment requires immediate action, not
formal documentation. B is incorrect — removing the story from the sprint requires
Product Owner involvement and is premature before attempting to resolve the block. D is
incorrect — waiting until the Sprint Review allows the block to persist for the remainder
of the sprint, jeopardizing the sprint goal. C is correct — removing impediments is the
Scrum Master's primary responsibility; following up with the blocking party the same day
is the expected action.

## Question 9

Which of the following is the most accurate description of the Definition of Done?

A. A list of tasks the development team must complete before sprint planning
B. The acceptance criteria attached to a specific user story
C. A shared agreement on the quality criteria every increment must meet before release
D. The Product Owner's approval signature on the sprint backlog

### Correct Answer: C

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — the Definition of Done applies to completed increments, not to
pre-sprint activities. B is incorrect — acceptance criteria are story-specific and may
vary per story; the Definition of Done is a project-wide quality standard that applies
to all increments. D is incorrect — the Product Owner's acceptance is one element, but
the DoD is a team-defined checklist covering code quality, testing, documentation, and
more. C is correct — the Definition of Done is a formal shared agreement on the minimum
quality standards every increment must satisfy before it is considered complete.

## Question 10

A project manager is transitioning her team from a traditional waterfall approach to
Scrum. She plans to continue assigning individual tasks to each developer during sprint
planning and tracking each person's hours daily. Which Agile principle does this most
directly conflict with?

A. Working software is the primary measure of progress
B. The best architectures emerge from self-organizing teams
C. Deliver working software frequently
D. Business people and developers must work together daily

### Correct Answer: B

### Distractor Analysis

A is incorrect — measuring hours rather than working software is a related concern, but
the scenario specifically describes task assignment and individual tracking. C is incorrect
— delivery frequency is not the issue described. D is incorrect — daily collaboration
between business and development is not what the scenario violates. B is correct —
assigning tasks to individuals and tracking hours directly contradicts the Agile principle
that self-organizing teams determine how best to accomplish their work; the PM should
allow the team to pull and self-assign work during sprint planning.
