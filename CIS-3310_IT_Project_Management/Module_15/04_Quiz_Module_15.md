**Question 1**
What is the primary purpose of conducting a Lessons Learned session during project closure?
*   A) To assign blame and document the team members responsible for project failures
*   B) To capture successes, failures, and process insights to improve performance on future organizational projects
*   C) To calculate final team member performance bonuses based on project outcomes
*   D) To archive server hardware logs and close service desk tickets related to the project
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Lessons learned sessions capture historical knowledge—what worked well, what didn't, and what should change—to build organizational learning and improve future project delivery.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* Lessons learned are a knowledge management tool, not an evaluation tool. The goal is continuous improvement—understanding root causes of problems and successes so that the organization learns from each project.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Assigning blame is never the purpose of a lessons learned session. A blame-focused approach discourages honesty, reduces participation, and destroys the psychological safety needed for genuine reflection.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Performance bonuses are a human resource and compensation management activity; they are not determined in a lessons learned session.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Archiving hardware logs and closing service tickets are administrative IT operations tasks—not a lessons learned activity. They may be part of project closeout, but they are not the purpose of a lessons learned session.

**Question 2**
Which of the following best defines a **Project Handoff** in project management?
*   A) The formal transfer of completed project deliverables, documentation, and operational responsibilities to the receiving team, including training and formal acceptance sign-off.
*   B) The temporary reassignment of a project manager to a new project while the old project continues without formal closure.
*   C) A financial transaction where project budget surplus is transferred to the receiving department's operational budget.
*   D) The process of handing off unfinished project work to an outside contractor for completion.
*   **Correct Answer:** A) A project handoff formally transfers ownership of all deliverables, support documentation, and operational responsibilities from the project team to the team that will own and maintain the result going forward.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why A is correct:* A successful handoff includes stakeholder acceptance, training for operational staff, documentation of how to maintain and operate the deliverable, and formal sign-off. Poor handoffs undermine the project's long-term value.
    *   *Why B is incorrect:* Reassigning a PM while leaving the old project open is a resource management decision, not a project handoff—and leaving a project without formal closure is a governance failure.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Transferring budget surplus to a department's operational budget is a financial accounting activity. Project handoffs transfer deliverables and responsibilities, not budget balances.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Sending unfinished work to an outside contractor describes a procurement or outsourcing decision, not a project handoff. A handoff transfers completed, accepted deliverables.

**Question 3**
Which of the following best describes the relationship between a Project, a Program, and a Portfolio?
*   A) Programs are sub-components of projects; portfolios are sub-components of programs.
*   B) A project produces a unique deliverable; a program groups related projects for coordinated benefit; a portfolio groups projects and programs to achieve strategic objectives.
*   C) Projects and programs are the same thing at different scales; portfolios are groups of unrelated projects.
*   D) A portfolio produces deliverables; programs monitor portfolio performance; projects approve the portfolio strategy.
*   **Correct Answer:** B) A project creates a unique result; a program coordinates multiple related projects to deliver benefits unavailable from managing them separately; a portfolio aligns projects and programs with organizational strategy.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* The PMI hierarchy is: Portfolio > Program > Project. Portfolios align investment with strategy. Programs deliver coordinated benefits. Projects deliver individual outputs.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* The hierarchy is inverted in this answer. Programs contain projects, not the reverse. Portfolios contain programs and projects, not the reverse.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Projects and programs are distinct, not the same concept at different scales. Programs deliver benefits through coordinated management that no individual project can achieve alone.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* This reverses the typical purpose of each level. Projects produce deliverables. Portfolios align investments with strategy—they do not produce deliverables themselves.

**Question 4**
A project manager has confirmed that all deliverables have been accepted by the client, but realizes that vendor invoices are still outstanding and no contract closure documentation has been signed. What must the PM complete before the project can be administratively closed?
*   A) Submit a risk register update to document any open risks
*   B) Complete Contract Closeout — verify all contract deliverables were met, resolve outstanding claims, and obtain formal contract termination sign-off
*   C) Archive all project documents in the organizational document management system
*   D) Release all project team members to their functional managers
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Contract Closeout must be completed before Administrative Closure can occur; all vendor obligations must be settled and formally terminated before the project is fully closed.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* Contract closeout is a prerequisite for administrative closure. Leaving contracts open creates legal and financial liability. All vendor invoices must be settled, deliverables verified, claims resolved, and contracts formally terminated.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Updating the risk register is a monitoring activity during execution, not a closure prerequisite. Once deliverables are accepted, risk monitoring is no longer the primary concern.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Archiving documents is an important administrative closure activity but cannot be completed before contract closeout; open contracts are themselves documents that cannot yet be finalized.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Releasing team members is a resource management activity that typically happens after contracts are closed and the project is formally closed—not a prerequisite to contract closeout.

**Question 5**
At the end of a major ERP implementation project, the project team holds a meeting to discuss what worked well, what caused problems, and what should be done differently on the next implementation. The insights are documented and stored for future project teams. What document is being created?
*   A) Post-Implementation Review Report
*   B) Lessons Learned Register
*   C) Risk Closure Report
*   D) Project Status Report
*   **Correct Answer:** B) A Lessons Learned Register documents the knowledge gained during the project—both positive practices to repeat and failures to avoid—and stores it in organizational process assets for use by future project teams.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* The Lessons Learned Register is the PMI-standard document for capturing and storing project knowledge during and after project execution. It becomes part of the organization's institutional memory.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* A Post-Implementation Review Report is a similar concept but is typically focused on measuring whether the project delivered its intended business benefits after go-live—it is a separate output from a lessons learned session.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* A "Risk Closure Report" is not a standard PMI document name. Risk outcomes may be discussed in lessons learned, but there is no separate closure report for risks.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* A Project Status Report is a periodic communication document created during project execution to report progress to stakeholders. It is not a knowledge capture tool created at project closure.
