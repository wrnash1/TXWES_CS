# Reading Guide: Module 15 - Portfolio and Program Management
## Course: CIS-3310_IT_Project_Management (CompTIA Project+ / PMI CAPM)

### Introduction
Welcome to **Module 15 - Portfolio and Program Management**! While earlier modules focused on managing individual projects, this module examines the broader organizational context: how multiple related projects are grouped into programs and how collections of projects and programs are managed as a portfolio aligned with organizational strategy. Understanding these distinctions is important for both the Project+ and CAPM exams, and it is increasingly relevant in enterprise IT environments where PMs must justify projects in terms of business value.

This module also covers project closure in depth—the formal processes for closing individual projects and contracts, capturing lessons learned, and transitioning deliverables to operational teams.

---

### 1. High-Yield Glossary
Review these essential definitions carefully. The certification exam expects you to know these concepts inside and out:

*   **Administrative Closure**: The formal process of finalizing all project activities, confirming that all deliverables have been accepted, updating organizational records, archiving project documents, and officially releasing project resources. Administrative closure must occur before the project can be considered complete. It ensures a clean handoff and establishes the documented history of the project.
*   **Project Handoff**: The formal transfer of a completed project's deliverables, documentation, and operational responsibilities to the receiving team or business unit. A successful handoff includes training the operational staff, providing support documentation, and obtaining a formal acceptance sign-off. Poor handoffs are a leading cause of post-project operational failures.
*   **Contract Closeout**: The formal process of verifying that all contract terms and deliverables have been satisfied, resolving any remaining open claims or disputes, and formally terminating the contractual relationship with each vendor or contractor. Contract closeout must be completed before administrative project closure can occur.
*   **Lessons Learned**: A formal knowledge management activity conducted at the end of each project (and often at the end of major phases) to identify what went well, what went poorly, and what should be done differently on future projects. Lessons learned are documented in a Lessons Learned Register and stored in the organizational process assets so that future project teams can benefit from the experience.
*   **Post-Mortem Reports**: Detailed documents produced after project completion that analyze project performance—including schedule adherence, budget performance, quality outcomes, and risk responses—and identify root causes of successes and failures. Post-mortem reports are a primary input to organizational learning and process improvement.

### 2. Certification Exam Tips
*   **Project+ Tip – Closing Process Group Activities**: The Project+ exam tests what happens during formal project closure: final deliverable acceptance, stakeholder sign-off, contract closeout, resource release, lessons learned documentation, and archive records. Know that Closing comes after Monitoring & Controlling and is the last process group.
*   **CAPM Tip – Program vs. Project vs. Portfolio**: A Program is a group of related projects managed in a coordinated way to obtain benefits not available from managing them individually (e.g., a digital transformation program). A Portfolio is a collection of projects, programs, and operations managed to achieve strategic business objectives. Projects can be in a portfolio without being in a program. Know how these three levels relate to each other.
*   **Scenario Trap**: A lessons learned session should not be used to "assign blame" or "punish underperformers." The purpose is knowledge capture and process improvement. If a question presents a blame-focused review as an option, it is always wrong.
*   **Study Resource**: For a practical overview of project closure activities and lessons learned facilitation, search [YouTube: Project Closure Lessons Learned Post Mortem Project Management](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=project+closure+lessons+learned+post+mortem+project+management). Many resources provide free lessons learned template downloads.

### Lab & Command Integration
In this week's hands-on lab, you will perform the following steps to apply these concepts:
*   **Draft a lessons learned survey questionnaire**: Create a 10-question lessons learned survey for your IT deployment project that covers schedule performance, budget adherence, communication effectiveness, risk management, team collaboration, and stakeholder satisfaction.
*   **Complete a final project acceptance sign-off document**: Write a one-page project acceptance document for your IT deployment scenario, listing all deliverables, their acceptance criteria, and providing signature lines for the project manager and key stakeholders.
*   **Write post-mortem report outlines**: For your IT deployment project, draft a one-page post-mortem report outline covering: project summary, performance vs. baselines, top three successes, top three improvement areas, and three specific recommendations for future similar projects.
