# Course Administration Pack: CIS-3310 IT Project Management
**Texas Wesleyan University – Computer and Information Sciences**
**Target Certification:** CompTIA Project+ (PK0-005)
**Complementary Alignment:** PMI CAPM (Certified Associate in Project Management)

---

## Course Description

CIS-3310 IT Project Management is a 16-week undergraduate course that prepares students to plan, execute, monitor, and close IT projects using industry-standard methodologies aligned with PMI's PMBOK framework and the CompTIA Project+ (PK0-005) certification objectives. Students apply project management concepts through hands-on simulation labs covering scope definition, scheduling, cost estimation, risk management, Agile/Scrum, and stakeholder communication. Upon successful completion, students will possess the foundational knowledge required to sit for the CompTIA Project+ certification examination and enter entry- to mid-level project management or project coordination roles in IT organizations.

## Required Materials

1. **Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC):** All primary course materials are provided at no cost through Canvas. No textbook purchase is required.
2. **OER Textbook (Free):** [Project Management Open Textbook – BC Campus](https://opentextbc.ca/projectmanagement/) — A peer-reviewed, openly licensed project management textbook aligned with PMI methodology. All required reading assignments reference specific chapters from this text.
3. **Free Video Lectures:** [CompTIA Project+ PK0-005 Playlist by Joseph Phillips](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=comptia+project%2B+PK0-005+joseph+phillips) — Publicly available YouTube series covering all Project+ exam domains. Assigned video links are provided in each module's Reading Guide.
4. **Computer & Web Browser:** A modern computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux) with a current web browser is required to access Canvas, complete labs, and use free project management tools.
5. **Free Project Management Tools (student choice — no purchase required):**
   - [Trello](https://trello.com) — Free Kanban board tool for Agile and general task management labs.
   - [ClickUp Free Tier](https://clickup.com) — Free project management platform for scheduling, Gantt charts, and team collaboration exercises.
   - [draw.io / diagrams.net](https://www.diagrams.net) — Free browser-based diagramming tool for WBS diagrams, network diagrams, and flowcharts.
   - [GanttProject](https://www.ganttproject.biz) — Free desktop Gantt chart and CPM scheduling tool.
6. **CompTIA Project+ Exam Objectives (Free Download):** Available at [comptia.org/certifications/project](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/project). Students are encouraged to download the official exam objectives document and use it as a study reference throughout the course.

## Grading Policy

| Component | Weight | Description |
|---|---|---|
| PM Module Quizzes (×16) | 20% | 5-question multiple-choice quiz at the end of each module, testing terminology, concepts, and scenario application aligned with Project+ exam style. |
| Project Simulation Labs (×16) | 30% | Hands-on weekly lab activities applying course concepts to a running IT project scenario. Labs build on each other across modules, culminating in a complete project portfolio. |
| Discussion Boards (×8) | 20% | Bi-weekly facilitated discussions connecting course concepts to real-world IT project scenarios, current events, and certification exam topics. Graded on quality of contribution and peer engagement. |
| Final Certification Exam | 30% | A comprehensive 90-question, timed final exam administered in Canvas, structured and scored to mirror the CompTIA Project+ PK0-005 exam format and passing standard (710/900). |

## Course Policies

### Academic Integrity
All submitted work must be the student's own. Collaboration on individual assignments constitutes academic dishonesty. Students may discuss concepts with peers but must write their own responses independently. Use of AI-generated text submitted as original work is a violation of Texas Wesleyan's academic integrity policy.

### Accessibility
Students requiring academic accommodations should contact the Texas Wesleyan Accessibility Services office and provide documentation to the instructor within the first two weeks of class. The course is designed to be accessible; all materials are available in Canvas in text and video formats.

### Certification Exam Note
Successful completion of this course prepares students to sit for the CompTIA Project+ (PK0-005) exam. The CompTIA exam is administered independently by CompTIA at authorized testing centers or online via Pearson VUE. Exam registration fees are separate from course tuition. Students are encouraged to register for the exam within 60 days of course completion while material is fresh.
