### Question 1
What is the official modality of CIS-3310?
- A) 100% Online Asynchronous with weekly deadlines
- B) In-person classroom lectures on campus
- C) Synchronous Zoom meetings every evening
- D) Self-paced with no due dates
**Correct Answer:** A
**Distractor Analysis:**
- A is correct: This course is delivered 100% online asynchronously.
- B is incorrect: There are no mandatory campus meetings.
- C is incorrect: There are no required live evening Zoom sessions.
- D is incorrect: Assignments have strict weekly Sunday deadlines.
