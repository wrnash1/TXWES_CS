## Scenario Background

Westgate Community College is implementing a new Student Information System (SIS) to replace a 15-year-old legacy platform. The new SIS will manage student enrollment, financial aid, grading, degree auditing, and reporting. The project has a $1,400,000 budget and a 16-month implementation timeline. Go-live is tied to the start of the fall academic semester — a fixed deadline that cannot move.

The project team includes:

- Kira Mendez — Project Manager (you)
- Dr. Alan Park — VP of Academic Affairs (project sponsor)
- Sandra Walsh — IT Director
- James Liu — Lead Systems Analyst
- Maria Gomez — Database Administrator
- Two external implementation consultants from the SIS vendor
- A student representative (undergraduate, part-time involvement)

Other identified stakeholders include:

- Dean of Students office (will use SIS daily for enrollment and advising)
- Financial Aid Department (SIS integration with federal aid reporting systems is critical)
- Faculty Senate President (concerned about grade entry changes and faculty workload)
- Registrar's Office (primary operational users of the new system)
- IT Help Desk (will support the system after go-live)
- State Higher Education Board (receives compliance data from the SIS quarterly)
- Current SIS vendor (legacy system; contract ends at go-live)
