**Question 1**
A project has a Cost Performance Index (CPI) of 0.85 and a Schedule Performance Index (SPI) of 1.10. What is the status?
*   A) Under budget and ahead of schedule
*   B) Over budget and behind schedule
*   C) Over budget and ahead of schedule
*   D) Under budget and behind schedule
*   **Correct Answer:** C) CPI < 1 indicates the project is spending more than planned (over budget). SPI > 1 indicates tasks are ending ahead of schedule.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* CPI < 1 indicates the project is spending more than planned (over budget). SPI > 1 indicates tasks are ending ahead of schedule.
    *   Value 1.0 is right on targets; values below 1 are negative/late.
