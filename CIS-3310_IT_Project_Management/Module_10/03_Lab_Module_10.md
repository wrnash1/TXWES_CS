## Scenario Background

Redstone Health Network is a mid-sized regional hospital system with 6 hospitals and 22 outpatient clinics. The IT leadership team is launching a Patient Portal Upgrade Project to replace the current patient-facing web portal with a modern, mobile-responsive platform. The new portal must support appointment scheduling, prescription refill requests, lab result viewing, and secure messaging with care teams.

The project has a $900,000 budget and an 18-month timeline. The go-live deadline is tied to a state health data reporting mandate that cannot be moved.

The project team has identified four major components of work:

1. Portal software platform — a new patient-facing web and mobile application
2. Identity and access management (IAM) module — single sign-on and multi-factor authentication
3. Integration layer — connections between the portal and existing EHR, lab, and pharmacy systems
4. End-user training — staff training for 1,800 clinical and administrative employees

The internal IT team has strong skills in EHR integration and custom development but has no experience with modern IAM implementation. The team has moderate capacity constraints due to two other concurrent IT projects. Redstone's executive team has placed a premium on data security and has expressed concern about sharing patient identity data with vendors.
