**Question 1**
Which contract type carries the highest cost risk for the buyer but low risk for the seller?
*   A) Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP)
*   B) Cost-Reimbursable (CR)
*   C) Time and Materials (T&M)
*   D) Fixed-Price-Incentive-Fee (FPIF)
*   **Correct Answer:** B) In cost-reimbursable contracts, the buyer pays all of the seller's actual costs plus an agreed fee; any cost overrun is fully borne by the buyer, making it the highest-risk contract type for the buyer.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* Under CR contracts, if the project takes longer or costs more than anticipated, the buyer pays the difference. The seller has minimal cost risk because all legitimate costs are reimbursed.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Firm-Fixed-Price puts cost risk on the seller, not the buyer. If the seller's actual costs exceed the fixed price, the seller absorbs the overrun.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* T&M is a shared risk arrangement—the buyer pays actual hours and materials, which can grow unpredictably. While risky, it is not the highest-risk type for the buyer; CR contracts are riskier because there is typically no ceiling on reimbursable costs.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* FPIF is a variant of fixed-price with an incentive structure to encourage cost savings; it still puts the majority of cost risk on the seller, as in all fixed-price contracts.

**Question 2**
Which of the following best defines a **Request for Proposal (RFP)** in project procurement?
*   A) A formal document issued to invite vendors to submit a proposed approach, qualifications, and pricing for completing a complex scope of work.
*   B) A document issued to invite vendors to submit a price quote only, used for commodity purchases with fully defined specifications.
*   C) A legal agreement between the project manager and project sponsor defining the project's scope, timeline, and budget.
*   D) A project closure document that summarizes all vendor deliverables accepted and contract obligations fulfilled.
*   **Correct Answer:** A) An RFP is a formal procurement solicitation that asks vendors to describe both their methodology and their pricing, enabling the buyer to evaluate best value across technical approach and cost.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why A is correct:* RFPs are used for complex, non-standard work where multiple valid approaches exist. The buyer evaluates vendor proposals on criteria including technical merit, experience, and price—not price alone.
    *   *Why B is incorrect:* Inviting vendors to submit a price only for a commodity purchase describes a Request for Quote (RFQ) or Invitation for Bid (IFB), not an RFP.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* An agreement between PM and sponsor defining scope/timeline/budget describes the project charter or project management plan, not a procurement solicitation document.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* A document summarizing accepted deliverables and fulfilled contract obligations describes a procurement closure document or final acceptance document—this is a closing output, not a planning/solicitation document.

**Question 3**
During stakeholder analysis, the project team discovers that the VP of Finance has high power (influence) over project funding but low interest in day-to-day activities. Which stakeholder engagement strategy is most appropriate?
*   A) Manage Closely – schedule weekly meetings and involve in all decisions
*   B) Monitor Only – send minimal updates and involve only at final milestones
*   C) Keep Satisfied – provide regular high-level updates and involve for key funding decisions
*   D) Keep Informed – send detailed daily status reports about all project activities
*   **Correct Answer:** C) High-power, low-interest stakeholders should be "kept satisfied"—they need to be informed about significant decisions that affect funding, but do not want to be burdened with operational detail.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why C is correct:* The Power/Interest Grid places high power/low interest stakeholders in the "Keep Satisfied" quadrant. These stakeholders can block or kill the project if unhappy, so the PM must ensure they remain supportive—through concise, timely updates on issues that matter to them.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* "Manage Closely" is for high-power, high-interest stakeholders who want to be involved in decisions. Applying this to a low-interest VP will overwhelm them with unwanted detail and waste PM time.
    *   *Why B is incorrect:* "Monitor Only" is for low-power, low-interest stakeholders who need minimal engagement. A VP with funding authority who is ignored could become a significant blocker if a key issue is not escalated to them.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Sending daily detailed reports to a high-power, low-interest executive is a misuse of communication bandwidth and will likely irritate the stakeholder rather than satisfy them.

**Question 4**
A project vendor consistently misses the contractually agreed 4-hour response time for critical system outages. What project management tool defines this performance expectation and the consequences of missing it?
*   A) Risk Register
*   B) Change Control Log
*   C) Service Level Agreement (SLA)
*   D) Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
*   **Correct Answer:** C) A Service Level Agreement (SLA) is the contractual document that defines minimum performance standards (such as response time) and the penalties or remedies that apply when the vendor fails to meet them.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why C is correct:* SLAs formally document the performance commitments vendors must meet and what happens when they don't (e.g., financial credits, contract termination rights). Response time thresholds are a classic SLA metric.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* The Risk Register identifies and tracks project risks; it would note "vendor response time failures" as a risk, but it does not define the contractual performance standard.
    *   *Why B is incorrect:* A Change Control Log documents approved and rejected change requests to project scope, schedule, and budget; it has no role in defining or enforcing vendor performance standards.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* The WBS decomposes the project scope into work packages; it defines what work must be done, not the performance standards that apply to vendor-delivered services.

**Question 5**
A stakeholder who was very supportive at the project kickoff has become increasingly resistant to the project, raising objections in steering committee meetings. Which action should the project manager take first?
*   A) Escalate to the project sponsor and request the stakeholder be removed from the committee.
*   B) Conduct a one-on-one conversation with the stakeholder to understand the concerns driving their resistance.
*   C) Document the resistance in the issue log and continue the project as planned.
*   D) Reduce the stakeholder's involvement level to prevent further disruption.
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Understanding the root cause of stakeholder resistance through direct conversation is the first and most professional response—resistance often signals unaddressed concerns that, if resolved, can restore support.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* PMI's Stakeholder Engagement approach emphasizes proactive communication. A stakeholder's shift from support to resistance is a warning sign. Engaging them directly to understand their concerns is more likely to resolve the issue than escalation or avoidance.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Escalating to remove a stakeholder immediately is an extreme response that will likely damage relationships and politicize the project. It should be a last resort, not a first action.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Simply logging the resistance without addressing it allows it to escalate. A growing resistant stakeholder can derail the project if their concerns are ignored.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Reducing a stakeholder's involvement without explanation may increase their suspicion and resistance. If they are a high-power stakeholder, disengaging them could have serious consequences for the project.
