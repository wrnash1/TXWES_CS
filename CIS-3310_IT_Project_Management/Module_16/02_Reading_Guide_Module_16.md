### Introduction
Welcome to **Module 16 - Final Exam Prep & Project+ Certification**! This is your final module, and its purpose is comprehensive review. You have covered the full scope of IT project management—from project initiation and planning through execution, monitoring, and closure, including both traditional (PMBOK) and Agile approaches. Now it is time to consolidate that knowledge, identify your weak areas, and prepare strategically for the CompTIA Project+ (PK0-005) certification exam.

The Project+ exam consists of approximately 90 questions (maximum) in a 90-minute window. Questions are scenario-based and multiple-choice. The passing score is 710 on a 900-point scale. Use this module to review all previous content, practice under timed conditions, and focus your final study time where it will have the greatest impact.

### 1. High-Yield Glossary
Review these critical integrated concepts across the full course:

*   **Project+ Exam Domain Breakdown (PK0-005)**: The five exam domains with approximate weights: Project Management Concepts (33%), Project Life Cycle Phases (30%), Tools and Documentation (20%), Basics of IT Projects (10%), and Interpersonal and Professional Skills (7%). The first two domains together account for 63% of the exam—prioritize process group knowledge, knowledge area concepts, and lifecycle activities.
*   **Key Formula Summary**: CV = EV - AC; SV = EV - PV; CPI = EV/AC; SPI = EV/PV; EAC = BAC/CPI; Communication Channels = N(N-1)/2; Float = LS - ES (or LF - EF). These are the calculation questions you are most likely to encounter. Write them from memory before each practice session to ensure retention.
*   **PMBOK 10 Knowledge Areas**: Integration, Scope, Schedule, Cost, Quality, Resource, Communications, Risk, Procurement, Stakeholder. Each knowledge area maps to specific processes and documents. For the exam, be able to identify which knowledge area owns a given document or process (e.g., Risk Register → Risk Management; WBS → Scope Management; RACI → Resource Management).

### 2. Certification Exam Tips
*   **Project+ Tip – Read Every Option Before Answering**: CompTIA scenario questions are designed so that multiple answers seem plausible. Always read all four choices before selecting. The best answer is the one that follows PMI/CompTIA methodology most precisely—not just the one that sounds correct at first glance.
*   **CAPM Tip – Use Process of Elimination**: If you don't know the answer, eliminate the two most obviously wrong options first. Many CAPM questions narrow to two possible answers. Understanding the PMI framework deeply usually resolves the final choice.
*   **Time Management**: With 90 questions in 90 minutes, you have approximately 1 minute per question. Flag difficult questions and return to them—don't spend 5 minutes on one question and leave 5 questions unanswered.
*   **Final Study Strategy**: In your last week of study: (1) Review your weakest domain by retaking module quizzes for those topics. (2) Take at least one full-length timed practice exam. (3) Review all EVM and CPM formulas daily. (4) Re-read the glossary terms from every module once more.
*   **Study Resource**: For free Project+ practice exams and exam objectives, visit the official CompTIA page: [CompTIA Project+ PK0-005 Exam Objectives](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/project). For video-based final review, search [YouTube: CompTIA Project+ PK0-005 Full Course Final Review](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=comptia+project%2B+PK0-005+full+course+final+review+2024).
