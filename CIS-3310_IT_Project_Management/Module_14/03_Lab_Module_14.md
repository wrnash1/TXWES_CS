## Scenario Background

Cascade Health Network is a regional healthcare organization with twelve hospital locations. The organization is implementing a new Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system to replace three aging systems: financial management, supply chain, and human resources. The project has a total budget of $4,800,000 and an 18-month timeline.

Key project facts:

- The internal IT team has 14 staff members, none of whom have ERP implementation experience
- The selected ERP platform (HealthCore ERP) is a commercial off-the-shelf product that requires specialized configuration expertise from certified implementation partners
- Three components of the project have been identified as procurement candidates: ERP implementation services, hardware infrastructure for on-premises hosting, and change management and training services
- The project sponsor wants to minimize Cascade's financial exposure because the organization has had two large IT projects run significantly over budget in the past three years
- The implementation partner market for HealthCore ERP has four certified vendors with varying experience levels and pricing models
- Federal healthcare regulations require that all ERP data handling meet HIPAA compliance standards, which must be enforced through contractual requirements
