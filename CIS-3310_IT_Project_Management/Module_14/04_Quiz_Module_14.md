**Question 1**
What is the primary role of a Change Control Board (CCB) in project management?
*   A) To write programming code
*   B) To review, evaluate, and approve or reject requested project scope modifications
*   C) To buy software licenses
*   D) To hire developers
*   **Correct Answer:** B) The CCB is a formal group of stakeholders that verifies changes before they are integrated into baselines.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* The CCB is a formal group of stakeholders that verifies changes before they are integrated into baselines.
    *   CCB handles scope governance, not raw development.
