**Question 1**
What does the 'A' stand for in a RACI assignment matrix?
*   A) Assigned
*   B) Accountable
*   C) Authorized
*   D) Approved
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Accountable represents the single person who owns the outcome and is ultimately answerable for the correct and thorough completion of the task (only one 'A' is permitted per task).
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* The RACI model distinguishes between the person who does the work (Responsible) and the person who owns the outcome (Accountable). Only one person can be Accountable per task to ensure clear ownership.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* "Assigned" is not a RACI role; the closest RACI role for the person performing work is "Responsible" (R), not assigned.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* "Authorized" is not a RACI acronym component; authorization is a governance concept but is not one of the four RACI roles.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* "Approved" is not a RACI role. Approval is a process step that may be associated with the Accountable person, but the A stands specifically for Accountable.

**Question 2**
Which of the following best defines **Resource Conflicts** in project management?
*   A) Situations where two or more project activities require the same resource at the same time, or where project demands compete with operational demands for the same people or equipment.
*   B) Disagreements between project team members about technical approaches or work priorities.
*   C) Disputes between the project manager and functional managers over budget allocations.
*   D) Competing requirements from multiple stakeholders that cannot all be satisfied within the project scope.
*   **Correct Answer:** A) Resource conflicts occur when the same person, piece of equipment, or material is required for multiple activities simultaneously, creating an over-allocation that must be resolved through leveling or negotiation.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why A is correct:* Resource conflicts are a scheduling and resource management issue. They are resolved through resource leveling (adjusting the schedule), negotiating with functional managers, or crashing/fast-tracking the schedule.
    *   *Why B is incorrect:* Technical disagreements between team members describe interpersonal or team conflict, which is addressed through conflict resolution techniques (collaboration, compromise)—a different topic from resource allocation conflicts.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Disputes over budget allocations describe a governance or stakeholder issue, not a resource scheduling conflict.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Competing stakeholder requirements describe a scope management challenge (managing expectations and requirements), not a resource loading conflict.

**Question 3**
A project team has 6 members. Using the communication channels formula N × (N-1) / 2, how many communication channels exist?
*   A) 12
*   B) 15
*   C) 18
*   D) 30
*   **Correct Answer:** B) 6 × (6-1) / 2 = 6 × 5 / 2 = 30 / 2 = 15 communication channels.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* The formula N(N-1)/2 calculates all unique pairwise connections. With 6 team members: 6 × 5 = 30 ÷ 2 = 15. This formula is a common Project+ exam calculation.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* 12 would result from N × (N-1) / 2.5 or some other incorrect variation of the formula; it does not match the standard calculation.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* 18 would result from 6 × 3 or some other incorrect arithmetic. The correct denominator in the formula is 2, not 2/3 of the numerator.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* 30 is an intermediate step in the calculation (6 × 5 = 30) but must be divided by 2. Forgetting to divide by 2 is the most common formula mistake—watch for this trap.

**Question 4**
A project manager sends a weekly status report email to all project stakeholders. No reply is expected; stakeholders simply receive the update. Which communication method does this represent?
*   A) Interactive Communication
*   B) Pull Communication
*   C) Push Communication
*   D) Formal Communication
*   **Correct Answer:** C) Push communication is a one-way method where information is sent directly to specific recipients who do not need to actively seek it out; status report emails are a classic example.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why C is correct:* Push communication (emails, memos, letters, reports) sends information to a defined audience. The sender controls distribution and timing. No dialogue is required.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Interactive communication is real-time, two-way exchange (meetings, phone calls, video calls). A one-way email with no expected reply is not interactive.
    *   *Why B is incorrect:* Pull communication is stored in a central location that recipients access on demand (e.g., a project SharePoint site, document repository). The recipient must actively go retrieve the information—unlike an email sent directly to inboxes.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* "Formal communication" refers to whether the communication follows organizational protocols (written reports, official meetings), not the method of delivery. All three types (interactive, push, pull) can be formal or informal.

**Question 5**
During a software project, the project manager discovers that the lead developer is assigned to three different critical tasks simultaneously during the same two-week sprint. Which resource management technique should the PM apply first to resolve this conflict?
*   A) Crashing – add another developer to the team immediately
*   B) Resource Leveling – adjust the schedule so the developer's tasks are sequenced rather than overlapping
*   C) Fast-tracking – overlap unrelated tasks from other team members to make room
*   D) Gold plating – reduce the quality requirements for two of the three tasks
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Resource leveling adjusts activity timing to eliminate over-allocation, ensuring the developer's tasks are sequenced so workload stays within capacity—typically the first and lowest-risk corrective action.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* Resource leveling is the standard technique for resolving over-allocation. It adjusts start and finish dates of activities (often extending the schedule) so no resource is required beyond its available capacity.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Crashing (adding resources) is a valid schedule compression technique but is typically applied when the schedule needs to be shortened, not as the first response to over-allocation. It also increases cost.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Fast-tracking overlaps activities to compress the schedule; it increases risk and is not the appropriate response to an over-allocated resource situation.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Reducing quality requirements (gold plating in reverse) is never an acceptable response to resource conflicts. It directly compromises deliverable quality and violates the quality management plan.
