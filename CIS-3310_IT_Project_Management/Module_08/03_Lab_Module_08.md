## Scenario Background

Clearwater County is implementing a new Citizen Services Portal — a web-based platform that consolidates permit applications, tax payment, and service requests into a single interface for county residents. The project team and stakeholders include:

- Diana Reyes — Project Manager
- Kevin Osei — Web Developer (Technical Lead)
- Priya Gupta — UX Designer
- Marcus Bell — Database Administrator
- Sonia Vasquez — Business Analyst
- Chief Information Officer (CIO) — executive sponsor (Diana's direct reporting line)
- County Administrator — approves all public-facing technology releases
- IT Help Desk Manager — will support the portal post-launch
- Communications Director — manages resident-facing messaging and launch announcements
- Vendor Representative — third-party accessibility testing contractor
- Three Department Heads — Permits, Tax, and Community Services departments (separate stakeholders)

The project has a 6-month timeline. Diana must build a communications approach that keeps all parties informed without overwhelming any individual stakeholder.
