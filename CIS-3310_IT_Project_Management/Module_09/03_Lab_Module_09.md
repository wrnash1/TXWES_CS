## Scenario Background

Northgate University is implementing a new cloud-based Student Information System (SIS) to replace a 15-year-old on-premises system. The project has a $1.2 million budget, a 12-month timeline, and a go-live date set for the start of the fall enrollment period. Missing the go-live date would delay student registration for an entire semester, which is an unacceptable outcome.

The project team has identified the following twelve risks during the initial risk identification workshop:

1. The primary SIS vendor may miss contracted delivery milestones due to its own development backlog.
2. Data migration from the legacy system may introduce errors in student academic records.
3. The university's IT infrastructure team may lack the capacity to support the cloud migration alongside other scheduled projects.
4. Faculty adoption of the new system may be slower than planned due to resistance to change.
5. A competing university in the region recently adopted the same SIS platform, which may allow Northgate to leverage that institution's lessons learned and accelerate implementation.
6. Integration with the financial aid system may fail due to undocumented legacy APIs.
7. The project's technical lead may leave the university before go-live.
8. The vendor's cloud platform may experience an outage during final user acceptance testing.
9. New state-level data privacy regulations may take effect mid-project, requiring additional security controls.
10. Federal funding for higher education IT modernization may become available, potentially subsidizing 15% of the project cost.
11. The university's change management lead may be able to repurpose training materials from a prior HR system rollout, reducing training development time by three weeks.
12. The vendor may release a major platform update during the project, introducing features not yet documented in the project scope.

## Part 3: Risk Response Plans

### Part 3 Objective

Develop risk response plans for four of the five threats documented in Part 2.

### Part 3 Instructions

For four of the five Risk Register entries you completed in Part 2, write a risk response plan. Each response plan must:

1. Select a response strategy (Avoid, Transfer, Mitigate, or Accept)
2. Describe the specific action the project team will take to implement that strategy
3. Explain why you selected this strategy over at least one alternative
4. Identify any secondary risks created by the response
