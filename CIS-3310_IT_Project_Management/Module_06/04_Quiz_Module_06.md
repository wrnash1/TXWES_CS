**Question 1**
Which cost estimation technique uses historical data from similar projects as the basis for the current estimate?
*   A) Parametric Estimating
*   B) Analogous (Top-down) Estimating
*   C) Bottom-up Estimating
*   D) Three-point Estimating
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Analogous estimating compares the current scope to past projects, providing quick but less precise estimates.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Analogous estimating compares the current scope to past projects, providing quick but less precise estimates.
    *   Parametric estimating uses statistical modeling (e.g. cost per square foot).
