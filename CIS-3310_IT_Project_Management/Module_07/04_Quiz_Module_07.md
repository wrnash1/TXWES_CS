## Question 1

A project manager is creating a responsibility assignment matrix and needs to designate who is ultimately answerable for the completion of a specific deliverable. Which RACI role should be assigned to that person?

- A) Responsible
- B) Accountable
- C) Consulted
- D) Informed

Correct Answer: B) The Accountable role identifies the single person who owns the outcome of a task and is ultimately answerable for its success or failure. Only one person can hold the A designation per task.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why B is correct:* The Accountable role is the governance role — the person who signs off and bears ultimate responsibility. The RACI rule requiring exactly one A per task exists to prevent ambiguity about ownership.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Responsible designates the person or people who do the actual work. Multiple Rs are permitted on the same task. The R role is about execution, not ownership.
- *Why C is incorrect:* Consulted identifies subject matter experts whose input is needed before a decision is finalized. They provide input but do not own the outcome.
- *Why D is incorrect:* Informed identifies stakeholders who receive progress updates. They have no active role in completing the work and are not answerable for results.

## Question 2

A RACI matrix for a software deployment project shows that both the Project Manager and the IT Director are listed as Accountable for the go-live approval task. Which RACI rule does this violate?

- A) Every task must have at least one Responsible person
- B) Every task must have exactly one Accountable person
- C) Consulted and Informed roles cannot appear on the same task
- D) A person cannot be both Responsible and Accountable simultaneously

Correct Answer: B) The RACI rule that is violated is that every task must have exactly one Accountable person. Listing two people as Accountable for the same task creates shared ownership, which eliminates clear accountability.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why B is correct:* The "exactly one A per task" rule is the most frequently tested RACI rule on the Project+ exam. When two people share accountability, there is no clear owner if the task fails.
- *Why A is incorrect:* The scenario does not describe a task with zero Responsible persons; it describes two people as Accountable. This rule is not violated.
- *Why C is incorrect:* There is no RACI rule prohibiting C and I from appearing on the same task. Both are common on complex tasks where multiple parties need to be consulted or informed.
- *Why D is incorrect:* A person can legitimately be both Responsible and Accountable on the same task. This is written as A/R and is explicitly permitted in RACI methodology.

## Question 3

An organization's project manager must negotiate with the IT department manager to obtain developer resources for a project. The PM has no authority to directly assign or release team members. Which organizational structure best describes this environment?

- A) Projectized
- B) Strong Matrix
- C) Functional
- D) Balanced Matrix

Correct Answer: C) In a Functional organization, employees report to functional managers (such as a department head), and the PM has little to no formal authority over resources. The PM must negotiate and coordinate rather than direct.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why C is correct:* The defining characteristic of a Functional organization is that resource control rests entirely with the functional manager. PMs in this structure are coordinators, not commanders.
- *Why A is incorrect:* In a Projectized organization, the PM has full authority over resources, who report directly to the PM. No negotiation with a functional manager is needed.
- *Why B is incorrect:* In a Strong Matrix, the PM has moderate to high authority — greater than the functional manager. The PM does not need to negotiate to obtain resources in the same way.
- *Why D is incorrect:* In a Balanced Matrix, authority is shared between the PM and the functional manager. While some negotiation occurs, it is a shared arrangement, not a situation where the PM has no authority.

## Question 4

Which of the following conflict resolution techniques is identified by PMI as the MOST preferred approach because it produces a win-win outcome?

- A) Compromise/Reconcile
- B) Smooth/Accommodate
- C) Force/Direct
- D) Collaborate/Problem-Solve

Correct Answer: D) Collaborate/Problem-Solve is PMI's most preferred conflict resolution technique because both parties work together toward a mutually satisfying solution, producing a durable, win-win resolution.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why D is correct:* PMI consistently identifies Collaborate/Problem-Solve as the best approach because it addresses the root cause of the conflict rather than managing symptoms. The resolution holds because both parties are satisfied.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Compromise/Reconcile is PMI's second preferred technique. Both parties give up something to reach a middle ground (win-some/lose-some), but it is less durable than collaboration because neither party is fully satisfied.
- *Why B is incorrect:* Smooth/Accommodate emphasizes areas of agreement and minimizes the disagreement. It provides temporary relief but does not resolve the underlying issue.
- *Why C is incorrect:* Force/Direct is PMI's least preferred technique. It produces a win-lose outcome and generates resentment. It should only be used in genuine emergencies requiring an immediate decision.

## Question 5

A project team is in Week 2 of a new project. Team members are polite and cooperative, but several have privately expressed uncertainty about their roles and responsibilities. Formal processes have not yet been established. Which Tuckman stage is the team currently in?

- A) Storming
- B) Norming
- C) Forming
- D) Performing

Correct Answer: C) The team is in the Forming stage. Characteristics of Forming include polite behavior, uncertainty about roles, and the absence of established processes and norms.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why C is correct:* Forming is the first stage, marked by politeness, uncertainty about roles, and limited conflict. Team members are getting acquainted and have not yet challenged each other or developed collaborative norms.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Storming is characterized by conflict, competition, and challenges to authority. The scenario describes no conflict — only uncertainty and politeness.
- *Why B is incorrect:* Norming occurs after the team has resolved initial conflicts and established collaborative norms and trust. The scenario describes the absence of norms, not their presence.
- *Why D is incorrect:* Performing is characterized by high productivity, motivated self-direction, and minimal PM intervention. The scenario describes a team that has just started and is still uncertain about roles.

## Question 6

A PM notices that one developer and one business analyst on the same team are in serious conflict over project requirements priorities. The PM wants to find a long-term solution that satisfies both team members. Which action best reflects the Collaborate/Problem-Solve technique?

- A) Tell both team members that their priorities will be averaged together
- B) Direct the developer to follow the business analyst's requirements list without discussion
- C) Facilitate a joint working session where both parties articulate their needs and develop a shared prioritization approach
- D) Temporarily assign the two team members to separate work streams to reduce contact

Correct Answer: C) Facilitating a joint working session where both parties articulate their needs and work toward a shared solution is the practical implementation of Collaborate/Problem-Solve.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why C is correct:* Collaboration requires both parties to actively engage, share their concerns, and co-create a solution. A joint session where both express needs and build a shared approach is the textbook implementation.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Averaging priorities is a form of Compromise/Reconcile, not Collaboration. It produces a middle-ground result where neither party is fully satisfied rather than a jointly crafted solution.
- *Why B is incorrect:* Directing one party to comply with the other is Force/Direct — the least preferred technique. It imposes a winner and creates resentment.
- *Why D is incorrect:* Assigning team members to separate work streams to avoid contact is Withdraw/Avoid. It postpones the conflict rather than resolving it and may cause downstream integration problems.

## Question 7

A project manager adjusts the project schedule to move a task that was assigned to an over-allocated developer from Week 5 to Week 7, when the developer has available capacity. Which resource management technique is the PM using?

- A) Crashing
- B) Fast-tracking
- C) Resource leveling
- D) Scope reduction

Correct Answer: C) Resource leveling adjusts the schedule to resolve over-allocation by delaying activities until the overloaded resource has available capacity. This typically extends the overall project schedule.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why C is correct:* Resource leveling is specifically the technique for resolving over-allocation. By moving the task to a week when the developer is available, the PM eliminates the overallocation at the cost of potentially extending the schedule.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Crashing adds resources (such as overtime or additional workers) to shorten the schedule. It does not involve moving tasks to a later date to match resource availability.
- *Why B is incorrect:* Fast-tracking overlaps tasks that were originally planned in sequence to compress the schedule. It does not address resource availability constraints.
- *Why D is incorrect:* Scope reduction removes work from the project entirely. Moving a task to a later date retains all the work — it only changes when it is performed.

## Question 8

According to Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory, which of the following is classified as a hygiene factor rather than a motivator?

- A) Recognition for a job well done
- B) Opportunities for professional advancement
- C) Base salary and working conditions
- D) Achievement of a challenging deliverable

Correct Answer: C) Salary and working conditions are hygiene factors according to Herzberg. Their absence causes dissatisfaction, but their presence does not create motivation. Only motivators (such as recognition and achievement) drive positive engagement.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why C is correct:* Herzberg defined hygiene factors as environmental conditions (pay, policies, physical workspace) that prevent dissatisfaction when adequate but do not generate satisfaction or motivation when present. A salary increase alone will not produce long-term performance improvement.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Recognition is a motivator in Herzberg's model. It addresses intrinsic fulfillment and drives positive behavior. Recognizing good work increases motivation.
- *Why B is incorrect:* Advancement opportunities are motivators. They address growth needs and create genuine engagement because they contribute to career development and self-actualization.
- *Why D is incorrect:* Achievement is one of Herzberg's core motivators. Successfully completing a challenging deliverable produces a sense of accomplishment that drives continued high performance.

## Question 9

During a project retrospective, the team notes that Priya and James now resolve most technical disagreements independently without escalating to the PM. Meetings run efficiently and the team regularly delivers above expectations with minimal direction. Which Tuckman stage does this describe?

- A) Forming
- B) Storming
- C) Norming
- D) Performing

Correct Answer: D) The team is in the Performing stage. Performing is characterized by self-direction, high productivity, minimal need for PM intervention, and a team that resolves issues autonomously.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why D is correct:* In Performing, the team is fully functional and motivated. The PM shifts to a delegating leadership style and focuses on removing obstacles rather than directing work. Autonomous conflict resolution between team members is a hallmark of this stage.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Forming is the initial stage where roles are unclear and norms do not yet exist. The scenario describes a team with well-established norms and high productivity.
- *Why B is incorrect:* Storming is characterized by conflict, competition, and tension. The scenario describes successful collaboration and independent conflict resolution — the opposite of Storming behavior.
- *Why C is incorrect:* Norming describes a team that is building trust and establishing norms. While closer to the scenario than Forming or Storming, Norming teams still require more PM involvement than the fully self-directing team described.

## Question 10

A project manager is building a RACI matrix and wants to indicate that the change management lead should receive updates on ETL pipeline development progress but has no input into technical decisions. Which RACI designation is correct?

- A) R — because the change management lead is involved with the task
- B) A — because the change management lead is the most senior stakeholder on the communication
- C) C — because the change management lead needs to stay connected to the project
- D) I — because the change management lead receives information but does not provide input

Correct Answer: D) Informed (I) designates a stakeholder who receives one-way communication about task progress or decisions. They do not provide input and are not involved in execution or approval.

Distractor Analysis:

- *Why D is correct:* Informed is the correct designation when communication is one-directional — the stakeholder receives updates but does not act on them or contribute decisions. This is exactly the scenario described.
- *Why A is incorrect:* Responsible designates someone who performs the work. The change management lead is not doing ETL development work; they are receiving updates about it.
- *Why B is incorrect:* Accountable designates the single person who owns the task outcome. Seniority alone does not determine the A designation. There is already an Accountable person for ETL development, and only one A per task is permitted.
- *Why C is incorrect:* Consulted designates someone whose input is solicited before decisions are finalized — two-way communication. The scenario explicitly states the change management lead has no input into technical decisions, which rules out Consulted.
