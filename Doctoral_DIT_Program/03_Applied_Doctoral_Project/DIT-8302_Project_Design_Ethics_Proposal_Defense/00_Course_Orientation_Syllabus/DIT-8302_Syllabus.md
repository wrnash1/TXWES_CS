## 1. Course Description
Methodological architecture, ethical review, IRB protocols, feasibility assessment, project prospectus development, and formal defense. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 2, 5
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing project design, ethics, and proposal defense.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Selecting Methodological Approach (DSR vs Empirical) — Matching Research Design to Applied Problem Scopes
* **Module 02:** Data Collection Instruments & Protocol Design — Telemetry Extraction, Surveys, and Elite Interview Guides
* **Module 03:** Ethical Oversight & IRB Protocols at Texas Wesleyan — Human Subjects Protections, Confidentiality, and Data Security
* **Module 04:** Organizational Feasibility & Resource Budgeting — Gantt Scheduling, Technology Access, and Risk Contingency
* **Module 05:** Drafting the Complete Doctoral Project Proposal — Integrating Chapters 1, 2, and 3 into Cohesive Manuscript
* **Module 06:** Proposal Rehearsal & Committee Presentation Polish — Slide Design, Defense Etiquette, and Anticipating Scrutiny
* **Module 07:** Formal Doctoral Project Proposal Defense — Oral Proposal Defense before Doctoral Committee
