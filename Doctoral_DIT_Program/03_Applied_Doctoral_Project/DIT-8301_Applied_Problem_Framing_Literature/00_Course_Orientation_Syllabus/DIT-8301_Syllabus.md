## 1. Course Description
Scoping an authentic organizational IT problem, critical literature synthesis, stakeholder mapping, and conceptual framework formulation. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 1, 5
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing applied problem framing and literature synthesis.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Identifying the Authentic Practice Problem — Distinguishing Symptoms from Root Organizational IT Causes
* **Module 02:** Contextualizing Problem Within Organizational Setting — Sponsor Engagement, Access Permissions, and Scope Boundaries
* **Module 03:** Systematic Literature Search & Database Querying — IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and Scopus Systematic Queries
* **Module 04:** Constructing the Doctoral Synthesis Matrix — Analyzing Convergent and Divergent Academic Findings
* **Module 05:** Formulating Defensible Research Questions — Applied Inquiry Questions vs. Commercial Consulting Goals
* **Module 06:** Drafting Chapter 1 & Chapter 2 Outlines — Background, Problem Statement, Purpose, and Literature Review
* **Module 07:** Doctoral Project Milestone 1 Defense — Problem Framing & Literature Synthesis Prospectus Defense
