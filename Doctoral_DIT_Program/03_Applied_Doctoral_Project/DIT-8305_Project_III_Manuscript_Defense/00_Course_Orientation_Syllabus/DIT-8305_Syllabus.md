## 1. Course Description
Complete doctoral manuscript integration, peer-review polish, oral defense before faculty committee, and academic approval. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 5, 6
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing applied doctoral project iii: manuscript and defense.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Manuscript Structural Integration (Chapters 1–5) — Formatting, Citations, Appendices, and Institutional Style
* **Module 02:** Doctoral Writing Precision & Scholarly Polish — Eliminating Ambiguity, Assertive Voice, and Flow
* **Module 03:** Formal Committee Review & Revision Iteration — Addressing Faculty Committee Feedback and Revisions
* **Module 04:** Developing the Defense Slide Deck & Executive Summary — High-Impact Visual Defense for Academic and Industry Peers
* **Module 05:** Oral Defense Simulation & Mock Cross-Examination — Defense Preparation, Rebuttal Strategy, and Delivery
* **Module 06:** Formal Oral Defense of the Applied Doctoral Project — Terminal Oral Defense before Doctoral Committee
* **Module 07:** Post-Defense Final Edits & University Archival — Submitting Final Bound/Digital Manuscript for Degree Conferral
