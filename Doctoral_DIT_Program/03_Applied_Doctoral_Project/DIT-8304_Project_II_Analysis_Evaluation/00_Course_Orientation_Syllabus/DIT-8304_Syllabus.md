## 1. Course Description
Analytical evaluation of findings, validity threat testing, organizational implications, and evidence-based intervention refinement. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 3, 5
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing applied doctoral project ii: analysis and evaluation.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Executing Primary Statistical or Qualitative Analysis — Hypothesis Testing, DSR Evaluation, or Qualitative Thematics
* **Module 02:** Evaluating Intervention Performance & ROI — Pre-vs-Post Metrics, Residual Risk, and Efficiency Gains
* **Module 03:** Triangulating Empirical Findings with Theory — Relating Results Back to Literature and National Standards
* **Module 04:** Stress-Testing Threats to Validity & Limitations — Internal, External, and Construct Validity Defenses
* **Module 05:** Drafting Chapter 4 (Findings and Results) — Data Visualizations, Empirical Tables, and Narrative Synthesis
* **Module 06:** Drafting Chapter 5 (Implications and Recommendations) — Strategic Recommendations for IT Practitioners
* **Module 07:** Doctoral Project Milestone 4 Review — Comprehensive Analysis & Results Defense to Chair
