## 1. Course Description
Executive briefing creation, conference manuscript preparation, peer-reviewed article submission, and organizational impact evaluation. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 4, 6
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing technology impact and dissemination practicum.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Translating Doctoral Research for Industry Executives — Crafting C-Suite Whitepapers and Executive Summaries
* **Module 02:** Formulating Peer-Reviewed Journal Submissions — Adapting Applied Findings for IEEE, ACM, or MISQ Journals
* **Module 03:** Preparing Academic & Industry Conference Presentations — Submitting Proposals for Academic & Executive Conferences
* **Module 04:** Architectural Intellectual Property & Open Licensing — Navigating Patents, Copyrights, and Artifact Dissemination
* **Module 05:** Measuring Long-Term Technology Transfer & Impact — Post-Implementation Organizational Auditing Frameworks
* **Module 06:** Doctoral Leadership & Mentorship in Industry — The D.I.T. Scholar-Practitioner Identity in Enterprise
* **Module 07:** Culminating Dissemination Portfolio Defense — Final Public Dissemination Defense and Program Capstone
