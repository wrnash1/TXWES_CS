## 1. Course Description
Design-science research (DSR), qualitative case study design, semi-structured elite interviews, thematic coding, and methodological triangulation. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 1, 5
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing qualitative and design-based inquiry in technology.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Qualitative Foundations in Information Systems — Philosophical Paradigms and Interpretive Research
* **Module 02:** Design Science Research (DSR) Methodologies — The Hevner DSR Framework and Artifact Creation
* **Module 03:** Elite Stakeholder Interviews & Delphi Studies — Protocol Formulation, Interviewing CIOs/CISOs, and Rigor
* **Module 04:** Qualitative Data Analysis & Thematic Coding — Inductive/Deductive Coding and Qualitative Software Tools
* **Module 05:** Organizational Case Study Methodology — Single vs. Multiple Embedded Case Designs (Yin Framework)
* **Module 06:** Methodological Triangulation & Research Ethics — IRB Navigation, Confidentiality, and Validity Standards
* **Module 07:** Culminating DSR Artifact Evaluation & Defense — Design-Science Artifact Defense and Rigor Justification
