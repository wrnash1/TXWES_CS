## 1. Course Description
Statistical inference, multivariate regression, reproducible data pipelines, and quantitative modeling of organizational technology performance. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 3, 5
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing quantitative analytics for technology leaders.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Statistical Inference in Enterprise Technology — Probability Distributions and Sampling Architectures
* **Module 02:** Multivariate Regression & Econometric Modeling — OLS, Logistic Regression, and Interaction Effects
* **Module 03:** Quasi-Experimental Design & Causal Inference — Difference-in-Differences and Propensity Score Matching
* **Module 04:** IT Performance Metrics & Operational Telemetry — MTTR, Availability Modeling, and SLA Statistical Control
* **Module 05:** Reproducible Analytical Pipelines (R & Python) — Jupyter/RMarkdown Workflows and Data Governance
* **Module 06:** Evaluating Predictive Models in Enterprise Systems — ROC-AUC, Precision-Recall, and Overfitting Mitigation
* **Module 07:** Culminating Quantitative Empirical Briefing — Executive Data Defense and Empirical Modeling Report
