## 1. Course Description
Enterprise IT strategy alignment, COBIT 2019, ITIL 4, ESG criteria in technology, fiduciary oversight, and ethical dilemmas in emerging tech. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 2, 4
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing technology strategy, governance, and ethics.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Strategic Alignment of IT and Enterprise Mission — Henderson-Venkatraman Strategic Alignment Model
* **Module 02:** Enterprise IT Governance Frameworks — COBIT 2019 Goals Cascade and ITIL 4 Service Value System
* **Module 03:** Fiduciary Duties of the Technology Executive — Board Governance, Audit Committees, and Fiduciary Care
* **Module 04:** Ethical Dilemmas in Emerging Technology Adoption — Algorithmic Bias, Surveillance Capitalism, and Labor Impact
* **Module 05:** Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG) in IT — Green Computing, Data Center Energy, and Sustainable IT
* **Module 06:** Executive Stakeholder Communication & Advocacy — Translating Technical Debt into Balance Sheet Exposure
* **Module 07:** Culminating Strategic IT Governance Blueprint — Enterprise Strategic Plan and Governance Framework Defense
