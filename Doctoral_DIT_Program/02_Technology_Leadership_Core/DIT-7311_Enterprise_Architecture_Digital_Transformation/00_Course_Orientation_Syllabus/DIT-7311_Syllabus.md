## 1. Course Description
TOGAF Standard 10th Edition, Zachman framework, legacy systems modernization, cloud-native migration patterns, and organizational change. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 1, 4
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing enterprise architecture and digital transformation.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Foundations of Enterprise Architecture — TOGAF Architecture Development Method (ADM) and Zachman Matrix
* **Module 02:** Business Architecture & Capability Mapping — Strategic Value Streams and Business Capability Modeling
* **Module 03:** Data & Application Architecture Integration — Event-Driven Architectures and Microservices Ecosystems
* **Module 04:** Technology Infrastructure & Hybrid Cloud Patterns — Multi-Cloud Interoperability and Edge Infrastructure
* **Module 05:** Legacy Modernization Roadmaps & Technical Debt — The Strangler Fig Pattern and Mainframe Decoupling
* **Module 06:** Measuring Digital Transformation ROI & Agility — Lead Time, Deployment Frequency, and Value Stream Metrics
* **Module 07:** Culminating Enterprise Architecture Blueprint — Target State Architecture Defense and Transition Roadmap
