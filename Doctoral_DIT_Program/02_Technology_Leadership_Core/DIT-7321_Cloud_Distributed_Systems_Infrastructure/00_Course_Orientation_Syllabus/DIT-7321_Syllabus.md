## 1. Course Description
Distributed systems consensus, CAP theorem, multi-cloud resilience, Kubernetes container orchestration, FinOps, and quantum-safe cryptography. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 2, 3
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing cloud, distributed systems, and emerging infrastructure.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Theoretical Foundations of Distributed Computing — CAP Theorem, PACELC, and Distributed Consensus (Raft/Paxos)
* **Module 02:** Cloud Infrastructure Resilience & SRE Principles — Site Reliability Engineering, Error Budgets, and SLIs/SLOs
* **Module 03:** Enterprise Container Orchestration & Service Meshes — Kubernetes Multi-Cluster Networking and Envoy/Istio Meshes
* **Module 04:** Multi-Cloud Networking & SD-WAN Interconnects — Cloud Exchange Hubs, Direct Connects, and BGP Transit
* **Module 05:** Cloud Financial Management (FinOps) & Economics — Unit Economics, Reserved Capacity, and Cloud Cost Optimization
* **Module 06:** Emerging Infrastructure: Edge Computing & Post-Quantum Cryptography — Quantum-Safe Migration (NIST FIPS 203/204) and Edge Compute
* **Module 07:** Culminating Cloud Infrastructure Resilience Strategy — Global Distributed Infrastructure Architecture Defense
