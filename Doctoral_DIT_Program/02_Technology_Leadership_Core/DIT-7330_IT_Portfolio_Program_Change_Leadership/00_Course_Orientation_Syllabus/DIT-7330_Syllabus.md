## 1. Course Description
Strategic technology portfolio optimization, program management offices (PMO), Kotter/Prosci change management, and IT talent retention. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 4, 6
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing it portfolio, program, and change leadership.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Strategic IT Portfolio Management & Capital Allocation — Balancing Run-the-Bank vs. Change-the-Bank Portfolios
* **Module 02:** Program Management Office (PMO) Optimization — Agile at Scale (SAFe/Spotify) vs. Predictive Stage-Gate Controls
* **Module 03:** Leading Organizational Technology Change — Kotter’s 8-Step Model and Prosci ADKAR Implementation
* **Module 04:** Executive Communication & Cross-Functional Alignment — Partnering with the CFO, CMO, and Chief Legal Officer
* **Module 05:** Building High-Performance Engineering Cultures — Psychological Safety, Developer Velocity, and Talent Retention
* **Module 06:** Mergers, Acquisitions & Systems Integration — IT Due Diligence, Technical Carve-Outs, and Day-1 Synergy
* **Module 07:** Culminating Strategic IT Program Portfolio Defense — Executive Portfolio Reallocation Briefing & Defense
