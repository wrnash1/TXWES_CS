## 1. Course Description
Privacy engineering, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, HIPAA, cross-border data sovereignty, intellectual property in software, and AI liability. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 2, 3
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing policy, law, privacy, and technology.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** Global Privacy Jurisdictions & Data Protection Law — Comparative Analysis of GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and State Statutes
* **Module 02:** Privacy by Design & Technical Privacy Engineering — NIST Privacy Framework and Differential Privacy Principles
* **Module 03:** Cross-Border Data Transfers & National Sovereignty — Schrems II, EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and Localization
* **Module 04:** Intellectual Property & Open Source Governance — Patents, Trade Secrets, Copyrights, and Permissive vs. Copyleft
* **Module 05:** Legal Liability & Duty of Care in Cyber Breaches — FTC Enforcement Actions, Negligence Tort, and Class Actions
* **Module 06:** Emerging Tech Regulation: Biometrics, AI, and Quantum — Facial Recognition Bans, Export Controls, and Tech Sanctions
* **Module 07:** Culminating Enterprise Privacy & Legal Compliance Charter — Organizational Data Governance Charter and Legal Defense
