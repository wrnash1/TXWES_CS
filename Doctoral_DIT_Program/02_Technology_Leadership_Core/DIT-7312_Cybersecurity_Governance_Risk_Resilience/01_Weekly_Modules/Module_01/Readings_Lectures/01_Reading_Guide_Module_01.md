# Reading Guide: Module 01 — Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance & Board Accountability
## DIT 7312: Cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Resilience | Texas Wesleyan University
**Professor William Nash, Ph.D.**

---

### Executive Overview
NIST CSF 2.0 Govern Function (GV.OC, GV.RM, GV.SC), SEC 4-day material incident reporting (Form 8-K), risk appetite framing, and board-level fiduciary oversight.

### Key Theoretical & Regulatory Foundations
1. **National Standards Alignment:** Detailed exploration of applicable NIST CSF 2.0 categories and subcategories governing this domain.
2. **Empirical Literature Review:** Examination of primary studies regarding implementation barriers, quantitative cost-benefit models, and organizational resilience.
3. **Architectural Trade-Off Analysis:** Balancing operational agility, capital expenditure, cryptographic overhead, and regulatory scrutiny.

### Required Weekly Readings
* NIST Special Publications & Regulatory Releases relevant to Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance & Board Accountability.
* Peer-reviewed articles from *IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security*, *ACM Digital Threats*, and *MIS Quarterly Executive*.
* Case study documentation on recent Fortune 500 cybersecurity governance failures and successful resilience transformations.

### Guiding Doctoral Questions for Reflection
* How does executive leadership substantiate 'reasonable security' under heightened regulatory enforcement?
* What quantitative metrics best communicate technical risk exposure to a non-technical Board of Directors?
