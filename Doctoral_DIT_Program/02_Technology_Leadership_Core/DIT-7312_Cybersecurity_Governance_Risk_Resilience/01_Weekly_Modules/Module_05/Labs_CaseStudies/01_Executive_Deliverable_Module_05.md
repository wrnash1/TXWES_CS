# Executive Deliverable (M05): Executive Policy: Enterprise Incident Response Plan & Executive Crisis Escalation Framework
## DIT 7312 Applied Doctoral Case Study & Architectural Milestone
**Texas Wesleyan University | Doctor of Information Technology**

---

### Project Overview
Doctoral scholars must synthesize theoretical literature and technical standards into an authoritative, actionable executive artifact suitable for C-suite presentation.

### Deliverable Requirements
1. **Executive Summary (1 Page):** Problem diagnosis, strategic justification, and anticipated ROI/risk reduction.
2. **Technical Framework & Architectural Design (3–4 Pages):** Detailed technical specifications, data flow diagrams, control mappings to NIST CSF 2.0, and implementation phases.
3. **Risk & Cost-Benefit Quantification (1–2 Pages):** Capital expenditure (CapEx) vs. operational expenditure (OpEx), staffing models, and estimated reduction in Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE).
4. **Governance & Compliance Verification Matrix (1 Page):** Cross-mapping against regulatory frameworks (SEC, HIPAA, GDPR, PCI-DSS 4.0).

### Evaluation
Evaluated strictly using the **Texas Wesleyan Doctoral 4-Tier Analytic Rubric** (Problem Framing, Methodological Rigor, Regulatory Mapping, and Executive Synthesis).
