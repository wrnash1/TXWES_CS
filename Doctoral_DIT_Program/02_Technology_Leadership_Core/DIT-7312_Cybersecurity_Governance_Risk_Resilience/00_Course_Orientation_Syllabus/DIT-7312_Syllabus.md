## 1. Course Description
An advanced doctoral seminar examining enterprise cybersecurity governance, strategic risk management, and organizational resilience. Drawing heavily upon the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**, **NIST SP 800-207 (Zero Trust Architecture)**, and **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**, students evaluate the fiduciary, regulatory, and technical obligations of executive technology leadership. The course investigates board-level oversight, SEC material incident disclosures, cyber supply chain risk management (C-SCRM), continuous telemetry, crisis orchestration, and business continuity. Students formulate and defend an applied enterprise cyber resilience strategy designed to withstand systemic adversarial pressure.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) & Course Learning Outcomes (CLO)
* **PLO 2:** Design secure, resilient and ethically responsible IT strategies with measurable implementation criteria.
* **PLO 3:** Evaluate AI, cloud, data and cybersecurity technologies for organizational value, limitations, privacy and risk.

### Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
Upon successful completion of this doctoral course, students will be able to:
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Appraise enterprise cybersecurity governance models against the NIST CSF 2.0 *Govern* function and SEC regulatory mandates.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate a comprehensive Cyber Supply Chain Risk Management (C-SCRM) framework incorporating SBOM validation and vendor risk tiering.
3. **CLO 3 (Create):** Design a Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) migration roadmap adhering to NIST SP 800-207 for complex hybrid enterprise environments.
4. **CLO 4 (Evaluate):** Analyze security operations architectures using MITRE ATT&CK to eliminate telemetry blind spots and optimize adversary dwell time.
5. **CLO 5 (Lead & Defend):** Direct an enterprise incident response and crisis communication strategy balancing legal privilege, regulatory compliance, and operational recovery.
6. **CLO 6 (Formulate):** Construct an enterprise cyber resilience blueprint integrating immutable data protection, chaos engineering, and cyber insurance qualification criteria.
