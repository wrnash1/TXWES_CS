## 1. Course Description
NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), generative AI governance, algorithmic transparency, bias auditing, and organizational AI adoption. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 2, 3
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Govern–Protect / NIST AI RMF Govern–Manage

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing data, ai, and responsible innovation.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** NIST AI RMF 1.0 Foundations & The GOVERN Function — AI Risk Governance, Executive Accountability, and Culture
* **Module 02:** Mapping AI Risks & Organizational Context (MAP Function) — Categorizing Systemic, Societal, and Enterprise AI Risks
* **Module 03:** Measuring AI Trustworthiness & Metrics (MEASURE Function) — Evaluating Accuracy, Explainability, Robustness, and Fairness
* **Module 04:** Managing Algorithmic Risk in Production (MANAGE Function) — Post-Deployment Monitoring, Guardrails, and Incident Response
* **Module 05:** Generative AI & Large Language Model Enterprise Integration — RAG Architectures, Data Leakage, and IP Infringement
* **Module 06:** Global AI Regulation, EU AI Act & Compliance — Risk Tiering, Prohibited AI Practices, and Conformity Audits
* **Module 07:** Culminating Enterprise AI Governance Framework — Comprehensive Organizational AI Adoption Policy & Defense
