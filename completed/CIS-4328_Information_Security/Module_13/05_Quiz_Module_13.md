# Quiz: Module 13 — Risk Management for Security+

## Course: CIS-4328 Information Security

**Certification Alignment:** CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)

---

## Instructions

This quiz contains 20 questions aligned to Security+ SY0-701 exam objectives. Time limit: 30 minutes. Each question is worth 5 points. A score of 75 or higher (15/20) is required to pass.

---

## Questions

**Question 1**

An organization's Asset Value (AV) is $500,000. The Exposure Factor (EF) is 0.40. The Annual Rate of Occurrence (ARO) is 0.25. What is the Annual Loss Expectancy (ALE)?

- A. $200,000
- B. $50,000
- C. $125,000
- D. $125

---

**Question 2**

A security team calculates that a proposed intrusion prevention system (IPS) costs $20,000/year to operate. Without the IPS, the ALE is $120,000. With the IPS, the projected ALE is $30,000. What is the net annual benefit of deploying the IPS?

- A. $90,000
- B. $70,000
- C. $150,000
- D. $110,000

---

**Question 3**

A company decides not to move their customer data into the cloud because the legal liability from a potential cloud breach is unacceptable under their risk appetite. Which risk response strategy does this represent?

- A. Risk mitigation
- B. Risk transference
- C. Risk acceptance
- D. Risk avoidance

---

**Question 4**

An organization purchases cyber liability insurance that will reimburse breach notification costs, forensic investigation expenses, and regulatory fines in the event of a data breach. Which risk response strategy does this represent?

- A. Risk mitigation
- B. Risk transference
- C. Risk acceptance
- D. Risk avoidance

---

**Question 5**

A security team implements patch management, network segmentation, and MFA to reduce the likelihood and impact of a ransomware attack. After implementing these controls, the risk level falls from Critical to Medium. Which risk response strategy does this represent?

- A. Risk avoidance
- B. Risk transference
- C. Risk mitigation
- D. Risk acceptance

---

**Question 6**

After applying all feasible controls to a known risk, the security team documents the remaining risk level as "Low" and the CISO formally acknowledges this in writing. No further treatment is planned. Which risk response strategy does this represent?

- A. Risk avoidance
- B. Risk transference
- C. Risk mitigation
- D. Risk acceptance

---

**Question 7**

Which of the following BEST describes the difference between inherent risk and residual risk?

- A. Inherent risk applies to high-impact events; residual risk applies to low-impact events
- B. Inherent risk is the risk before any controls are applied; residual risk is the risk remaining after controls are implemented
- C. Inherent risk is managed by technical controls; residual risk is managed by administrative controls
- D. Inherent risk is calculated quantitatively; residual risk is always assessed qualitatively

---

**Question 8**

An organization's risk appetite statement reads: "We will not accept any risk that could result in loss of patient data." Despite this, the CISO formally documents and accepts a known risk of a data breach via a legacy system that cannot be immediately decommissioned. What is this documented decision an example of?

- A. Risk transference
- B. Risk avoidance
- C. Risk acceptance (exception to stated risk appetite with documented rationale)
- D. Residual risk elimination

---

**Question 9**

A hospital determines that their EHR system cannot be offline for more than 8 hours before patient safety is compromised. Their disaster recovery plan targets a 10-hour recovery time. Which BIA concept does this gap violate?

- A. The RPO exceeds the MTD
- B. The RTO exceeds the MTD
- C. The MTBF is too low
- D. The ALE has not been calculated

---

**Question 10**

A company performs daily database backups. A ransomware attack encrypts the production database at 3:00 PM on a Tuesday. The database is restored from the Monday night backup. How much data was lost?

- A. 24 hours
- B. Approximately 15 hours (from Monday night backup to 3 PM Tuesday)
- C. Zero — all data was in the backup
- D. One week

---

**Question 11**

An organization uses a risk matrix that plots likelihood against impact on a 3×3 grid. A risk is assessed as High Likelihood and High Impact. Which risk rating cell would this risk fall into?

- A. Low
- B. Medium
- C. High
- D. Critical

---

**Question 12**

A firewall that blocks unauthorized inbound network traffic is BEST classified as which type of security control?

- A. Physical preventive
- B. Technical detective
- C. Technical preventive
- D. Administrative preventive

---

**Question 13**

A SIEM system monitors security logs and sends an alert when it detects multiple failed login attempts from the same IP address within a short time window. Which type of security control is this?

- A. Technical preventive
- B. Technical detective
- C. Administrative corrective
- D. Physical deterrent

---

**Question 14**

An organization cannot patch a legacy industrial control system because the patch requires an 8-hour maintenance window that production operations cannot support. They implement continuous network traffic monitoring specifically for that system. The monitoring system is an example of which type of control?

- A. Preventive
- B. Directive
- C. Compensating
- D. Deterrent

---

**Question 15**

An organization's security policy requires all employees to complete annual cybersecurity training. This policy is an example of which type of security control?

- A. Technical preventive
- B. Physical detective
- C. Administrative directive
- D. Technical compensating

---

**Question 16**

Which of the following is the BEST definition of the term "risk owner" in a risk register?

- A. The security analyst who identified the risk
- B. The CISO, who is responsible for all organizational security risks
- C. The individual responsible for managing the risk, typically the owner of the affected asset or business process
- D. The IT manager who implements the risk mitigation controls

---

**Question 17**

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework organizes security activities into five core functions. An organization is implementing network monitoring, user behavior analytics, and intrusion detection systems. Which CSF function do these activities primarily support?

- A. Identify
- B. Protect
- C. Detect
- D. Respond

---

**Question 18**

An organization's annual security risk assessment produces a long list of identified risks. The security team wants to perform detailed quantitative analysis on the highest-priority risks and use a faster, less resource-intensive method for low-priority risks. Which approach does this describe?

- A. Using qualitative analysis for all risks first to identify priority risks, then applying quantitative analysis to the highest-rated risks
- B. Applying quantitative analysis to all risks and then qualitative analysis to validate results
- C. Performing only qualitative analysis because quantitative analysis is not applicable to cybersecurity
- D. Delegating all risk analysis to external consultants

---

**Question 19**

A company's RPO for their customer database is 4 hours. The current backup strategy creates daily backups at midnight. A database failure occurs at 6:00 PM. How much data could potentially be lost and does this meet the RPO?

- A. 18 hours of data lost — does not meet the RPO
- B. 4 hours of data lost — meets the RPO
- C. No data lost — backups cover all scenarios
- D. 24 hours of data lost — does not meet the RPO

---

**Question 20**

An organization performs a Business Impact Analysis and determines that the payroll system has an MTD of 3 business days. The current disaster recovery plan provides an RTO of 2 business days. Which statement is TRUE?

- A. The RTO exceeds the MTD, creating a gap in the recovery plan
- B. The RTO is within the MTD, which is acceptable
- C. The MTD should always equal the RTO
- D. The RTO is irrelevant because the MTD covers the required recovery time
