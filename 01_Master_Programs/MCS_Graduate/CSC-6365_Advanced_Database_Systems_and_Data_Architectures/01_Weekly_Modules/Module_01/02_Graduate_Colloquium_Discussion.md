# Module 01 Graduate Colloquium: Database Engine Internals & Storage Hierarchy
**Course:** CSC-6365 | Texas Wesleyan University

### Colloquium Prompt
Critically analyze the trade-offs inherent in B+ Trees, LSM-Trees (Log-Structured Merge), Page Buffering, and Storage Engine Optimization. Consider an enterprise operating under strict latency SLA and five-nines (99.999%) availability mandates.
1. Formulate a technical design proposal defending your chosen architectural pattern.
2. Evaluate potential edge cases and failure domains.
3. Critique the proposals of at least two peers using empirical performance arguments.
