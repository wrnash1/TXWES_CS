## Background: CCNP ENCOR as a Career Milestone

The CCNP Enterprise certification is one of the most recognized professional credentials in network engineering. The ENCOR 350-401 exam — the core exam required for any CCNP Enterprise track — validates that a network engineer can design, implement, and troubleshoot enterprise networks spanning routing, switching, security, SD-WAN, network programmability, and automation. Industry salary surveys consistently show a 15–25% premium for CCNP-certified engineers compared to CCNA-level peers.

CSC-6361 Advanced Computer Networks was designed around the CCNP ENCOR blueprint deliberately. Every module maps to one or more ENCOR exam domains. The seven modules together cover approximately 80% of the exam content — the remaining 20% is network automation and assurance (Domains 4 and 6), which require additional self-study.

This final discussion is your opportunity to synthesize what you have learned, connect it to your professional trajectory, and commit to the certification path. Choose one of the three scenarios below.
