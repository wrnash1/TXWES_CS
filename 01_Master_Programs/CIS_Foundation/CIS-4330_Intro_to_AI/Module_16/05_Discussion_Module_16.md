### Responsible AI Principles — One-Line Definitions

- **Fairness:** Equitable outcomes across all groups
- **Reliability and Safety:** Performs as expected; fails safely
- **Privacy and Security:** Protects personal data; resists attacks
- **Inclusiveness:** Works for all people regardless of ability or background
- **Transparency:** Understandable to those who use and are affected by it
- **Accountability:** Humans are responsible for AI systems and impacts

### ML Types — One-Line Definitions

- **Supervised learning:** Trains on labeled data; learns input → output mapping
- **Unsupervised learning:** Finds patterns in unlabeled data (clustering)
- **Reinforcement learning:** Learns through reward/penalty signals
- **Regression:** Predicts a continuous numerical value
- **Classification:** Predicts a discrete category label
- **Clustering:** Groups similar items without predefined categories
