## Scenario A — The Smearing Laser Printer

You are the IT technician at a 40-person law firm. The office's primary laser printer has been producing pages where the toner smears when touched. The attorneys are complaining that documents are arriving at court with smudged text. You arrive on-site and confirm the symptom: fresh output from the printer smears easily when rubbed. The toner cartridge was replaced two weeks ago and print volume has been normal.

Address all three of the following in your post:

1. Identify which EP process step is responsible for this symptom and explain the physical reason that step produces smearing when it fails. Name the specific component that has most likely failed.
2. Describe the diagnostic steps you would perform to confirm your theory before ordering a replacement part, and explain what you would look for during each step.
3. The senior partner asks why the printer worked fine immediately after the last toner cartridge replacement but is now smearing two weeks later. Explain the relationship between print volume, component wear, and the failure you diagnosed — in terms the partner can understand without technical jargon.

## Scenario B — The Blank Receipt Mystery

You are called to a busy restaurant to fix their POS receipt printer. The cashiers report the printer has been producing completely blank receipts for the last hour. The printer is powered on, connected, and shows no error codes on its display. The cashiers have already replaced the paper roll once, but the new roll also produces blank output.

Address all three of the following in your post:

1. Identify the most likely cause of blank receipts on a direct thermal receipt printer, explain why this specific cause produces blank output rather than an error or partial output, and describe the exact fix a technician would perform.
2. The restaurant manager asks what "direct thermal" means and why their receipt printer does not need ink. Provide a clear, accurate explanation of how direct thermal printing technology works that a non-technical person could understand.
3. The manager then asks if they can use regular copy paper in the receipt printer to save money since "paper is paper." Explain why this would not work and what would happen if they tried, referencing the specific property of thermal paper that makes it functional.

## Scenario C — The 3D Print Failure Analysis

Your department has purchased a 3D FDM printer for producing custom enclosures and mounts for IT equipment. The printer arrived and the first test print — a small ABS plastic bracket — failed partway through: the corners lifted off the build plate and the print detached from the bed entirely. The printer's build plate is not heated. A second attempt with PLA filament on the same printer with the same settings succeeded without issue.

Address all three of the following in your post:

1. Explain specifically why the ABS print warped and detached while the PLA print succeeded under the same conditions. Reference the material properties that make ABS more susceptible to warping than PLA.
2. Describe three specific changes to the printer setup or slicer settings that would likely prevent the ABS warping problem, and explain the technical reason each change helps.
3. The department manager suggests simply switching all future prints to PLA since "it works." Identify one application scenario where ABS would be the better material choice over PLA, and explain which material property makes ABS more suitable for that scenario.

## A Note from Professor Nash

Printers generate more help desk tickets per device than almost anything else in an office environment. Every week, somewhere, a technician is called to a printer that smears, fades, jams, or produces blank output — and a user is standing there frustrated because their document needs to be in someone's hands right now. The scenarios in this discussion are versions of things that happen in real offices constantly. When you can walk up to a smearing laser printer and say with confidence "that is the fuser" — and be right — you have earned that technician title. Use this discussion to test whether you can get there. See you in the replies.
