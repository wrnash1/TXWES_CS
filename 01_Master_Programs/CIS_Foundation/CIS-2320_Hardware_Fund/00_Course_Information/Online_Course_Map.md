# Online Course Map: CIS-2320_Hardware_Fund
**Target Certification:** CompTIA A+

## 16-Week Breakdown
*   **Module 01:** Introduction to PC Hardware and Safety (ESD, grounding, component overview)
*   **Module 02:** Motherboards and Expansion Slots (form factors, chipsets, PCIe)
*   **Module 03:** Processors (CPUs) and Cooling (sockets, cores/threads, thermal paste, heat sinks)
*   **Module 04:** Memory (RAM) (DDR generations, DIMM vs SODIMM, dual-channel)
*   **Module 05:** Storage Devices (HDD, SATA SSD, M.2 NVMe, RAID 0/1/5/10)
*   **Module 06:** Power Supplies and System Cooling (PSU wattage, 80 Plus, airflow)
*   **Module 07:** Display Technologies and Connectors (LCD/OLED panels, HDMI, DisplayPort, DVI, resolution)
*   **Module 08:** Custom PC Configurations (CAD workstations, virtualization hosts, gaming PCs, NAS)
*   **Module 09:** Peripheral Devices and Interfaces (USB standards, Thunderbolt, KVM, biometrics)
*   **Module 10:** Troubleshooting Boot Issues (POST, beep codes, BSOD, UEFI boot order)
*   **Module 11:** Network Hardware and Connectors (cable categories, RJ-45/RJ-11, fiber connectors, T568A/B)
*   **Module 12:** Network Infrastructure Devices (switches, routers, WAPs, firewalls, PoE)
*   **Module 13:** Laptop Components and Disassembly (keyboard, battery, Wi-Fi card, LCD, power jack)
*   **Module 14:** Mobile Device Connectivity (Bluetooth, LTE/5G, Wi-Fi profiles, email ports)
*   **Module 15:** Printers and Imaging (laser EP process, inkjet maintenance, thermal, 3D printing)
*   **Module 16:** Final Exam Preparation (course-wide review, A+ troubleshooting methodology, practical assessment)
