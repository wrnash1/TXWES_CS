# 🏛️ Texas Wesleyan Academic Resources & Student Support Hub

Texas Wesleyan University provides comprehensive academic, technical, and personal support services to ensure your success:

* **Academic Success Center / Tutoring:** Free peer and professional tutoring in the West Library (817-531-4234).
* **West Library Online Databases:** 24/7 access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and ProQuest at `txwes.edu/library`.
* **IT Help Desk:** Contact for Canvas login or txwes email issues at `helpdesk@txwes.edu` or 817-531-4428.
* **Office of Disability Services / Student Access:** ADA accommodations support located in the West Library.
* **Title IX Coordinator:** For confidential reporting and support regarding discrimination or harassment.
