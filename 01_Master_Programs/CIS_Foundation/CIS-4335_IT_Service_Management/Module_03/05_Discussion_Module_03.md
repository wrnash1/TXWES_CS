# Discussion Forum: Module 03 — The Four Dimensions of Service Management

**Course:** CIS-4335 IT Service Management — Texas Wesleyan University
**Instructor:** Professor Nash
**Total Points:** 10

---

## Overview

This discussion asks you to apply the Four Dimensions of Service Management to realistic IT situations. Choose one scenario from the three options below. Write an initial post analyzing that scenario through the lens of the four dimensions, then reply constructively to at least two classmates.

Use the specific names of the four dimensions correctly. Generic responses that describe "people problems" or "technology problems" without using ITIL 4 dimension names will not earn full credit.

---

## Scenario A: The University IT Consolidation

A regional university is consolidating IT services across three campuses into a single centralized IT department. Each campus previously had its own IT team with its own tools, help desk procedures, ticketing systems, and vendor contracts. The central IT director has been given 12 months to create a unified service management environment.

The consolidation reveals: three incompatible ticketing systems with no data migration path; 14 different vendor contracts for the same categories of services, some redundant and some conflicting; no common understanding of what services are offered or what response time standards apply; and significant cultural tension between staff from the three campuses, who distrust each other's ways of working.

Respond to all three of the following prompts:

1. Identify which of the four dimensions is most severely stressed by this consolidation scenario. Explain your reasoning using specific evidence from the scenario.
2. Identify one action the IT director should take in each of the four dimensions during the first 90 days. These actions should be practical and specific, not generic advice.
3. Identify one PESTLE factor that could complicate the consolidation and explain how the IT director should account for it.

## Scenario B: The Manufacturer's New Smart Factory System

A manufacturing company is deploying an Industrial IoT (IIoT) platform to monitor equipment health in real time across 12 production lines. The system will use sensor data to predict equipment failures before they occur (predictive maintenance). The IT and OT (operational technology) teams have never collaborated before. The IIoT platform vendor is a startup with limited enterprise support history. Factory floor workers have never interacted with IT-managed systems as part of their daily work.

Respond to all three of the following prompts:

1. For each of the four dimensions, identify one specific challenge this deployment faces and explain why it belongs to that dimension.
2. The IT and OT teams have never collaborated. Which dimension does this challenge primarily belong to, and what does ITIL 4 say about how to address it? Reference a specific Guiding Principle that applies.
3. The IIoT vendor is a startup with limited enterprise support history. Which dimension does this concern primarily belong to? What two actions should the organization take before signing the vendor contract to address this risk?

## Scenario C: The Remote Work Transition

A mid-size accounting firm rapidly transitioned its 800 employees to full remote work. Eighteen months later, an internal survey reveals significant issues: staff are using a mix of personal and corporate devices with inconsistent security configurations; three different video conferencing tools are in use across departments with no standard; the helpdesk is overwhelmed because staff do not know which tool to use for which problem; and two key SaaS vendors have notified the firm of pending price increases that will exceed the IT budget.

Respond to all three of the following prompts:

1. For each of the four dimensions, identify the specific failure described in the scenario that belongs to that dimension. There is at least one for each dimension.
2. Which PESTLE factor most directly explains the pressure the firm now faces from the SaaS vendor price increases? Explain your reasoning.
3. If you were the IT director and could only address two of the four dimension failures immediately due to resource constraints, which two would you prioritize and why? Use ITIL 4 reasoning to justify your prioritization.

## A Note from Professor Nash

The four dimensions are most useful when you treat them as a diagnostic lens — a way of systematically asking "what have we not thought about yet?" Every major IT service failure I have encountered in practice had a root cause traceable to at least one dimension that was overlooked. The goal of this discussion is not to memorize four labels, but to build the habit of thinking through all four before a service goes live. That habit will serve you throughout your career.
