Welcome

Welcome to CIS-4328, Fundamentals of Information Systems Security! This course covers the full scope of the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) certification exam — threats and vulnerabilities, cryptography, identity and access management, network and cloud security, incident response, digital forensics, risk management, and governance/compliance.

This is a Hybrid course: we meet in person weekly on Wednesdays, 6:00 PM – 7:15 PM, in Armstrong Mabee Business Cntr, Room 214, and complete the remaining coursework asynchronously in Canvas each week.

How This Course Is Organized

The course is divided into 16 modules, one per week. Every module (except Module 16, which is exam preparation and capstone week) follows the same pattern:

  1. Video Lecture — short recorded lectures introducing the week's concepts. (Watch these before the in-person class meeting.)
  2. Reading Guide — a detailed study document with vocabulary, comparison tables, and exam tips. Use this as your primary study reference.
  3. In-Person Class Meeting — we apply and discuss the week's concepts together, work through examples, and answer questions.
  4. Lab Activity — a hands-on exercise (often using a Kali Linux VM and free security tools) applying the week's concepts.
  5. Discussion Board — a weekly discussion connecting the material to real breaches and organizational decisions.
  6. Quiz — a short quiz checking your understanding of the week's material.

Weekly Workflow and Due Dates

Unless otherwise noted, all weekly Labs, Discussions, and Quizzes are due Sunday at 11:59 PM Central Time. Check the Modules page and Syllabus for the specific schedule, and see the Late Work Policy in the Syllabus if you need it.
