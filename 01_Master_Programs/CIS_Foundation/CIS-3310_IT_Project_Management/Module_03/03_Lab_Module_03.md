## Lab Overview

In this lab you will apply Module 03 scope management concepts by completing three connected activities: writing a Project Scope Statement, building a Work Breakdown Structure, and creating a WBS Dictionary entry. All deliverables are written documents — no software installation or terminal commands are required.

Submit all deliverables as a single PDF or Word document to the Canvas assignment portal.

---

## Scenario Background

Crestview Community College (a fictional institution) is implementing a new Learning Management System (LMS) to replace their outdated platform. The new LMS must support online course delivery, gradebook management, student discussion boards, video content hosting, and integration with the college's student information system. The project must be completed within nine months. The approved budget is $195,000. The VP of Academic Affairs is the project sponsor. You are the project manager.

The following items are explicitly out of scope: building a custom mobile app, migrating more than five years of historical course data, and providing 24/7 vendor support contracts (those will be negotiated separately after go-live).

## Part 2: Work Breakdown Structure

### Part 2 Objective

Build a complete, properly structured WBS for the Crestview Community College LMS implementation using the 100% Rule and deliverable-oriented organization.

### Part 2 Instructions

Create a WBS with at least three levels. Use indented text or a numbered outline format. Level 1 is the project. Level 2 should include at least five major deliverable categories. Level 3 should break each Level 2 category into at least three sub-deliverables or work packages. All WBS entries must be noun phrases (deliverables), not verbs (tasks).

After completing the WBS outline, answer the following two questions in 2–3 sentences each:

Question A: Does your WBS satisfy the 100% Rule? Identify one deliverable that would be missing if you had not explicitly checked — or confirm that all deliverables from your Scope Statement appear in the WBS.

Question B: Identify one item that might tempt a team member to add as gold plating to this LMS project. Explain why it should be excluded and how you would handle a team member who added it anyway.

**Part 2 Point Value:** 40 points

Grading breakdown:

- WBS structure (15 pts): Correct hierarchy; all entries are noun phrases; at least 5 Level 2 categories; at least 3 work packages per category
- 100% Rule compliance (15 pts): All deliverables from Scope Statement appear in WBS; no out-of-scope items included
- Question A response (5 pts): Demonstrates understanding of 100% Rule
- Question B response (5 pts): Correctly identifies and explains gold plating with appropriate PM response

## Part 9 — Challenge Exercise

This section is optional for students seeking additional depth and exam preparation. It is not graded as part of the standard 100-point lab but may be used for extra credit at the instructor's discretion.

### Challenge Step 1: Requirements Traceability Matrix

Select three requirements from the Scope Statement you wrote in Part 1. Build a simple Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) with the following columns: Requirement ID, Requirement Description, Source (who requested it), WBS Element that delivers it, and Test/Acceptance Criterion that validates it. Explain in two sentences why the RTM is important for audit and quality assurance purposes on an IT project.

### Challenge Step 2: Scope Change Impact Analysis

A late-arriving stakeholder requests that the EduConnect LMS deployment include integration with an external plagiarism detection service (not in the current scope). Using the Scope Statement and WBS you built in Parts 1 and 2, write a brief impact analysis (4–6 sentences) identifying: which WBS elements would be affected, the estimated schedule impact, the estimated cost impact, and your recommendation to the Change Control Board (approve, defer, or reject with rationale).

### Challenge Step 3: WBS 100% Rule Audit

Exchange your Part 2 WBS outline with a peer (or self-audit). Review each Level 2 branch and verify that the sum of all Level 3 work packages completely and exclusively covers the parent Level 2 element — no overlap, no gaps. Document any violations you find and describe how you would correct them. This audit mirrors the quality review a PMO would conduct before baselining the WBS.
