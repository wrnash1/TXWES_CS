# Online Course Map
## CIS-4315: Cyber Governance, Risk & Compliance
### 16-Week Schedule with CISM Domain Alignment

---

## How to Use This Map

This document maps all 16 weeks of CIS-4315 to the four ISACA CISM domains, primary free readings, and weekly deliverables. Use it to:
- Plan your study schedule across the 16-week semester
- Understand which CISM domain each module strengthens
- Identify which modules are most heavily weighted in the CISM exam
- Track your progress toward CISM exam readiness

**CISM Domain Exam Weightings:**
- Domain 1 — Security Governance: ~17%
- Domain 2 — Information Risk Management: ~20%
- Domain 3 — Security Program Development and Management: ~33%
- Domain 4 — Incident Management: ~30%

---

## 16-Week Course Schedule

---

### Week 1 — Module 01: Information Security Governance Foundations
**CISM Domain:** Domain 1 — Security Governance
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — establishes all governance fundamentals

**Learning Objectives:**
- Define information security governance and distinguish it from security management
- Explain how governance structures (steering committees, boards, CISO) align security strategy with business objectives
- Describe the CIA triad and its relationship to governance decisions

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-39: Managing Information Security Risk](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-39/final) — Sections 2–3

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 01 Quiz
- Module 01 Lab: Security program alignment to corporate strategy

**CISM Exam Note:** Domain 1 questions expect you to think like a security executive advising the board, not a technical engineer.

---

### Week 2 — Module 02: Security Strategy and Business Alignment
**CISM Domain:** Domain 1 — Security Governance
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — policy framework is a core governance deliverable

**Learning Objectives:**
- Define and distinguish policies, standards, guidelines, and procedures
- Explain the policy life cycle and its management requirements
- Identify when to use each policy tier in practice

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-12 Rev. 1: Introduction to Information Security](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-12/rev-1/final) — Chapters 2 and 9

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 02 Quiz
- Module 02 Lab: Acceptable use policy draft and policy hierarchy exercise
- Discussion Board Post (if assigned this week)

**CISM Exam Note:** The exam frequently tests the policy-standard-guideline-procedure hierarchy. Know what is mandatory vs. recommended at each tier.

---

### Week 3 — Module 03: Risk Management Frameworks (NIST RMF, ISO 27005)
**CISM Domain:** Domain 2 — Information Risk Management
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — RMF is the structural backbone of risk management

**Learning Objectives:**
- Describe the seven steps of NIST SP 800-37 RMF in sequence
- Explain the purpose of system categorization using FIPS 199
- Distinguish between NIST RMF and ISO 27005 approaches

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2: Risk Management Framework](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-37/rev-2/final) — Chapter 2, Table 1

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 03 Quiz
- Module 03 Lab: RMF step mapping and FIPS 199 categorization exercise

**CISM Exam Note:** Know the RMF steps in order. The exam frequently tests which step comes next in a given scenario.

---

### Week 4 — Module 04: Risk Assessment and Treatment
**CISM Domain:** Domain 2 — Information Risk Management
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — asset classification and valuation underpin all risk decisions

**Learning Objectives:**
- Define information assets and explain asset inventory requirements
- Apply classification tiers (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) using impact criteria
- Calculate and apply asset valuation metrics to prioritize protection

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-60 Vol. 1: Guide for Mapping Information Types to Security Categories](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-60/vol-1-rev-1/final) — Section 2

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 04 Quiz
- Module 04 Lab: Asset inventory schema and classification exercise

**CISM Exam Note:** The exam tests asset ownership accountability. Remember: business unit managers own assets; IT is the custodian.

---

### Week 5 — Module 05: Compliance — HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOX, GDPR
**CISM Domain:** Domain 2 — Information Risk Management
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — quantitative risk analysis is frequently tested

**Learning Objectives:**
- Calculate SLE and ALE from given asset values, exposure factors, and ARO values
- Distinguish qualitative from quantitative risk assessment methods
- Define threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact and explain how they combine to produce risk

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1: Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-30/rev-1/final) — Chapter 3

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 05 Quiz
- Module 05 Lab: SLE/ALE calculation and qualitative risk matrix exercise
- Discussion Board Post (if assigned this week)

**CISM Exam Note:** Memorize: SLE = AV × EF; ALE = SLE × ARO. The exam may test these calculations or ask you to interpret results.

---

### Week 6 — Module 06: Information Security Policies and Standards
**CISM Domain:** Domain 2 — Information Risk Management
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — risk treatment selection is core Domain 2 content

**Learning Objectives:**
- Distinguish the four risk treatment options: mitigate, avoid, accept, transfer
- Select the appropriate treatment option for scenarios based on risk level and cost
- Explain residual risk and the accountability requirements for risk acceptance

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-39: Managing Information Security Risk](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-39/final) — Section 3.4 (Risk Response)

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 06 Quiz
- Module 06 Lab: Risk treatment plan drafting and risk register entry

**CISM Exam Note:** Risk acceptance must be formally documented and signed by management with appropriate authority — it cannot be delegated to IT staff.

---

### Week 7 — Module 07: Security Program Development and Management
**CISM Domain:** Domains 2 and 3 — Risk Management / Program Development
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — BIA bridges risk management and continuity planning

**Learning Objectives:**
- Define Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and explain its role in security program development
- Distinguish RTO, RPO, and MTD and explain the relationship between them
- Identify who leads BIA and why business units, not IT, drive the analysis

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1: Contingency Planning Guide](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-34/rev-1/final) — Section 3.2

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 07 Quiz
- Module 07 Lab: BIA questionnaire design and RTO/RPO derivation exercise
- Discussion Board Post (if assigned this week)

**CISM Exam Note:** RTO = downtime tolerance; RPO = data loss tolerance; MTD = survival limit. Recovery must meet RTO, not just survive within MTD.

---

### Week 8 — Module 08: Security Architecture and Design
**CISM Domain:** Domains 3 and 4 — Program Development / Incident Management
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — BCP/DRP spans Domains 3 and 4

**Learning Objectives:**
- Distinguish BCP (broad business continuity) from DRP (IT systems recovery)
- Evaluate hot, warm, and cold recovery site options against RTO requirements
- Describe DRP testing methods from tabletop through full interruption

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1: Contingency Planning Guide](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-34/rev-1/final) — Sections 3.5 and 3.6

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 08 Quiz
- Module 08 Lab: Tabletop exercise design and recovery site comparison

**CISM Exam Note:** An untested DRP is a governance failure. The exam emphasizes that plans must be tested regularly; surviving an actual disaster without a test is luck, not governance.

---

### Midterm Assessment Window — End of Week 9
**Coverage:** Modules 01–08 (all CISM Domains 1–2, and Domains 3–4 foundations)
**Format:** 30 scenario-based multiple choice questions
**Time Limit:** 60 minutes
**Availability:** Opens Monday, closes Sunday of Week 9

---

### Week 9 — Module 09: Incident Management – Preparation and Detection
**CISM Domain:** Domain 1 — Security Governance (Compliance)
**Domain Weight Contribution:** Moderate-High — regulatory compliance is a governance obligation

**Learning Objectives:**
- Identify the security requirements imposed by HIPAA, SOX, and GLBA
- Classify HIPAA Security Rule safeguards into Administrative, Physical, and Technical categories
- Determine which regulatory framework applies to given organizational scenarios

**Required Reading:**
- [HHS HIPAA Security Rule Guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/index.html)
- [NIST SP 800-66 Rev. 2: Implementing the HIPAA Security Rule](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-66/rev-2/final)

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 09 Quiz
- Module 09 Lab: HIPAA control mapping and SOX IT general controls exercise
- Discussion Board Post (if assigned this week)

**CISM Exam Note:** Know that HIPAA breach notification to individuals is within 60 calendar days of discovery. SOX Section 404 focuses on IT General Controls protecting financial reporting systems.

---

### Week 10 — Module 10: Incident Response – Containment, Eradication, Recovery
**CISM Domain:** Domain 1 — Security Governance (Privacy Compliance)
**Domain Weight Contribution:** Moderate — privacy regulations create governance obligations

**Learning Objectives:**
- Explain GDPR's scope, individual rights, and breach notification requirements
- Distinguish CCPA protections and their applicability to California residents' data
- Define PII and explain the "right to be forgotten" (right to erasure) under GDPR

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST Privacy Framework v1.0](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) — Core structure overview

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 10 Quiz
- Module 10 Lab: PII data flow mapping and GDPR erasure workflow exercise

**CISM Exam Note:** GDPR notification to supervisory authorities is within 72 hours of discovery — much faster than HIPAA's 60-day individual notification. The exam tests this distinction.

---

### Week 11 — Module 11: Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Planning
**CISM Domain:** Domain 1 — Security Governance (Industry Standards)
**Domain Weight Contribution:** Moderate-High — PCI DSS and ISO 27001 are commonly tested

**Learning Objectives:**
- List PCI DSS's six goals and 12 requirements at a conceptual level
- Distinguish mandatory PCI DSS compliance from voluntary ISO/IEC 27001 certification
- Explain ISMS scope and its implications for ISO 27001 certification

**Required Reading:**
- [PCI DSS v4.0 At a Glance](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/)
- [ISO/IEC 27001 Overview](https://www.iso.org/isoiec-27001-information-security.html)

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 11 Quiz
- Module 11 Lab: PCI DSS requirements mapping and ISO 27001 Annex A gap analysis
- Discussion Board Post (if assigned this week)

**CISM Exam Note:** PCI DSS is contractually mandatory for card data handlers; ISO 27001 is voluntary. Know when each applies.

---

### Week 12 — Module 12: Third-Party Risk and Vendor Management
**CISM Domain:** Domain 2 — Information Risk Management (Audit and Assurance)
**Domain Weight Contribution:** Moderate-High — audit assurance spans governance and risk management

**Learning Objectives:**
- Distinguish design effectiveness from operating effectiveness in control testing
- Explain the importance of auditor independence and why IT staff cannot audit their own controls
- Develop a corrective action plan in response to an audit finding

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5: Assessing Security and Privacy Controls](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53a/rev-5/final) — Chapter 2

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 12 Quiz
- Module 12 Lab: Audit evidence request list and finding memo

**CISM Exam Note:** The correct response to an audit finding is a documented remediation plan, not dispute or delay.

---

### Week 13 — Module 13: Data Classification and Privacy Management
**CISM Domain:** Domain 2 — Information Risk Management (Third-Party Risk)
**Domain Weight Contribution:** Moderate-High — vendor risk is a growing exam topic

**Learning Objectives:**
- Explain how vendor risk fits within the organization's overall risk management program
- Distinguish SOC 2 Type I from SOC 2 Type II and know when each is sufficient
- Design a risk-tiered vendor management program

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1: Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-161/rev-1/final) — Section 2

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 13 Quiz
- Module 13 Lab: SOC 2 report review and vendor SLA security requirements
- Discussion Board Post (if assigned this week)

**CISM Exam Note:** Organizations cannot outsource accountability for data security. When a vendor breaches your data, you remain responsible to regulators and customers.

---

### Week 14 — Module 14: Audit and Assurance
**CISM Domain:** Domain 3 — Security Program Development and Management
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — security awareness is a core program management responsibility

**Learning Objectives:**
- Design an effective phishing simulation and awareness training campaign
- Measure security awareness program effectiveness using behavioral outcome metrics
- Apply role-based training principles to different employee segments

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-50: Building an IT Security Awareness and Training Program](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-50/final) — Sections 3–4

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 14 Quiz
- Module 14 Lab: Phishing simulation campaign design and role-based training curriculum

**CISM Exam Note:** Effectiveness metrics = behavioral change (click rates, incident rates), not input metrics (hours of training produced, satisfaction scores).

---

### Week 15 — Module 15: Security Metrics and KPIs
**CISM Domain:** Domain 4 — Incident Management
**Domain Weight Contribution:** High — incident governance is a major Domain 4 component

**Learning Objectives:**
- Design pre-defined escalation pathways for incidents across severity levels
- Map regulatory notification obligations (GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA) to incident timelines
- Conduct a post-incident review and translate findings into security program improvements

**Required Reading:**
- [NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2: Computer Security Incident Handling Guide](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-61/rev-2/final) — Chapter 3, Section 3.4

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 15 Quiz
- Module 15 Lab: Escalation flowchart, regulatory notification timeline, and post-incident review template
- Discussion Board Post (if assigned this week)

**CISM Exam Note:** Post-incident reviews are mandatory governance requirements, not optional activities. Every significant incident must generate documented lessons learned.

---

### Week 16 — Module 16: Final Exam Prep & ISACA CISM Certification
**CISM Domain:** All Four Domains — Integration and Review
**Domain Weight Contribution:** Comprehensive coverage across all domains

**Learning Objectives:**
- Demonstrate integrated understanding of all four CISM domains through scenario analysis
- Identify personal knowledge gaps and develop a targeted study plan for the CISM exam
- Explain CISM exam logistics, work experience requirements, and certification maintenance

**Required Reading:**
- Review all module glossaries (Modules 01–15)
- [ISACA CISM Exam Candidate Guide](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism)

**Deliverables Due:**
- Module 16 Quiz
- Module 16 Capstone Lab: Scenario set, personal CISM study plan, and executive improvement memo
- Course Final Exam (opens Week 16 Monday, closes Sunday — 50 questions, 90-minute time limit)

**CISM Exam Note:** When two answers seem correct, choose the management perspective. CISM tests judgment, not memorization.
