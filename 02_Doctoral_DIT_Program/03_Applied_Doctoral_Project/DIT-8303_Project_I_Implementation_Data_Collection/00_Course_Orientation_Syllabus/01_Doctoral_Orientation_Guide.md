# DIT-8303: Doctoral Orientation & Delivery Guide
## Texas Wesleyan University | Doctor of Information Technology

### Course Expectations
* **Doctoral Rigor:** Analysis must reflect Bloom's Revised Taxonomy Levels 5–6 (Evaluate & Create).
* **Evidence Base:** All proposals must cite national frameworks (NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure–Manage) and peer-reviewed literature.
* **Deadlines:**
  * Thursday 11:59 PM CT: Initial Colloquium Post (minimum 500 words).
  * Sunday 11:59 PM CT: Peer Critiques (2 minimum), Weekly Deliverable, and Assessment.
