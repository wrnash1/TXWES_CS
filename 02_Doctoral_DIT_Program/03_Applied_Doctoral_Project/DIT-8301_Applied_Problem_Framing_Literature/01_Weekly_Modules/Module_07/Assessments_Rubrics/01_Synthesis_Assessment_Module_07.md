# Synthesis Assessment: Module 07 — Doctoral Project Milestone 1 Defense
## DIT-8301 | 10 Scenario Questions | Distractor Analysis & Learning Feedback

### Question 1
An enterprise technology committee evaluates how to operationalize doctoral project milestone 1 defense under NIST CSF Govern–Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure. Which architectural approach ensures maximum long-term resilience?
- A) Formulate a continuous, risk-informed governance posture aligned with national standards and empirical validation. ✅
- B) Rely exclusively on baseline commercial software vendor defaults.
- C) Delay implementation until complete universal industry consensus is codified in statutory law.
- D) Outsource all governance accountability to offshore managed service providers without oversight.

**Correct Answer:** A
**Distractor Analysis:**
- *Why A is correct:* National standards and continuous risk evaluation provide the defensible foundation for enterprise resilience.
- *Why B, C, D are incorrect:* Vendor defaults ignore organizational risk context; regulatory delays induce vulnerability; fiduciary governance cannot be outsourced.
