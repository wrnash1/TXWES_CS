## DIT-7331 | SACSCOC Regular & Substantive Interaction

### Executive Prompt
Assume the role of Chief Information Officer (CIO) or Principal Enterprise Technology Lead. Critically analyze:
> **Organizational Dilemma:** An enterprise must modernize its operational capabilities in legal liability & duty of care in cyber breaches while navigating regulatory mandates, budget limits, and risk constraints.
