## 1. Course Description
Advanced evidence appraisal, doctoral research questions, theoretical framing, validity, and translating empirical research into executive technology decisions. Grounded in national standards (**NIST CSF Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure**), this doctoral seminar prepares senior technology executives, enterprise architects, and applied researchers to lead complex institutional transformations.

## 2. Program Learning Outcomes (PLO) Alignment
* **Program Alignment:** PLO 1, 5
* **Competency Framework:** NIST CSF Identify / NIST AI RMF Map–Measure

### Measurable Doctoral Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
1. **CLO 1 (Evaluate):** Critically evaluate theoretical models, empirical evidence, and national standards governing doctoral inquiry and evidence for it decisions.
2. **CLO 2 (Synthesize):** Formulate evidence-based executive strategies that balance technical architecture with fiduciary, legal, and operational risks.
3. **CLO 3 (Design):** Design robust organizational frameworks, technical architectures, or empirical research protocols suitable for enterprise adoption.
4. **CLO 4 (Lead & Defend):** Defend strategic technology interventions before academic committees, executive boards, and regulatory bodies.

## 4. 7-Week Modular Course Outline
* **Module 01:** The Epistemology of Applied Doctoral Inquiry — Evidence-Based IT Decision Making and Theoretical Foundations
* **Module 02:** Framing Authentic Organizational Technology Problems — Problem Statement Scoping and Stakeholder Mapping
* **Module 03:** Critical Literature Appraisal & Synthesis — Systematic Literature Reviews and Synthesis Matrices
* **Module 04:** Theoretical Frameworks & Conceptual Modeling — Theory of Change and Systems Thinking in IT
* **Module 05:** Construct Validity, Internal Validity & Threat Analysis — Research Rigor and Methodological Triangulation
* **Module 06:** Translating Research Evidence into Executive Interventions — Evidence-to-Practice Translation and Policy Briefing
* **Module 07:** Culminating Doctoral Inquiry Defense — Doctoral Research Prospectus and Proposal Defense
