## Scenario A: The Hiring Algorithm

A large technology company deploys an AI-powered applicant screening tool to reduce the time hiring managers spend reviewing resumes. The model was trained on five years of historical hiring data — resumes of candidates who were hired and those who were rejected. After deployment, a data analyst notices the model assigns significantly lower scores to resumes that list involvement in women's professional organizations or historically Black colleges and universities. The hiring manager says the model is just "learning from patterns in the data" and that the company cannot be held responsible for bias in the training data.

In your initial post (175-225 words), address all of the following:

- Identify whether this system is traditional AI, machine learning, or deep learning, and explain your reasoning.
- Identify which Microsoft responsible AI principle is most clearly violated and explain specifically how the scenario demonstrates that violation.
- The hiring manager argues the company is not responsible for bias in training data. Do you agree or disagree? Apply the Accountability principle to support your position.

## Scenario B: The Inventory Prediction System

A national grocery chain replaces its manual inventory ordering process with a machine learning model that predicts how many units of each product to order each week. The model was trained on three years of sales data, promotions, and seasonal patterns. During the first winter after deployment, the model over-orders perishable items by 40% in several regions, resulting in significant food waste. The operations team discovers that the model was trained primarily on data from warm-climate stores and never saw patterns from stores that experience heavy snowfall, which reduces foot traffic.

In your initial post (175-225 words), address all of the following:

- Classify this system using the correct learning paradigm (supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement learning) and explain your reasoning with specific details from the scenario.
- Identify which of the five ML workflow stages — training data collection, feature engineering, model training, evaluation, or deployment — most likely caused this failure, and explain why.
- Propose one concrete technical change and one process change the company should implement to prevent this type of failure in the future.

## Scenario C: The Medical Imaging Assistant

A hospital adopts a deep learning model to assist radiologists in reviewing chest X-rays. The model flags areas of concern for the radiologist to examine more carefully. In a pilot study, the model achieves 94% accuracy on a held-out test set, which exceeds the average radiologist's accuracy of 87% on the same images. Hospital administrators propose eliminating the radiologist review step entirely and relying on the model for final diagnoses to reduce costs and increase throughput.

In your initial post (175-225 words), address all of the following:

- Explain why chest X-ray analysis is classified as a deep learning task rather than traditional machine learning or rule-based AI.
- The model achieved 94% accuracy. Explain why this metric alone may be insufficient to justify eliminating human oversight, referencing at least one responsible AI principle by name.
- Take a position: should the hospital eliminate the radiologist review step? Defend your answer using course concepts.

## Peer Response Guidelines

After submitting your initial post, read your classmates' responses and reply to at least two peers who chose different scenarios than you did. Each peer response must be at least 50 words and must do more than agree — add new analysis, a counter-argument, or a relevant example.

Suggested peer response approaches:

## Grading Rubric (10 Points Total)

### Initial Post (6 Points)

- **6 pts:** Addresses all required sub-questions with technical accuracy. Correctly applies course terminology (learning paradigm, responsible AI principle, ML workflow stage). Meets the 175-225 word requirement. Demonstrates original thinking beyond restating definitions.
- **4-5 pts:** Addresses most sub-questions with generally accurate analysis. Minor terminology errors or missing one sub-question. Meets minimum word count.
- **2-3 pts:** Addresses fewer than half the sub-questions, or contains significant factual errors in classification or responsible AI analysis. May miss word count.
- **0-1 pts:** Initial post is missing, incomplete (fewer than 100 words), or does not engage with the chosen scenario.

### Peer Responses (4 Points)

- **4 pts:** Substantive responses to at least two peers from different scenarios. Each response adds new analysis, examples, or challenges an assumption. Minimum 50 words each.
- **2-3 pts:** Responds to two peers but responses are brief or primarily agree without adding substance. Or responds to only one peer with a strong response.
- **0-1 pts:** Responds to fewer than two peers, or all responses are superficial (e.g., "Great post! I agree.").
