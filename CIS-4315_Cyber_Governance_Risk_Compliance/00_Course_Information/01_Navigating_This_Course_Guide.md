# 🧭 Navigating This Course & 3-Credit-Hour Workload Guide

Welcome to **CIS-4315: Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)** at Texas Wesleyan University! This guide outlines how this 100% online asynchronous course is structured and how to succeed.

---

## 🏛️ SACSCOC / THECB 3-Credit-Hour Workload Statement
In compliance with the Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board (THECB) and federal accreditation guidelines, a 3-credit-hour undergraduate course requires approximately **135 total hours of student engagement** across the 15-week semester (**~9 hours per week**):
* **3.0 Hours/Week:** Direct online instructional engagement (Reading Guides, visual architectural models, video lecture overviews).
* **3.5 Hours/Week:** Hands-on simulation labs, coding exercises, diagnostics, and technical deliverable write-ups.
* **2.5 Hours/Week:** Threaded discussion board inquiry, weekly auto-graded scenario quizzes, and certification review.

---

## 📅 Weekly Rhythm & Submission Timetable
All modules unlock on Monday mornings. Plan your weekly study schedule around these standard deadlines:
* **Thursday by 11:59 PM CT:** Initial substantive post to the weekly Discussion Board.
* **Sunday by 11:59 PM CT:** 
  1. Hands-on Simulation Lab report and screenshot deliverables.
  2. Two peer replies on the weekly Discussion Board (60+ words each).
  3. Weekly Auto-Graded Scenario Quiz (30-minute timer).

---

## 📬 Instructor Communication & Feedback
* **Q&A Discussion Forum:** For all general course and assignment questions, post in the Canvas *Q&A Forum*.
* **Email:** For confidential matters, email Dr. William Nash at **nash@txwes.edu**. Replies are provided within 24–48 hours (Monday–Friday).
* **SpeedGrader Feedback:** Grades and rubric feedback are posted within **7 calendar days** of each Sunday deadline.
