## Course Description

CIS-4315 is a 16-week upper-division course that develops students' competency in information security governance, risk management, regulatory compliance, and incident management. The course is designed to prepare students for the **ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM)** certification examination, one of the most recognized credentials in enterprise information security management.

The course approaches cybersecurity from a management and governance perspective — not a purely technical one. Students will learn to design security programs, assess and treat organizational risk, navigate regulatory compliance obligations, and manage security incidents as the business decisions they fundamentally are. Graduates will be prepared for roles such as Information Security Manager, GRC Analyst, Compliance Manager, Risk Manager, and CISO career tracks.

**Credit Hours:** 3
**Level:** Upper Division (Junior/Senior)
**Prerequisites:** CIS-3321 (Network Administration) or CIS-4328 (Information Security), or instructor approval
**Delivery:** Online asynchronous with optional synchronous discussion sessions

---

## Target Certification

**ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM)**

CISM is a globally recognized management-level certification awarded by ISACA that validates expertise in:
- **Domain 1:** Information Security Governance (~17% of exam)
- **Domain 2:** Information Risk Management (~20% of exam)
- **Domain 3:** Information Security Program Development and Management (~33% of exam)
- **Domain 4:** Incident Management (~30% of exam)

All course content maps to one or more CISM domains. The CISM exam consists of 150 scenario-based multiple choice questions with a 4-hour time limit. Passing candidates must also meet ISACA work experience requirements to receive the CISM designation. Students may take the exam during enrollment or after graduation.

**Exam Registration:** [https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism)

## Grading Policy

| Assessment Component | Weight | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Module Quizzes (Modules 01–16) | 30% | 5-question quiz per module; scenario-based, CISM-aligned. Each quiz = 1.875% of final grade. |
| Lab Activities (Modules 01–16) | 25% | Hands-on documentation exercises per module. Each lab = ~1.56% of final grade. |
| Discussion Board Posts (Selected Modules) | 15% | Weekly professional discussions applying module concepts to real-world scenarios. One original post + two peer responses required. |
| Midterm Assessment (Modules 01–08) | 15% | 30-question scenario-based midterm covering all topics from Modules 01–08. Open from Monday to Sunday of Week 9. |
| Final Exam (Modules 01–16) | 15% | 50-question comprehensive scenario-based final exam in CISM exam format. Timed (90 minutes). Proctored via Canvas. |

### Late Work Policy

Assignments submitted after the due date will receive a 10% deduction per day late, up to a maximum of 50% deduction. Assignments more than 5 days late will not be accepted. Students experiencing extenuating circumstances should contact the instructor before the due date to discuss extensions.

### Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be the student's own. Use of AI-generated text submitted as original student work without disclosure constitutes academic dishonesty. Collaboration on individual assignments is not permitted. Quizzes and exams are individual assessments.

Refer to the Texas Wesleyan University Academic Integrity Policy in the official student handbook.
