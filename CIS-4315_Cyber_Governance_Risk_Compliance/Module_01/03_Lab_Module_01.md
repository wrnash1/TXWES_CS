## Course: CIS-4315 Cyber Governance, Risk, and Compliance

---

## Lab Overview

This lab develops practical governance documentation skills. You will produce four governance artifacts that security managers create in real organizations: a CIA triad asset analysis, a security steering committee charter, a governance-versus-management responsibility matrix, and a risk appetite statement template. No terminal commands are required. All work is document-based and submitted as a single PDF or Word file.

Estimated Time: 90–120 minutes

Point Value: 100 points

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this lab you will be able to:

- Apply the CIA triad to evaluate real information assets
- Draft a security steering committee charter with defined membership and decision authority
- Distinguish governance responsibilities from management responsibilities across organizational roles
- Articulate a risk appetite statement at the board policy level

## Part 2: Security Steering Committee Charter (25 points)

### Part 2 Background

A security steering committee is a governance body that provides cross-functional oversight of the information security program. Without a formal charter, the committee lacks defined authority, membership, and decision-making rules. This part of the lab tasks you with drafting that charter.

### Part 2 Instructions

Using the template structure below, draft a Security Steering Committee Charter for Meridian Regional Hospital. Write each section in complete, professional prose as it would appear in an actual governance document. Do not substitute bullet lists for full sentences in the Purpose and Scope sections.

#### Charter Section 1: Purpose

Write 2–3 sentences stating why the committee exists and what governance problem it solves.

#### Charter Section 2: Scope

Write 2–3 sentences defining the boundary of the committee's authority — what it does and does not control.

#### Charter Section 3: Membership

List a minimum of six committee members by role title (not by name). For each role, write one sentence explaining why that role must be represented.

#### Charter Section 4: Meeting Cadence

Specify how often the committee meets, who may call an emergency meeting, and what constitutes a quorum.

#### Charter Section 5: Decision Authority

Describe three categories of decisions the committee is authorized to make and one category of decision that escalates to the full board.

#### Charter Section 6: Reporting and Accountability

Explain to whom the committee reports, how often, and in what format.

#### Charter Section 7: Charter Review

State how often the charter itself is reviewed and who must approve revisions.

### Part 2 Grading Rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| All seven charter sections present and substantively completed | 10 |
| Membership section includes at least six roles with rationale | 5 |
| Decision authority section identifies appropriate governance vs. escalation boundary | 5 |
| Professional language and document formatting throughout | 5 |

## Part 4: Risk Appetite Statement Draft (25 points)

### Part 4 Background

A risk appetite statement is a board-level governance document that defines the types and amounts of risk the organization is willing to accept. It guides every risk management and security investment decision made by the CISO and the security team.

### Part 4 Instructions

Draft a Risk Appetite Statement for Meridian Regional Hospital. Your statement must address all five sections below. Write in clear, formal, board-policy language that a board member without a technical background could read and understand.

#### Risk Appetite Section 1: Organizational Context

Write 3–4 sentences describing the nature of Meridian's business, the types of information it handles, and why information security is a priority.

#### Risk Appetite Section 2: Overall Risk Appetite Declaration

Write the formal risk appetite declaration. Specify whether the organization's overall posture is risk-averse, risk-neutral, or risk-seeking, and explain the rationale in 2–3 sentences.

#### Risk Appetite Section 3: Risk Appetite by Category

Complete the following table with the organization's stated appetite for each risk category.

|---|---|---|

#### Risk Appetite Section 4: Risk Tolerance Boundaries

Write 2–3 sentences describing the conditions that would trigger escalation to the full board — that is, what level or type of risk, if realized, would require direct board involvement.

#### Risk Appetite Section 5: Review and Approval

State how often the risk appetite statement is reviewed and who must approve it.
