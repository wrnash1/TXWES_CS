## Lab Overview

This lab develops practical security strategy documentation skills. You will produce four artifacts that information security managers create when building or updating a security strategy: a business context analysis, a current state gap assessment, a three-year security roadmap, and an executive summary memo. No terminal commands are required. All work is document-based and submitted as a single PDF or Word file.

Estimated Time: 90–120 minutes

Point Value: 100 points

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this lab you will be able to:

- Analyze business context and identify security implications for each contextual input
- Conduct a gap analysis between current and target security states
- Construct a time-phased security roadmap with prioritized initiatives
- Write an executive-level security strategy summary in business risk language

## Scenario: Pinnacle Financial Services

Pinnacle Financial Services is a mid-size regional bank with 1,400 employees, 22 branch locations, and a growing online banking platform. Pinnacle processes consumer and small business loans, manages deposit accounts, and recently launched a mobile banking application. The bank is state-chartered and subject to examination by federal financial regulators. Its technology infrastructure includes on-premises core banking systems, a hybrid cloud environment, and a third-party managed SOC provider.

The newly hired CISO has been asked by the CEO and board to develop Pinnacle's first formal information security strategy. The previous security program operated without a written strategy and was driven primarily by annual regulatory exam findings. The board has approved a moderate security investment increase over the next three years. The bank's stated business objectives include growing the mobile platform user base by 40 percent, acquiring a smaller community bank within 18 months, and achieving compliance with the FFIEC Cybersecurity Assessment Tool framework within 12 months.

## Part 4: Executive Strategy Summary Memo (25 points)

### Part 4 Background

The executive strategy summary is the CISO's primary communication tool for presenting the security strategy to the board and CEO. It must present strategy in business language — connecting security investments to business objectives and risk reduction, not to technical capabilities.

### Part 4 Instructions

Draft a one-page Executive Strategy Summary Memo from the CISO to the Pinnacle CEO and Board of Directors. Your memo must contain all five sections below. Write in clear, professional language that a board member without a security background can read and act on. Avoid unexplained technical jargon.

#### Memo Section 1: Strategic Context (3–4 sentences)

Briefly describe the business environment and the key risk conditions that make a formal information security strategy necessary for Pinnacle at this time.

#### Memo Section 2: Current Risk Posture (3–4 sentences)

Summarize the organization's current security posture in business risk terms — not as a list of technical gaps, but as a description of the risks the current posture creates for Pinnacle's business objectives.

#### Memo Section 3: Strategic Direction (4–5 sentences)

State the vision and top three strategic objectives for the information security program over the next three years. Each objective should be stated in terms that connect to business outcomes.

#### Memo Section 4: Roadmap Summary (use a brief table or list)

Summarize the key themes of each year of the strategic roadmap in two to three bullet points per year. Frame each theme in terms of what business capability or risk reduction it delivers.

#### Memo Section 5: Resource and Governance Request (3–4 sentences)

State what resource commitment and governance engagement the CISO is requesting from the board to execute the strategy. Be specific about what the board needs to approve, oversee, or provide.

### Part 4 Grading Rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| All five memo sections present and substantively completed | 10 |
| Business risk language used throughout — security presented in terms of business impact | 8 |
| Strategic objectives are specific and connected to Pinnacle's business goals | 4 |
| Memo is professional in tone and could realistically be presented to a board | 3 |
