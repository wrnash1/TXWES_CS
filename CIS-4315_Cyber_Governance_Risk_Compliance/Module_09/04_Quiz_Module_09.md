**Question 1**
Which regulatory law mandates strict electronic security and privacy controls to protect patient health records?
*   A) SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act)
*   B) HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)
*   C) GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act)
*   D) FISMA (Federal Information Security Management Act)
*   **Correct Answer:** B) HIPAA's Security Rule establishes mandatory administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why B is correct:* HIPAA directly regulates the security and privacy of protected health information in the healthcare industry.
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* SOX targets financial reporting integrity for public companies, not patient health data.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* GLBA governs financial institutions' protection of customer non-public personal information, not health records.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* FISMA applies to U.S. federal government information systems, not private sector healthcare organizations.

**Question 2**
Which of the following most accurately describes the **Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)** and its relevance to information security?
*   A) A U.S. federal law requiring healthcare organizations to protect patient health information through administrative, physical, and technical safeguards
*   B) A U.S. federal law requiring public companies to establish internal controls over financial reporting, with IT security controls protecting the integrity of financial systems and data
*   C) An international standard providing requirements for an information security management system applicable to any organization
*   D) A payment card industry standard mandating security controls for organizations that process, store, or transmit cardholder data
*   **Correct Answer:** B) SOX was enacted to prevent financial fraud; Section 404 requires public companies to assess IT general controls that protect financial reporting systems.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* That describes HIPAA's Security Rule, not SOX.
    *   *Why B is correct:* SOX focuses on financial data integrity and accuracy; IT security controls are required to protect the systems that generate financial reports.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* That describes ISO/IEC 27001, an international voluntary standard, not a U.S. statute.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* That describes PCI DSS, an industry standard administered by the PCI Security Standards Council.

**Question 3**
A hospital's IT security manager is developing a HIPAA Security Rule compliance program. Which three safeguard categories must the program address?
*   A) Preventive, Detective, and Corrective
*   B) Administrative, Physical, and Technical
*   C) Governance, Risk, and Compliance
*   D) Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability
*   **Correct Answer:** B) The HIPAA Security Rule organizes all required and addressable implementation specifications into Administrative, Physical, and Technical safeguard categories.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Preventive/Detective/Corrective is a general control classification framework, not the HIPAA Security Rule's own structure.
    *   *Why B is correct:* The HIPAA Security Rule's three safeguard categories are explicitly defined in 45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Governance, Risk, and Compliance is a program management framework, not a HIPAA safeguard taxonomy.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* CIA Triad is a foundational security model; it is not the HIPAA Security Rule's organizational structure for implementation specifications.

**Question 4**
A publicly traded company's external auditor is assessing SOX Section 404 compliance. Which IT activity is most directly in scope for this audit?
*   A) Reviewing whether the company's web application has been tested for SQL injection vulnerabilities
*   B) Assessing whether IT General Controls (access management, change control, computer operations) adequately protect the integrity of financial reporting systems
*   C) Verifying that all employee workstations have current antivirus signature updates
*   D) Confirming that the company's data center meets Tier III uptime standards
*   **Correct Answer:** B) SOX Section 404 audits focus on IT General Controls that protect the reliability and integrity of financial data — particularly access management, change management, and computer operations.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* Application security testing is a vulnerability management activity; while important, it is not the primary SOX Section 404 control category.
    *   *Why B is correct:* IT General Controls (ITGCs) are the foundation of SOX IT audits — they ensure financial systems operate with integrity and that changes to financial applications are authorized and tested.
    *   *Why C is incorrect:* Antivirus currency is a basic endpoint control that may be assessed, but it is not the primary SOX Section 404 focus.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* Data center Tier certification is an infrastructure consideration, not a SOX-specific financial controls requirement.

**Question 5**
An organization that processes electronic health records discovers a ransomware attack has encrypted ePHI affecting 750 patient records. Under HIPAA, what is the maximum number of days the organization has to notify affected individuals after discovering the breach?
*   A) 10 days
*   B) 30 days
*   C) 60 days
*   D) 90 days
*   **Correct Answer:** C) HIPAA's Breach Notification Rule requires covered entities to notify affected individuals no later than 60 calendar days after discovery of a breach involving unsecured PHI.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why A is incorrect:* A 10-day timeline does not exist under HIPAA's Breach Notification Rule; some state breach laws impose faster requirements, but HIPAA's standard is 60 days.
    *   *Why B is incorrect:* 30 days is a common distractor; while some state laws use 30 days, HIPAA's federal standard is 60 days.
    *   *Why C is correct:* 45 CFR § 164.404 specifies notification to individuals "without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 calendar days" after discovery.
    *   *Why D is incorrect:* 90 days exceeds the HIPAA notification deadline and would constitute a compliance violation.
