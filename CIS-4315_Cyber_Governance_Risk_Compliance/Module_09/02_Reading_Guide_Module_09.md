# Reading Guide: Module 09 - Incident Management – Preparation and Detection
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

### Introduction
Welcome to **Module 09 - Incident Management: Preparation and Detection**! This module covers regulatory compliance frameworks that directly affect how organizations design and operate their security programs. HIPAA, SOX, and GLBA are the major U.S. regulatory frameworks that information security managers must understand to ensure the security program satisfies legal obligations.

CISM candidates should understand compliance not as a checklist but as a risk management obligation — each regulation exists to protect specific stakeholders (patients, investors, consumers) from specific harms. Aligning controls to those underlying purposes makes compliance sustainable.

---

### 1. High-Yield Glossary
Review these essential definitions carefully. The certification exam expects you to know these concepts inside and out:

*   **Regulatory compliance**: The state of adhering to laws, regulations, standards, and contractual obligations that apply to an organization's industry, geography, and data types. For information security managers, compliance obligations define minimum required security controls, breach notification timelines, and audit requirements that must be incorporated into the security program.
*   **Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)**: A U.S. federal law enacted in 2002 that requires public companies to establish internal controls over financial reporting to prevent fraud and protect investors. IT security controls supporting SOX (specifically Section 404) must protect the integrity, accuracy, and availability of financial data and the systems that process it.
*   **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)**: A U.S. federal law that establishes security and privacy standards for Protected Health Information (PHI). The HIPAA Security Rule requires healthcare organizations and their business associates to implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect electronic PHI (ePHI).
*   **Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA)**: A U.S. federal law requiring financial institutions to protect the security and confidentiality of customer non-public personal information (NPI). GLBA's Safeguards Rule requires financial institutions to develop, implement, and maintain a written information security program with appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.

### 2. Certification Exam Tips
*   **HIPAA Safeguard Categories:** Know the three HIPAA Security Rule safeguard categories: Administrative (policies, training, risk analysis), Physical (access controls, workstation security), and Technical (encryption, audit controls, transmission security). The exam may ask which category a specific control falls into.
*   **SOX vs. HIPAA:** SOX protects financial data integrity for investors; HIPAA protects patient health information privacy and security. The exam may present a scenario and ask which regulation applies.
*   **Breach Notification Requirements:** HIPAA requires covered entities to notify affected individuals within 60 days of discovering a breach involving 500+ records; HHS must also be notified. Know that state laws may impose stricter timelines.
*   **Study Resource:** [HHS.gov: HIPAA Security Rule Summary](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/index.html) — The official HHS guidance on the HIPAA Security Rule is free and authoritative. The Security Rule text and implementation guidance are directly applicable to CISM exam scenarios involving healthcare organizations.

### Lab & Command Integration
In this week's hands-on lab, you will apply compliance framework concepts through the following activities:
*   **Map HIPAA Security Rule requirements**: Create a table listing all HIPAA Security Rule Required and Addressable implementation specifications and classify each under Administrative, Physical, or Technical safeguard categories.
*   **Identify SOX IT general controls**: Given a scenario describing a public company's financial systems, identify five IT General Controls (ITGCs) that would be required under SOX Section 404 and explain how each protects financial data integrity.
*   **Compare HIPAA and SOX audit scope**: Draft a one-page comparison of what an auditor would examine in a HIPAA Security Rule audit vs. a SOX IT controls audit, noting key differences in scope, evidence, and findings.
