## Scenario A: The Certification Study Plan

A colleague just enrolled in CIS-4337 and plans to take the Terraform Associate 003 exam immediately after completing the course. They have 30 days from today and are unsure how to allocate their study time across the nine exam domains. They have hands-on lab experience from the course but have not worked with Terraform in a production environment.

Discuss: How would you advise them to structure their 30-day study plan? Which domains require the most attention for someone with lab but not production experience? What hands-on exercises or projects beyond the course labs would best prepare them? What exam-day strategy would you recommend for managing the 57-question, 60-minute format?

---

## Scenario B: Building the Organizational IaC Standard

You have just been asked to establish the Terraform standard for a 200-person engineering organization that currently has no IaC governance. Different teams use different Terraform versions, some use local state, and no one follows a consistent module or naming convention. You have three months and one engineer to help you.

Discuss: What are the highest-priority governance items to address first — version pinning, state management, module standards, or CI/CD automation? How would you enforce the standards without alienating teams that have been doing their own thing for years? Which capabilities would you use from HCP Terraform versus enforcing through CI/CD pipelines and open-source tooling?

## Scenario C: The Capstone Retrospective

Reflect on the capstone lab you completed in this module. The lab brought together: VPC networking with for_each subnets, dynamic block security groups, module composition, sensitive variable handling, conditional expressions for environment sizing, and Checkov security scanning.

Discuss: Which part of the capstone was most technically challenging? Was there any concept you had to revisit from an earlier module to complete the capstone? What would you add to the capstone if you were redesigning it — a feature, a cloud service, or a CI/CD integration — and why would that addition be valuable? Connect your answer to at least two specific Terraform Associate exam domains.
