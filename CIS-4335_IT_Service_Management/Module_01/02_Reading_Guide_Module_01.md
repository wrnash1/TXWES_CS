### 1. High-Yield Glossary

Review these essential definitions carefully. The ITIL 4 Foundation exam expects you to know these concepts precisely:

*   **IT Service Management (ITSM)**: A set of policies, practices, and capabilities for designing, delivering, managing, and improving IT services aligned to business needs. ITSM focuses on ensuring IT activities create genuine value for customers and stakeholders rather than simply delivering technical outputs. ITIL 4 is the most widely adopted ITSM framework globally.
*   **ITIL (IT Infrastructure Library)**: A globally recognized best-practice framework for IT service management, originally developed by the UK government's Central Computer and Telecommunications Agency (CCTA) in the 1980s. ITIL evolved through versions v1, v2, v3/2011, and ITIL 4 (published 2019), expanding from operational guidance into a holistic service value model. ITIL 4 is not a prescriptive standard but a flexible framework organizations adapt to their context.
*   **Key ITIL 4 Components**: ITIL 4 is built around the **Service Value System (SVS)**, which has five components: the Guiding Principles, Governance, the Service Value Chain, Practices, and Continual Improvement. A critical exam point: ITIL 4 uses the term **"practices"** — not "processes" — to reflect that effective service activities require people, technology, partners, and information working together, not just defined process steps.

### 2. Certification Exam Tips

*   **Terminology Trap:** ITIL 4 replaced "processes" with **"practices."** If an exam question uses "process" to describe an ITIL 4 management activity, it is likely a distractor. Always select answers using ITIL 4 vocabulary.
*   **Value Co-Creation:** ITIL 4 defines value as co-created between the service provider and consumer — not simply delivered by the provider to the customer. Exam scenario questions frequently test whether you recognize this shift from older delivery-centric thinking.
*   **Know the 7 Guiding Principles:** These apply universally across the ITIL SVS: Focus on Value; Start Where You Are; Progress Iteratively with Feedback; Collaborate and Promote Visibility; Think and Work Holistically; Keep It Simple and Practical; Optimize and Automate.
*   **Know the 4 Dimensions:** Every service must be considered across Organizations & People; Information & Technology; Partners & Suppliers; and Value Streams & Processes. Exam questions describe a scenario and ask which dimension is most relevant.
*   **Study Resource:** The official ITIL 4 Foundation syllabus and glossary are published by Axelos at [Axelos ITIL 4 Foundation](https://www.axelos.com/certifications/itil-service-management/itil-4-foundation) — Axelos owns the ITIL framework and provides the authoritative definitions used on the exam.
