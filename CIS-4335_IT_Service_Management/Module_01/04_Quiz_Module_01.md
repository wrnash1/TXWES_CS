## Question 1

What is the primary focus of ITIL 4?

* A) Writing and maintaining software source code
* B) Co-creating value through service relationships between providers and consumers
* C) Managing virtualization and cloud infrastructure platforms
* D) Designing relational database schemas for enterprise applications

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because ITIL 4 centers the entire framework on value co-creation — both the service provider and service consumer participate in generating outcomes, not merely exchanging deliverables. A is incorrect because ITIL is a service management framework, not a software development methodology. C is incorrect because ITIL 4 addresses management practices across all IT activities; virtualization is a technology choice within the Information and Technology dimension, not ITIL's primary concern. D is incorrect because database design is an implementation detail; ITIL 4 operates at the service management and organizational level.

## Question 2

Which of the following best explains why ITIL 4 uses the term "practices" instead of "processes"?

* A) Practices are informal guidelines with no defined steps, while processes are formal and mandatory.
* B) Practices combine organizational resources — people, technology, information, and partners — to accomplish work, whereas processes describe only sequential steps.
* C) Practices are limited to technical IT operations, while processes cover business management.
* D) Practices replace all ITIL v3 lifecycle stages with a single repeatable workflow.

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because ITIL 4 uses "practices" to signal that effective service management requires people, technology, information, and partners working together — not just documented procedure flowcharts. A is incorrect because practices are not informal; they have defined purposes and recommended activities. C is incorrect because practices span all areas of the SVS including business-facing activities. D is incorrect because ITIL 4 did not collapse everything into one workflow; it defined 34 practices organized within the Service Value System.

## Question 3

An organization wants to understand which SVS component transforms demand and opportunity into value. Which component directly performs this transformation?

* A) Governance
* B) Guiding Principles
* C) Service Value Chain
* D) Continual Improvement

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because the Service Value Chain contains six interconnected activities — Plan, Improve, Engage, Design and Transition, Obtain/Build, and Deliver and Support — that organizations combine to convert inputs into value. A is incorrect because governance directs and controls the organization but does not itself execute the transformation of demand into value. B is incorrect because the Guiding Principles are decision-making recommendations, not an operational transformation model. D is incorrect because Continual Improvement is an ongoing activity embedded throughout the SVS, not the primary transformation mechanism.

## Question 4

A company's IT team reviews a failed service rollout. The post-mortem reveals that the technical solution was sound but teams worked in silos, suppliers were not consulted, and end-user needs were not validated. Which of the ITIL 4 Four Dimensions was most neglected?

* A) Information and Technology only
* B) Value Streams and Processes only
* C) Organizations and People, and Partners and Suppliers
* D) All four dimensions were equally neglected

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because siloed teams indicate a failure in the Organizations and People dimension (roles, culture, collaboration), and not consulting suppliers indicates a failure in the Partners and Suppliers dimension. A is incorrect because the technical solution was described as sound, so Information and Technology was not the primary failure. B is incorrect because Value Streams and Processes concerns workflow design; the scenario focuses on people and supplier failures. D is incorrect because the scenario points to specific dimension failures, not a uniform breakdown.

## Question 5

Which statement correctly distinguishes ITIL 4 from ITIL v3?

* A) ITIL v3 used "practices" while ITIL 4 reverted to "processes" for clarity.
* B) ITIL v3 organized IT service management around a service lifecycle; ITIL 4 uses a Service Value System with a Service Value Chain.
* C) ITIL 4 eliminated all previous ITIL guidance and introduced entirely new concepts with no connection to prior versions.
* D) ITIL v3 and ITIL 4 are functionally identical; ITIL 4 is simply a rebranding exercise.

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because ITIL v3 structured practices across five lifecycle phases while ITIL 4 replaces this with the SVS and a flexible Service Value Chain. A is incorrect — this is reversed: ITIL v3 used "processes" and ITIL 4 introduced "practices." C is incorrect because ITIL 4 builds on and evolves ITIL v3 guidance. D is incorrect because ITIL 4 introduced significant structural changes including the SVS, four dimensions, and explicit integration with Agile, DevOps, and Lean.

## Question 6

A student contacts the campus IT help desk because her email account is inaccessible. She is unable to receive assignments from her professor. In ITIL 4 terms, which role does the student hold?

* A) Customer
* B) Sponsor
* C) User
* D) Service provider

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because the student is a person who uses the service on a day-to-day basis — that is the ITIL 4 definition of a user. A is incorrect because a customer defines service requirements and owns the outcomes of service consumption at a contractual or organizational level. B is incorrect because a sponsor authorizes the budget for service consumption; the student has no budget authority. D is incorrect because the student receives the service, not delivers it.

## Question 7

An IT department provides a file storage service that can store, retrieve, and share any file type up to 50 GB. However, the service is only available 60% of the time due to frequent outages. Which statement best describes this service in ITIL 4 terms?

* A) The service has both utility and warranty.
* B) The service has utility but lacks adequate warranty.
* C) The service has warranty but lacks utility.
* D) The service lacks both utility and warranty.

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because the service does what users need (storing, retrieving, and sharing files = utility, fit for purpose) but fails to meet reasonable availability expectations (60% uptime = inadequate warranty, not fit for use). A is incorrect because 60% availability clearly fails warranty requirements. C is incorrect because the storage and sharing functionality demonstrates clear utility. D is incorrect because utility is present.

## Question 8

Which ITIL 4 Guiding Principle is best illustrated by an organization that assesses its existing workflows before redesigning its incident management process rather than discarding them?

* A) Focus on Value
* B) Keep It Simple and Practical
* C) Start Where You Are
* D) Optimize and Automate

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because "Start Where You Are" directs organizations to assess the current state and reuse what already works rather than starting from scratch. A is incorrect because Focus on Value concerns ensuring actions contribute to stakeholder value, not specifically about assessing the current state. B is incorrect because Keep It Simple and Practical is about eliminating unnecessary complexity, not assessing existing capability. D is incorrect because Optimize and Automate refers to improving efficiency through automation, not the principle of building on existing capability.

## Question 9

Which of the following most accurately describes how ITIL 4 views the creation of value?

* A) Value is created solely by the service provider and then transferred to the customer.
* B) Value is an objective measure calculated from service costs and delivery times.
* C) Value is co-created through active collaboration between the service provider and the service consumer.
* D) Value is determined exclusively by the service sponsor based on budget outcomes.

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because ITIL 4 explicitly defines value as co-created — it emerges from the interaction and collaboration between provider and consumer, not from one side alone. A is incorrect because this describes a delivery-centric model that ITIL 4 deliberately moved away from. B is incorrect because ITIL 4 defines value as a perception, not an objective metric. D is incorrect because while sponsors play a role, users and customers also shape what value means.

## Question 10

An organization is considering outsourcing its server maintenance to a cloud provider. The decision requires evaluating contractual terms, SLA commitments, and the provider's geographic coverage. Which of the ITIL 4 Four Dimensions is the primary focus?

* A) Organizations and People
* B) Information and Technology
* C) Partners and Suppliers
* D) Value Streams and Processes

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because the Partners and Suppliers dimension covers relationships with external parties, including contracts, SLA commitments, and vendor evaluation. A is incorrect because Organizations and People focuses on internal roles, culture, and accountability, not external vendor relationships. B is incorrect because Information and Technology covers technical tools and data infrastructure, not the contractual aspects of a cloud provider. D is incorrect because Value Streams and Processes covers internal workflow design, not supplier relationship management.
