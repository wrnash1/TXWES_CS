**Question 1**
What is the primary difference between an incident and a problem in ITIL?
*   A) Incidents are larger than problems
*   B) Incidents focus on restoring service; problems focus on identifying root causes
*   C) Problems are reported by users; incidents by software
*   D) None of the above
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Incident management restores service quickly. Problem management investigates the underlying causes of incidents.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Incident management restores service quickly. Problem management investigates the underlying causes of incidents.
    *   A and C are common misconceptions.
