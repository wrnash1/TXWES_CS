# Online Course Map: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management
**Target Certification:** ITIL 4 Foundation

## 16-Week Breakdown
*   **Modules 1-4:** ITIL 4 Foundations — Introduction to ITIL 4, the Service Value System, the Four Dimensions of Service Management, and the Seven Guiding Principles
*   **Modules 5-8:** Service Value Chain and Core Practices — Service Value Chain Activities, Continual Improvement, Change Enablement, and Incident Management
*   **Modules 9-12:** Advanced Service Management Practices — Problem Management, Service Desk, Service Level Management, and Deployment Management
*   **Modules 13-15:** Technical and Asset Management Practices — Service Request Management and Release Management, IT Asset Management and Service Configuration Management, and Monitoring and Event Management
*   **Module 16:** Final Exam Preparation and ITIL 4 Foundation Certification Exam
