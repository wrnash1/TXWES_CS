### Practice Question 1

An IT service has been running slowly for the past three days. Multiple users have called the service desk, and technicians have identified that the slowness is caused by a memory leak in the application code. The code defect has been documented with a temporary restart procedure that eliminates the slowness for approximately 12 hours.

What ITIL terms apply to the memory leak (cause) and the restart procedure (temporary solution)?

A. Incident and change

B. Problem and workaround

C. Known error and event

D. Service request and resolution

**Answer: B** — The undocumented cause of repeated incidents is a **problem**. The temporary restart procedure that reduces impact while awaiting a full fix is a **workaround**.

### Practice Question 2

An organization is building a new customer portal service. During design, the team considers the tools and technologies required, the data flows, and the information security controls. They also consider the cultural and staffing changes needed in the support team, and the third-party cloud hosting partner required.

Which ITIL 4 concept encompasses all of these considerations?

A. The Service Value Chain

B. The Four Dimensions of Service Management

C. The Guiding Principles

D. The Continual Improvement Register

**Answer: B** — Considering tools/technology (Information & Technology), staffing/culture (Organizations & People), and third-party partners (Partners & Suppliers) represents the **Four Dimensions** framework.

### Practice Question 3

A service desk analyst receives a call from a user who cannot log in to the HR portal. The analyst resolves the issue by resetting the user's account password. Which ITIL practice is primarily responsible for handling this interaction?

A. Problem Management

B. Change Enablement

C. Service Request Management

D. Incident Management

**Answer: D** — An inability to log in is an **unplanned interruption** to service (even if caused by a password issue). This is an **incident**, handled by Incident Management. Note: if the user proactively requested a password reset before being locked out, it would be a service request. Context matters.

### Practice Question 4

A company's SLA states that the ERP system will be available 99.5% of the time each month. The operations team reports 99.7% availability — above the SLA target. However, the finance director complains that the system is consistently slow during month-end reporting runs, making it nearly unusable for an hour each month.

What ITIL concept does this scenario illustrate?

A. The watermelon SLA effect

B. A service level objective breach

C. A change failure

D. An operational level agreement gap

**Answer: A** — The SLA metric (availability %) shows green, but the customer experience is red (poor performance during critical periods). This is the **watermelon SLA** — green on the outside, red on the inside. The metric being tracked does not capture the customer's actual experience.

### Practice Question 5

An organization wants to deploy a new security patch to 500 servers. The patch deployment process is fully documented, has been tested, follows a defined procedure, and has been approved as a low-risk repeatable process. No individual change approval is required for each instance. What change type is this?

A. Emergency change

B. Normal change

C. Standard change

D. Unauthorized change

**Answer: C** — A **standard change** is pre-authorized because it is low-risk, well-documented, and follows a defined procedure. No individual CAB review is needed per execution.

### Practice Question 6

Which component of the ITIL 4 Service Value System is described as "a set of interconnected activities that an organization performs to deliver a valuable product or service to its consumers"?

A. Service Value Chain

B. Continual Improvement

C. Guiding Principles

D. Governance

**Answer: A** — The **Service Value Chain** is defined as the set of interconnected activities that transforms demand into value. It consists of six activities: Plan, Improve, Engage, Design and Transition, Obtain/Build, and Deliver and Support.

### Practice Question 7

A user contacts the service desk requesting a copy of a software application she is entitled to receive under her department's enterprise license. She does not report any problem with an existing service — she simply needs the software installed on her laptop.

Which ITIL practice is responsible for handling this request?

A. Incident Management

B. Change Enablement

C. Service Request Management

D. IT Asset Management

**Answer: C** — The user is requesting something she is entitled to — this is a **service request** by definition. Service Request Management handles requests for information, standard service actions, and access that users are entitled to receive. No incident (nothing is broken) and no change authorization (it is a pre-approved, routine action) is involved.

### Practice Question 8

The principle of "Think and Work Holistically" is most relevant when:

A. An IT team decides to automate a single repetitive task to save time.

B. A service desk team reviews a user satisfaction survey and improves their call-handling script.

C. An incident manager resolves a reported outage but realizes that the fix affects a dependent service that was not considered during the restoration.

D. A project manager chooses to use an iterative delivery approach instead of a waterfall plan.

**Answer: C** — "Think and Work Holistically" warns against solving problems in isolation without considering downstream effects. The scenario where an incident fix creates a problem in a dependent service is the classic failure this principle addresses. Option A is "Optimize and Automate." Option B is iteration with feedback. Option D is "Progress Iteratively."

### Practice Question 9

An IT organization is reviewing whether to continue using an aging help desk platform or migrate to a modern cloud-based ITSM tool. The IT manager suggests starting the evaluation by documenting what is working well and what is not in the current system before looking at new tools.

Which ITIL 4 Guiding Principle does the IT manager's recommendation reflect?

A. Progress Iteratively with Feedback

B. Keep It Simple and Practical

C. Start Where You Are

D. Focus on Value

**Answer: C** — Assessing the current state before proposing changes — understanding what works and what does not — is the core behavior described by "Start Where You Are."

### Practice Question 10

Which statement correctly describes the relationship between a service, utility, and warranty?

A. Utility is "fit for use" and warranty is "fit for purpose."

B. A service has value when it provides either utility or warranty, but not necessarily both.

C. Utility and warranty are both required for a service to deliver value to the consumer.

D. Warranty is optional for services where the customer does not have an SLA.

**Answer: C** — Both utility (fit for purpose — does what is needed) and warranty (fit for use — available, reliable, secure) are required for value. A service that is always available but does not meet the need has utility but no warranty. A service that meets the need but is unreliable has utility but no warranty. **Both are required.**

### Practice Question 11

An organization's change management team has been told to "reduce bureaucracy." In response, they eliminate the requirement for any change documentation, allowing engineers to deploy changes to production without recording what changed, when, or why.

Which ITIL 4 Guiding Principle is being violated?

A. Optimize and Automate

B. Keep It Simple and Practical

C. Collaborate and Promote Visibility

D. Focus on Value

**Answer: C** — Eliminating documentation removes the visibility that supports collaboration, incident investigation, and audit compliance. "Collaborate and Promote Visibility" requires that information is shared across the organization — change records provide that visibility. Note: "Keep It Simple" is often cited as justification for removing process steps, but simplification should remove non-value steps — not eliminate traceability that serves multiple practices.

### Practice Question 12

An organization experiences a major outage affecting 4,000 users. The incident manager declares a major incident. Which action is specifically associated with the major incident process that would NOT typically apply to a standard Priority 2 incident?

A. Logging the incident in the ITSM tool.

B. Notifying affected users of the impact.

C. Conducting a post-incident review after resolution.

D. Assigning the incident to the first available service desk analyst.

**Answer: C** — Major incidents require a formal **post-incident review** after resolution to understand what happened, assess the response, and identify improvements. Standard P2 incidents may receive a brief closure note but typically do not require a formal post-incident review. Options A, B, and D apply to all incidents.

### Practice Question 13

The ITIL 4 practice responsible for ensuring that accurate information about the configuration of services and CIs is available when and where it is needed is:

A. IT Asset Management

B. Service Configuration Management

C. Deployment Management

D. Knowledge Management

**Answer: B** — **Service Configuration Management** maintains the CMDB with accurate CI records and relationships, ensuring this information is available to support all other practices (incident, change, problem management, etc.).

### Practice Question 14

A financial services company's IT department provides services to internal business units. The network team commits to the service desk that they will restore network connectivity within 2 hours of any outage. What ITIL term describes this internal commitment?

A. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

B. Underpinning Contract (UC)

C. Operational Level Agreement (OLA)

D. Service Level Objective (SLO)

**Answer: C** — An **OLA** (Operational Level Agreement) is an agreement between internal IT teams. It supports the SLA by ensuring each internal team commits to the service levels they need to deliver. The network team to service desk commitment is an internal arrangement — an OLA.

### Practice Question 15

Which of the following is the BEST description of the "Obtain/Build" activity in the ITIL 4 Service Value Chain?

A. Understanding stakeholder needs and managing relationships to enable value co-creation.

B. Acquiring service components and resources needed to deliver services.

C. Planning and managing the deployment of new services to customers.

D. Monitoring the performance of services against agreed service levels.

**Answer: B** — The **Obtain/Build** activity ensures that service components are available when and where needed and meet specifications. This includes both acquiring from suppliers (Obtain) and internally developing (Build) components such as software, infrastructure, and processes.

### Practice Question 16

An IT team wants to improve deployment frequency from once per month to twice per week. Before changing the deployment process, the team leader suggests piloting the change with one application team for two sprints, measuring the results, and adjusting before rolling out to all teams.

Which ITIL 4 Guiding Principle best describes this approach?

A. Focus on Value

B. Start Where You Are

C. Progress Iteratively with Feedback

D. Think and Work Holistically

**Answer: C** — Running a limited pilot, measuring outcomes, and adjusting before full rollout is the definition of **Progress Iteratively with Feedback** — making incremental changes with feedback loops to guide the next step.

### Practice Question 17

Which of the following is an example of a "service request" rather than an "incident"?

A. A user cannot open their email client.

B. The organization's VPN is intermittently disconnecting for all remote workers.

C. A user requests access to a shared team folder they are entitled to.

D. The payroll system produces incorrect salary calculations for 47 employees.

**Answer: C** — A request for access to something the user is **entitled to receive** as a standard service action is a **service request**. Options A, B, and D all describe unplanned interruptions or service degradations — **incidents**.

### Practice Question 18

An IT service provider is designing a new data analytics service for a healthcare client. The team must consider HIPAA compliance requirements, data sovereignty laws in the client's state, and sustainability commitments affecting data center operations.

Which ITIL 4 concept encompasses these external constraints?

A. The Four Dimensions of Service Management

B. The Service Value System

C. PESTLE factors

D. The Guiding Principles

**Answer: C** — HIPAA is Legal, state data sovereignty is Legal/Political, and sustainability commitments are Environmental. These external factors that influence service design are captured by the **PESTLE** framework (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental) that surrounds the Four Dimensions.

### Practice Question 19

An organization's IT team regularly holds retrospective sessions after each sprint to identify what went well, what could be improved, and what actions to take in the next sprint. These sessions feed into an ongoing improvement backlog.

Which ITIL 4 practice is most closely aligned with this behavior?

A. Service Desk

B. Problem Management

C. Continual Improvement

D. Organizational Change Management

**Answer: C** — Regular structured retrospectives that identify improvement opportunities and feed an ongoing improvement backlog are the operational expression of the **Continual Improvement** practice. The Continual Improvement Register (CIR) is the equivalent of the improvement backlog.
