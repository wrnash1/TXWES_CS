## Question 1

Which SVC activity ensures a shared understanding of the vision, current status, and improvement direction for all four dimensions and all products and services?

* A) Engage
* B) Plan
* C) Improve
* D) Design and Transition

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because Plan is specifically defined as ensuring shared understanding of vision, current status, and improvement direction across the organization and all four dimensions. A is incorrect because Engage is about stakeholder relationship management and capturing demand, not organizational strategic alignment. C is incorrect because Improve is about continual enhancement of practices and services, not shared strategic direction. D is incorrect because Design and Transition is about ensuring new or changed services meet quality and time expectations, not organizational strategic alignment.

## Question 2

An organization's operations team monitors service performance, identifies a recurring pattern of VPN failures, and creates a formal improvement initiative to redesign the VPN authentication architecture. Which two SVC activities are most directly involved in this scenario?

* A) Engage and Design and Transition
* B) Plan and Obtain/Build
* C) Deliver and Support and Improve
* D) Improve and Design and Transition

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because monitoring and identifying patterns from service operation is Deliver and Support activity, and creating and processing formal improvement initiatives is the Improve activity. D is incorrect because while Design and Transition may be involved in implementing the solution, the scenario describes identification and initiation of an improvement, not the design phase. A is incorrect because Engage is stakeholder relationship management, not service performance monitoring. B is incorrect because planning and procurement are not the primary activities described.

## Question 3

A service desk agent receives a call from a customer who wants to understand why their service has been unavailable three times in the past month. The agent reviews the service record, explains the causes of each outage, and shares the planned remediation timeline. Which SVC activity does this interaction primarily represent?

* A) Plan
* B) Improve
* C) Engage
* D) Deliver and Support

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because Engage is defined as maintaining good understanding of stakeholder needs and establishing good relationships — communicating service performance to a customer and managing that relationship is a core Engage activity. D is incorrect because while Deliver and Support handles incident resolution, the scenario specifically describes customer communication and relationship management — a post-incident engagement. A is incorrect because Plan is about strategic direction, not individual customer interactions. B is incorrect because Improve is about processing improvement data, not customer communication.

## Question 4

A university IT department purchases and configures 50 new desktop computers for a student lab. The computers are delivered, imaged with the standard software load, and tested against the university's hardware and software specifications before being placed in service. Which SVC activity best describes this work?

* A) Design and Transition
* B) Obtain/Build
* C) Deliver and Support
* D) Plan

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because Obtain/Build covers ensuring service components are available when and where needed and meet agreed specifications — purchasing, configuring, and testing hardware to specification is the Obtain/Build activity. A is incorrect because Design and Transition covers designing and planning services, not procuring and configuring specific hardware components. C is incorrect because Deliver and Support covers day-to-day service delivery after components are deployed, not component acquisition and preparation. D is incorrect because Plan covers strategic direction, not physical asset procurement.

## Question 5

The Improve activity is unique among the six SVC activities because of which characteristic?

* A) It is the only activity that receives inputs from external stakeholders.
* B) It connects bidirectionally to all other SVC activities simultaneously.
* C) It is managed exclusively by senior IT leadership and not by operational staff.
* D) It only activates when a formal improvement request is submitted to the IT director.

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because ITIL 4 explicitly identifies the Improve activity as unique in that it connects bidirectionally to all other activities — every activity feeds improvement data into Improve, and Improve feeds improvement guidance back into every activity. A is incorrect because Engage specifically handles external stakeholder inputs; Improve receives inputs from internal SVC activities. C is incorrect because ITIL 4 applies the Improve activity at all organizational levels, not exclusively to senior leadership. D is incorrect because ITIL 4 describes Improve as a continuous, always-active component of the SVC, not one that only activates on formal request.

## Question 6

An IT team is testing a new payroll system before it goes live. They run integration tests with the HR system and the finance reporting platform, conduct user acceptance testing with payroll staff, and confirm that all performance requirements are met. Which SVC activity does this work belong to?

* A) Obtain/Build
* B) Deliver and Support
* C) Engage
* D) Design and Transition

Correct answer: D

Distractor analysis: D is correct because testing a service before going live — integration testing, user acceptance testing, and performance validation — is part of Design and Transition, which ensures products and services meet stakeholder expectations before operational deployment. A is incorrect because Obtain/Build covers acquiring and configuring service components, not testing them against stakeholder acceptance criteria. B is incorrect because Deliver and Support begins after the service is transitioned to live operation, not before go-live testing. C is incorrect because Engage covers stakeholder communication and requirements, not technical testing.

## Question 7

Which of the following best describes a value stream in ITIL 4?

* A) The complete set of all six SVC activities as documented in the ITIL 4 framework
* B) A specific combination of SVC activities followed to create and deliver a product or service to a consumer
* C) The inputs and outputs that flow between the SVC and external stakeholders
* D) A standardized sequence of SVC activities mandated for all organizations by ITIL 4

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because ITIL 4 defines a value stream as a series of steps an organization takes to create and deliver products and services — value streams are specific, contextual combinations of SVC activities, not the complete set. A is incorrect because the complete set of six activities describes the SVC itself, not a value stream. C is incorrect because inputs and outputs between the SVC and stakeholders describe the SVC's relationship with demand and value, not a value stream. D is incorrect because ITIL 4 explicitly does not mandate a standard sequence; value streams are flexible and organization-specific.

## Question 8

An organization's IT service desk handles 500 incidents per day. The service desk team resolves incidents, logs resolution notes, and tracks resolution times. At the end of each week, this data is compiled and reviewed to identify improvement opportunities. Which two SVC activities are described in this scenario?

* A) Plan and Engage
* B) Deliver and Support, and Improve
* C) Obtain/Build and Design and Transition
* D) Engage and Deliver and Support

Correct answer: B

Distractor analysis: B is correct because handling and resolving incidents is Deliver and Support, and the weekly review of data to identify improvement opportunities is the Improve activity. A is incorrect because Plan and Engage do not describe incident handling or operational performance review. C is incorrect because Obtain/Build and Design and Transition are not operational incident management activities. D is incorrect because the scenario describes incident resolution (Deliver and Support) and improvement review (Improve), not stakeholder engagement.

## Question 9

A service provider organization receives a formal requirement from a key customer: all IT services must support API integration with the customer's enterprise systems by the end of the fiscal year. The IT team captures this requirement, documents it formally, and communicates it to the relevant technical teams. Which SVC activity does this work primarily represent?

* A) Plan
* B) Design and Transition
* C) Engage
* D) Obtain/Build

Correct answer: C

Distractor analysis: C is correct because capturing formal requirements from a key customer and translating them into inputs for other SVC activities is the Engage activity — it provides the good understanding of stakeholder needs that the SVC depends on. A is incorrect because Plan produces strategic direction for the organization, not the capture of individual customer requirements. B is incorrect because Design and Transition designs and tests services based on requirements already captured; the scenario describes the capture step, not the design step. D is incorrect because Obtain/Build is about acquiring and building service components, not capturing requirements.

## Question 10

An organization's IT team realizes that its deployment process for software updates routinely takes longer than expected because the configuration of each environment must be manually verified before deployment. They implement an automated environment configuration check that runs before every deployment, reducing pre-deployment verification time by 75%. Which SVC activity and which Guiding Principle are most directly reflected in this improvement?

* A) Obtain/Build and Optimize and Automate
* B) Deliver and Support and Keep It Simple and Practical
* C) Design and Transition and Progress Iteratively with Feedback
* D) Improve and Start Where You Are

Correct answer: A

Distractor analysis: A is correct because automating the environment configuration check is an Obtain/Build activity (ensuring service components are available and meet specifications) that directly applies the Optimize and Automate Guiding Principle (automating a repetitive, rule-based task to free human attention). B is incorrect because Deliver and Support is operational service delivery, not deployment pipeline optimization; and Keep It Simple and Practical is about reducing complexity, not specifically automation. C is incorrect because Design and Transition is about service design and pre-go-live testing, not deployment pipeline automation. D is incorrect because while Improve is involved in identifying the problem, and Start Where You Are informs the approach, the actual work of implementing the automation is Obtain/Build activity guided by Optimize and Automate.
