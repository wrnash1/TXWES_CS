### Question 1

Which of the four Agile Manifesto values directly addresses the preference for ongoing stakeholder engagement over locked-in contractual scope?

* A) Working Software over Comprehensive Documentation
* B) Individuals and Interactions over Processes and Tools
* C) Customer Collaboration over Contract Negotiation
* D) Responding to Change over Following a Plan

Correct Answer: C) Customer Collaboration over Contract Negotiation prioritizes continuous stakeholder dialogue over a fixed-scope contract signed at project start.

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why C is correct:* This value explicitly names "Contract Negotiation" as what is deprioritized, and "Customer Collaboration" as what Agile teams emphasize throughout development.
* *Why A is incorrect:* This value addresses the trade-off between delivering software and producing documents, not stakeholder engagement.
* *Why B is incorrect:* This value addresses how teams communicate and are organized, not the relationship between delivery teams and clients.
* *Why D is incorrect:* This value addresses flexibility in planning when circumstances change, not the format of the customer relationship.

### Question 2

Which of the following is the most accurate definition of the Agile value "Responding to Change over Following a Plan"?

* A) Agile teams do not create plans; all work is performed spontaneously based on daily priorities.
* B) Agile teams value the ability to adjust direction when new information or changing requirements emerge, while still acknowledging that planning has value.
* C) Agile teams must re-plan every day regardless of whether circumstances have changed.
* D) Following a fixed plan is forbidden once a project has been declared Agile.

Correct Answer: B)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why B is correct:* The Manifesto says "Responding to Change *over* Following a Plan" — the word "over" means planning has value but adaptability is prioritized when the two conflict.
* *Why A is incorrect:* All Agile frameworks, including Scrum, include planning events (Sprint Planning, Release Planning). The Manifesto does not eliminate planning.
* *Why C is incorrect:* Daily re-planning regardless of circumstances is not an Agile principle; it would waste team capacity and destabilize Sprint Goals.
* *Why D is incorrect:* The Manifesto explicitly states "the items on the right have value" — following a plan has value, it is simply not the top priority when trade-offs arise.

### Question 3

Agile Principle 1 states that the highest priority is to satisfy the customer through early and continuous delivery of valuable software. Which Scrum practice most directly implements this principle?

* A) Writing a comprehensive requirements specification before the first Sprint begins
* B) Delivering a potentially releasable product increment at the end of every Sprint
* C) Holding a Sprint Retrospective to improve team processes
* D) Assigning story points to backlog items during refinement

Correct Answer: B)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why B is correct:* Delivering a potentially releasable increment every Sprint (1–4 weeks) operationalizes "early and continuous delivery of valuable software" directly.
* *Why A is incorrect:* Comprehensive upfront requirements are a Waterfall characteristic that Agile reacts against; they delay delivery rather than enabling it continuously.
* *Why C is incorrect:* The Sprint Retrospective improves team process (Principle 12), not the customer delivery cadence of Principle 1.
* *Why D is incorrect:* Story point estimation is a backlog management technique; it does not itself deliver software to the customer.

### Question 4

Agile Principle 8 states that Agile processes promote sustainable development — sponsors, developers, and users should be able to maintain a constant pace indefinitely. Which team behavior violates this principle?

* A) The team completes the same number of story points each Sprint and declines additional work beyond their established velocity.
* B) The team works 60-hour weeks for three consecutive Sprints to meet a release deadline, planning to rest afterward.
* C) The team splits a large user story across two Sprints because it exceeds their capacity.
* D) The Product Owner reduces scope for the current Sprint when a developer is unexpectedly unavailable.

Correct Answer: B)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why B is correct:* Deliberately working unsustainable hours violates the sustainable pace principle; "planning to rest afterward" acknowledges the pace cannot be maintained indefinitely.
* *Why A is incorrect:* Maintaining a consistent velocity and declining overload directly upholds the sustainable pace principle.
* *Why C is incorrect:* Splitting stories to fit capacity is a healthy backlog management practice that keeps work sustainable.
* *Why D is incorrect:* Adjusting Sprint scope in response to reduced capacity protects the team's sustainable pace and is consistent with Agile adaptation.

### Question 5

Agile Principle 12 states that at regular intervals, the team reflects on how to become more effective, then tunes and adjusts its behavior. Which Scrum event directly implements this principle?

* A) Sprint Planning — where the team selects backlog items and creates the Sprint Goal
* B) Daily Scrum — where developers synchronize work and identify impediments
* C) Sprint Review — where stakeholders inspect the product increment and discuss the backlog
* D) Sprint Retrospective — where the team inspects its own processes and identifies improvements

Correct Answer: D)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why D is correct:* The Sprint Retrospective is explicitly designed for the Scrum Team to inspect its own ways of working and create a plan for improvement — a direct implementation of Principle 12.
* *Why A is incorrect:* Sprint Planning focuses on what work will be done in the Sprint and how — it is forward-looking for delivery, not a reflection on team effectiveness.
* *Why B is incorrect:* The Daily Scrum is a 15-minute synchronization for the Developers; it addresses daily impediments but is not the structured improvement reflection described in Principle 12.
* *Why C is incorrect:* The Sprint Review inspects the product increment and adapts the Product Backlog; it is product-focused rather than a team process reflection.
