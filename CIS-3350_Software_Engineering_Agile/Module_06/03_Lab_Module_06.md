# Lab Activity: Module 06 – Product Backlog: Creation and Refinement

**Course:** CIS-3350 Software Engineering and Agile
**Certification Alignment:** PSM I (Professional Scrum Master I) – Scrum.org
**Instructor:** Professor Nash | Texas Wesleyan University
**Points:** 100

---

## Overview

This lab is a Product Backlog creation and refinement exercise. You will write a 10-item Product Backlog for a hypothetical project, prioritize (order) the items with justification, evaluate each item against the INVEST criteria, and perform a mini-refinement session on two items. No programming is required.

The ability to create and refine a Product Backlog is a foundational Agile skill directly tested by PSM I scenario questions.

Estimated time: 90–120 minutes

---

## Part 1 — Product Backlog Creation (50 points)

### Part 1 Instructions

You are the Product Owner for PetConnect — a mobile app that helps pet owners track their pets' veterinary appointments, vaccination records, medication schedules, and connect with a community of local pet owners.

Write a Product Backlog with exactly 10 user stories for PetConnect. Your user stories must follow the standard format: "As a [user type], I can [action] so that [benefit]."

For each story, provide:

- Story ID (PBI-01 through PBI-10)
- User story in standard format
- Story Point estimate (use Fibonacci scale: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21)
- Value statement (1–2 sentences explaining the business or user value)
- Acceptance criteria (at least 2 criteria per story, written as bullet points)

Your 10 stories should cover a meaningful range of app functionality. Do not write 10 variations of the same feature. A good backlog covers multiple user types (pet owner, veterinarian, admin) and multiple feature areas (record management, scheduling, community, notifications).

### Part 1 Grading (50 points)

- Each user story (10 × 5 pts):
  - Correct user story format: 1 pt
  - Story Point estimate provided: 1 pt
  - Value statement: 1 pt
  - At least 2 acceptance criteria: 2 pts

---

## Part 2 — Product Backlog Ordering (25 points)

### Part 2 Instructions

Order your 10 Product Backlog items from most important (rank 1) to least important (rank 10). Present your ordering as a numbered list showing: Rank — Story ID — Story Title (shortened).

Then write a 200–300 word justification for your ordering that addresses:

1. Which ordering factors you used (value, risk, dependency, learning value, market timing, cost of delay) — use at least three distinct factors
2. Why your top three items are ranked where they are — be specific
3. At least one dependency relationship between two items and how you handled it in the ordering

### INVEST Evaluation Table

After completing your ordering, evaluate each of your 10 PBIs against the INVEST criteria. Create a table:

| Story ID | I | N | V | E | S | T | Needs Refinement? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PBI-01 | Yes/No | Yes/No | Yes/No | Yes/No | Yes/No | Yes/No | Yes/No |

For any story that fails two or more INVEST criteria, mark it as "Needs Refinement" and briefly note which criteria fail and why (one sentence each).

### Part 2 Grading (25 points)

- Ordered list (10 items): 5 points
- Ordering justification (three factors, top-three explanation, dependency): 12 points (4 pts each component)
- INVEST evaluation table with notes on failing items: 8 points

---

## Part 3 — Backlog Refinement Simulation (25 points)

### Part 3 Instructions

Choose two of your Product Backlog items that you marked as "Needs Refinement" in Part 2 (or the two lowest-ranked items if all passed INVEST). Perform a written refinement session for each item.

For each selected item, your refinement output must include:

- Original story as written in Part 1
- Problems identified during refinement (which INVEST criteria fail and why)
- Refined version of the story (rewritten to address INVEST failures)
- If the story was too large: split it into 2–3 smaller stories, each meeting INVEST criteria
- Updated acceptance criteria (at least 3 for each refined story or sub-story)
- Updated story point estimate for the refined story or each sub-story

Then write a 100–150 word reflection on the refinement process: What changed most significantly between the original and refined versions? Why does this level of refinement matter for Sprint Planning?

### Part 3 Grading (25 points)

- Item 1 Refinement (original, problems, refined version, updated AC, updated estimate): 10 points
- Item 2 Refinement (original, problems, refined version, updated AC, updated estimate): 10 points
- Reflection on refinement process: 5 points
