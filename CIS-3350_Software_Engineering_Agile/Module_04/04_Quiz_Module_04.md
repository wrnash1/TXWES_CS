# Quiz: Module 04 – Scrum in Practice: Sprint Planning and Daily Scrum

## Course: CIS-3350_Software_Engineering_Agile (Professional Scrum Master (PSM I))

---

### Question 1

According to the Scrum Guide, the Daily Scrum is an event intended for which group?

* A) The entire Scrum Team including the Product Owner and Scrum Master
* B) The Developers only
* C) The Scrum Master and any impediment owners
* D) All project stakeholders who have interest in Sprint progress

Correct Answer: B) The Daily Scrum is held by and for the Developers to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog accordingly.

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why B is correct:* The Scrum Guide states the Daily Scrum is "for the Developers." The Product Owner and Scrum Master may attend if they are contributing as Developers, but they are not required attendees.
* *Why A is incorrect:* Requiring the entire Scrum Team misrepresents the Daily Scrum's purpose; it is specifically the Developers' synchronization event.
* *Why C is incorrect:* The Scrum Master is not required and impediment owners are not an event-specific attendee group in the Scrum Guide.
* *Why D is incorrect:* Stakeholders do not attend the Daily Scrum. It is an internal Developers' event; stakeholders engage with the Scrum Team at the Sprint Review.

### Question 2

Which of the following is the most accurate definition of a Sprint Goal?

* A) A detailed list of all tasks the Developers must complete during the Sprint.
* B) A single, cohesive objective that describes why the Sprint is valuable and gives Developers flexibility in how they achieve it.
* C) A contract between the Product Owner and Developers that cannot be modified for any reason.
* D) A status report submitted to management summarizing the Sprint's planned deliverables.

Correct Answer: B)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why B is correct:* The Scrum Guide defines the Sprint Goal as the single objective for the Sprint that provides coherent focus and allows Developers to adapt their approach if needed while still serving the goal.
* *Why A is incorrect:* A list of tasks is the Sprint Backlog (the plan), not the Sprint Goal. The Sprint Goal is a single unifying objective, not an enumeration of tasks.
* *Why C is incorrect:* Sprint Goals can be negotiated during the Sprint if new learning changes the situation, and in extreme cases the Product Owner may cancel the Sprint if the goal becomes obsolete.
* *Why D is incorrect:* The Sprint Goal is an internal team commitment — not a management status report or document.

### Question 3

A development team's Daily Scrum has grown to 45 minutes because each Developer gives a detailed status update to the Scrum Master, who then makes task assignments for the day. Which two Scrum principles are being violated?

* A) The Sprint timebox and the Definition of Done
* B) The 15-minute timebox and the Developers' self-management of their own work
* C) The Product Owner's backlog authority and the Sprint cancellation rule
* D) The Sprint Review's stakeholder inspection and the Sprint Retrospective's improvement process

Correct Answer: B)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why B is correct:* The Daily Scrum is timeboxed at 15 minutes (violated by a 45-minute meeting) and Developers self-assign their own work — the Scrum Master does not make task assignments (violation of self-management).
* *Why A is incorrect:* The Sprint timebox and Definition of Done are separate concepts unrelated to Daily Scrum duration or who assigns tasks.
* *Why C is incorrect:* These describe Product Owner and Sprint cancellation rules — neither is relevant to a Daily Scrum anti-pattern.
* *Why D is incorrect:* Sprint Review and Retrospective are separate events with different purposes; neither is related to Daily Scrum facilitation problems.

### Question 4

During Sprint Planning, after agreeing on the Sprint Goal, the Developers discover they have overcommitted — they selected more work than they can realistically complete. What should happen?

* A) The Scrum Master extends the Sprint length to accommodate the extra work.
* B) The Product Owner takes the Sprint Backlog items back and re-orders the Product Backlog.
* C) The Developers negotiate with the Product Owner to remove items from the Sprint Backlog until the forecast is achievable.
* D) The team proceeds anyway and completes as many items as possible, deferring the rest.

Correct Answer: C)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why C is correct:* Sprint Planning is a collaborative negotiation. Developers forecast what they can do; if they have overcommitted, they remove items from the Sprint Backlog — in collaboration with the Product Owner — until the forecast is realistic.
* *Why A is incorrect:* The Scrum Master does not extend Sprint lengths. The Sprint duration is fixed at the start of the Sprint; it does not change during the Sprint.
* *Why B is incorrect:* The Product Owner does not take Sprint Backlog items back unilaterally. Sprint Backlog ownership belongs to the Developers; collaborative removal is the correct approach.
* *Why D is incorrect:* Knowingly committing to more than can be completed sets an unreliable Sprint Goal and erodes stakeholder trust. Accurate forecasting is a core Scrum responsibility.

### Question 5

What are the three topics that Sprint Planning must address, as defined in the Scrum Guide?

* A) Budget approval, resource allocation, and risk log creation
* B) Why the Sprint is valuable, what can be done, and how the work will be accomplished
* C) Sprint velocity review, defect backlog grooming, and release scheduling
* D) Team skills assessment, stakeholder approval, and architecture review

Correct Answer: B)

Distractor Analysis:

* *Why B is correct:* The Scrum Guide explicitly states Sprint Planning addresses three topics: (1) Why — establishing the Sprint Goal; (2) What — selecting Product Backlog items; (3) How — Developers plan the work to create the Increment.
* *Why A is incorrect:* Budget, resource allocation, and risk logs are project management concepts outside the Scrum Guide's definition of Sprint Planning.
* *Why C is incorrect:* Velocity review, defect grooming, and release scheduling may be relevant practices but are not the three Scrum Guide-defined topics of Sprint Planning.
* *Why D is incorrect:* Skills assessment, stakeholder approval, and architecture review are not Sprint Planning topics in the Scrum Guide.
