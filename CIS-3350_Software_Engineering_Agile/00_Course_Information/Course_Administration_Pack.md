# Course Administration Pack: CIS-3350 – Software Engineering and Agile

## Texas Wesleyan University | College of Business and Computing

---

## Course Description

CIS-3350 Software Engineering and Agile is a 16-week undergraduate course that prepares students to design, develop, and deliver software using industry-standard engineering principles and Agile methodologies. The course covers the full software development life cycle, the Agile Manifesto and its 12 principles, the Scrum framework in theory and practice, user story writing, backlog management, estimation, testing practices (TDD/BDD), CI/CD pipelines, scaled Agile frameworks, and project metrics.

By the end of the course, students are eligible — and academically prepared — to sit for the **Professional Scrum Master I (PSM I)** certification assessment offered by Scrum.org. PSM I certification is globally recognized and valued by employers in software development, IT consulting, and product management roles.

## Zero-Textbook-Cost (ZTC) Materials

This course is fully ZTC-compliant. All required readings are free and openly available:

* **The Scrum Guide (2020):** [scrumguides.org](https://scrumguides.org/) — the official, free Scrum reference maintained by Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland. This is the only document PSM I is based on. Download the PDF or read online.
* **Agile Manifesto and 12 Principles:** [agilemanifesto.org](https://agilemanifesto.org/) — the original 2001 declaration. Both the values page and the principles page are required reading.
* **Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams:** [scrum.org/resources/kanban-guide-scrum-teams](https://www.scrum.org/resources/kanban-guide-scrum-teams) — free official guide.
* **Evidence-Based Management Guide:** [scrum.org/resources/evidence-based-management-guide](https://www.scrum.org/resources/evidence-based-management-guide) — free metrics framework.
* **Agile Alliance Glossary:** [agilealliance.org/agile101/agile-glossary](https://www.agilealliance.org/agile101/agile-glossary/) — free reference for supplementary Agile concepts.
* **Refactoring.Guru — Design Patterns:** [refactoring.guru/design-patterns](https://refactoring.guru/design-patterns) — free visual design pattern reference.

No commercial textbook purchase is required. Students needing supplemental depth may optionally consult library copies of *The Scrum Field Guide* (Mitch Lacey) or *User Stories Applied* (Mike Cohn), but these are not required.

## PSM I Certification Details

* **Issuer:** Scrum.org (co-founded by Ken Schwaber, co-creator of Scrum)
* **Exam format:** 80 questions, 60-minute time limit, online, open book
* **Passing score:** 85% (68 of 80 correct)
* **Question types:** Multiple choice, multiple answer (select all that apply), matching
* **Cost:** $200 USD per attempt (no mandatory training required)
* **Validity:** Lifetime — does not expire or require renewal
* **Registration:** [scrum.org/professional-scrum-master-i-certification](https://www.scrum.org/professional-scrum-master-i-certification)

Students who complete this course and score 90%+ on the free Scrum.org Open Assessment consistently are well-prepared to pass the PSM I on their first attempt.

### Late Work Policy

Lab assignments submitted within 48 hours of the deadline receive a 10% late penalty. Submissions more than 48 hours late receive a zero. Discussion posts submitted after the weekly deadline do not receive peer response credit. Contact the instructor before the deadline if extenuating circumstances apply.

## Academic Integrity

All quiz and exam submissions must represent the student's individual work. Sharing quiz answers, submitting AI-generated lab work without disclosure, or copying another student's lab submission constitutes academic dishonesty subject to university policy. The PSM I exam at Scrum.org is open book — similarly, quizzes in this course are designed to assess understanding rather than memorization; open-book exam strategies that rely on understanding will serve you better on both the course final and the certification exam.
