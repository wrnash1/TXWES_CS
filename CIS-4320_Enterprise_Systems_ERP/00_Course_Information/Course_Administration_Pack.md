### Texas Wesleyan University — Department of Computer Information Systems

## Course Description

CIS-4320 Enterprise Systems & ERP is a 16-week upper-division course that prepares students to work with the enterprise software platforms that run the world's largest organizations. Students develop hands-on proficiency with SAP S/4HANA and Salesforce — the two most widely deployed ERP and CRM platforms globally — while building the process modeling, data management, integration, and security skills required in enterprise IT roles.

The course is structured in three thematic blocks: ERP Foundations (Modules 01–06), covering SAP architecture, financial accounting, supply chain, and HR modules; CRM and Salesforce Platform (Modules 07–11), covering the Salesforce data model, automation, and integration; and Enterprise Operations (Modules 12–16), covering data migration, security, cloud hosting, post-implementation management, and certification readiness.

Upon successful completion, students will be prepared to sit for the **Salesforce Certified Associate** exam and have a strong foundation for the **SAP Certified Associate** exam in their chosen functional track.

## Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) Commitment

This course is a Zero Textbook Cost course. All required learning materials are free of charge. No textbook purchase is required or expected.

### Free Required Platform Accounts

Students must create the following free accounts before Week 1:

* **Salesforce Developer Org (free, permanent):** Sign up at [developer.salesforce.com/signup](https://developer.salesforce.com/signup). This provides a fully functional Salesforce environment for all CRM labs. Use a personal email address, not a school email, to ensure the org persists after graduation.
* **Salesforce Trailhead account (free):** Sign up at [trailhead.salesforce.com](https://trailhead.salesforce.com). All required readings are free Trailhead modules and Trailmixes. Trailhead badges earned here count toward Salesforce certification eligibility and can be added to your LinkedIn profile.
* **openSAP account (free):** Register at [open.sap.com](https://open.sap.com). This provides access to free SAP-produced MOOCs covering S/4HANA Finance, Materials Management, and SuccessFactors.
* **SAP Learning Hub Free Tier (optional):** Available at [learning.sap.com](https://learning.sap.com) — free SAP Learning Journeys provide guided paths for each SAP certification track.

### Late Work Policy

Assignments submitted after the due date receive a 10% deduction per day, up to a maximum of 50%. Assignments submitted more than 5 days late receive a maximum score of 50%. Module quizzes close at the end of their module week and cannot be taken late without an approved accommodation.

### Academic Integrity

All submitted work must be the student's own. Use of generative AI tools to complete lab write-ups, quiz answers, or discussion posts without disclosure is a violation of academic integrity policy. Students may use AI tools as a learning aid (e.g., asking for explanations of concepts) but must complete all assessed work independently.

## Salesforce Certification Information

### Salesforce Certified Associate

* **Exam cost:** $75 USD (discounts available through Trailhead Connect program)
* **Questions:** 40 multiple choice, 70 minutes
* **Passing score:** 62%
* **Registration:** [salesforce.com/training/certification](https://www.salesforce.com/training/certification)
* **Recommended after:** Completing Modules 07–11 and earning the Trailhead Associate Cert Prep Trailmix badges
* **Maintenance:** No maintenance exam required — the Associate credential does not expire

### SAP Certified Associate

* **Exam cost:** Approximately $530 USD through SAP's Certification Hub
* **Questions:** 80 questions, 180 minutes
* **Passing score:** Approximately 65% (varies by exam)
* **Registration:** [training.sap.com/certification](https://training.sap.com/certification)
* **Recommended track after this course:** SAP Certified Associate – SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition, Financial Accounting; or SAP Certified Associate – SAP S/4HANA Business Process Integration
* **Note:** SAP certification exams are significantly more expensive than Salesforce; students should complete additional SAP-specific training (openSAP courses, SAP Learning Hub) before attempting the exam
