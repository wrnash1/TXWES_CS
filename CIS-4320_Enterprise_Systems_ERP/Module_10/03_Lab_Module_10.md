## Part B — Complete P2P Accounting Entries (30 points)

### Scenario

Crestline purchases 500 units of titanium alloy bar stock (Material: TIT-BAR-001) from Apex Titanium Corp.

Details:

- Purchase Order: PO-20260615
- PO Price: $240.00 per unit
- PO Quantity: 500 units
- PO Total Value: $120,000
- Delivery Date: June 20, 2026
- Payment Terms: Net 30

Material Master (TIT-BAR-001) Accounting view:

- Price Control: Standard Price (S)
- Standard Price: $240.00 per unit
- Valuation Class: 3000 (Raw Materials)
- G/L Account for Inventory (from Valuation Class 3000): 500100 — Raw Materials Inventory
- GR/IR Clearing Account: 200010

### B-1: Goods Receipt Journal Entry

On June 20, all 500 units are received in good condition. Post the Goods Receipt (MIGO, Movement Type 101).

Construct the complete journal entry:

- List all accounts (by number and name)
- Indicate Debit or Credit for each account
- Show the amount for each line
- Verify the entry balances

### B-2: Invoice Verification Journal Entry (Perfect Match)

On June 25, the vendor invoice arrives for exactly 500 units at $240.00 each — a perfect three-way match. Post the invoice using MIRO.

Construct the complete journal entry:

- List all accounts
- Debit or Credit
- Amounts
- Verify the entry balances
- Identify which open item from the GR is cleared by this posting

### B-3: Invoice Verification Journal Entry (Price Variance)

Assume instead that the vendor invoice from Apex Titanium arrives with a price of $248.00 per unit (not the PO price of $240.00) for all 500 units — a total invoice of $124,000.

Construct the journal entry that MIRO would post:

- The GR/IR account must be debited for the standard price amount ($120,000)
- The vendor must be credited for the actual invoice amount ($124,000)
- The difference ($4,000) must be posted to the Price Difference account (G/L 900100)

Show the complete three-line entry and verify it balances.

### B-4: Goods Issue Journal Entry

On June 30, the production team withdraws 200 units of TIT-BAR-001 from inventory for Production Order PO-6612 (Movement Type 261).

Construct the journal entry:

- G/L Account for Production Order Cost (from Valuation Class 3000): 600100 — Raw Materials Consumption
- The standard price is used for goods issue valuation
- Show account numbers, names, D/C, and amounts

## Part C — Three-Way Match Variance Analysis (25 points)

### C-1: Variance Identification

Crestline's AP team processes the following four invoices in MIRO. For each invoice, determine whether a variance exists, identify the type of variance, and recommend the correct action.

**Invoice 1 — Fastener Corp**

- PO Price: $0.85 per unit, PO Quantity: 10,000 units
- GR Quantity received: 10,000 units
- Vendor Invoice: $8,500 for 10,000 units at $0.85

Variance? Action?

**Invoice 2 — Precision Tooling Ltd**

- PO Price: $4,200 per unit (specialty cutting tool), PO Quantity: 5 units
- GR Quantity received: 5 units
- Vendor Invoice: $4,410 per unit × 5 = $22,050

Variance? Action?

**Invoice 3 — Composite Materials Inc**

- PO Price: $18.50 per sheet, PO Quantity: 1,200 sheets
- GR Quantity received: 950 sheets (250 sheets were back-ordered)
- Vendor Invoice: $22,200 for 1,200 sheets at $18.50

Variance? Action?

**Invoice 4 — Safety Equipment Supply**

- PO Price: $145.00 per unit, PO Quantity: 40 units
- GR Quantity received: 40 units
- Vendor Invoice: $5,945.00 (no unit price breakdown provided)

Variance? Action?

### C-2: Variance Resolution Process

For Invoice 2 (Precision Tooling Ltd), the AP team contacts the vendor and is told that the price increase was due to a raw material surcharge announced after the PO was placed.

In 100–150 words, describe the correct SAP process for resolving this price variance. Should the company pay the higher amount, pay the original PO price, or something else? What SAP document would need to be changed or created to properly authorize and document the price change? Reference at least one SAP MM transaction code in your response.

## Part D — Material Master Configuration Analysis (25 points)

### D-1: Price Control Decision

Crestline is adding two new materials to their Material Master. For each material, recommend the appropriate Price Control setting (Standard Price S or Moving Average Price V) and justify your recommendation.

**Material A:** Aircraft-grade aluminum extrusion (commodity raw material purchased from multiple vendors; market price fluctuates weekly; purchased in large volumes frequently)

**Material B:** Precision-machined titanium landing gear bracket (manufactured in-house from raw materials; cost includes labor, overhead, and materials; Crestline wants to measure manufacturing variances against a planned cost)

For each recommendation: state S or V, explain why that method is appropriate for this material, and describe what happens financially when actual purchase cost differs from the master record price under your recommended method.

### D-2: Valuation Class Impact

Crestline's controller notices that all raw materials purchased last month posted to the same inventory G/L account (500100 — Raw Materials Inventory), even though some materials are aluminum, some are titanium, and some are composite materials with very different cost profiles.

In 100–150 words, explain how the Valuation Class in the Material Master Accounting view controls G/L account determination. If the controller wants separate G/L accounts for Aluminum Raw Materials, Titanium Raw Materials, and Composite Raw Materials — allowing the balance sheet to show each category separately — what configuration change would be required? Would this change require modifying individual Material Master records? Who in the organization would typically maintain this configuration?
