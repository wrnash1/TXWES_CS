## Course: CIS-4320_Enterprise_Systems_ERP (Salesforce Certified Associate / SAP Certified Associate)

### Introduction

Welcome to **Module 03 - ERP Selection & Vendor Landscape**! Choosing the right ERP platform is one of the most consequential technology decisions an organization makes. This module surveys the major ERP vendors, explains the criteria used to evaluate them, and walks through the formal procurement process — from issuing a Request for Proposal (RFP) to calculating Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).

Understanding the vendor landscape is directly relevant to both certifications: SAP exam questions frequently test knowledge of SAP's own product positioning, while the Salesforce Certified Associate exam tests understanding of why businesses choose cloud CRM platforms over legacy on-premise alternatives.

---

### 1. High-Yield Glossary

Review these essential definitions carefully. The certification exam expects you to know these concepts inside and out:

* **ERP vendors (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics)**: The three dominant enterprise software providers. SAP holds the largest share of large-enterprise ERP deployments globally; Oracle offers a competing suite with strong database roots; Microsoft Dynamics 365 targets mid-market organizations and integrates tightly with the Microsoft Office ecosystem.
* **Selection criteria**: The weighted factors an organization uses to compare ERP platforms — including functional fit, implementation cost, vendor support quality, upgrade roadmap, and existing IT infrastructure compatibility. A scoring matrix is typically used to make the comparison objective.
* **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)**: The full financial cost of an ERP system over its useful life, including license fees, implementation services, hardware, internal staff time, training, customization, and ongoing maintenance. TCO comparisons frequently reveal that a lower upfront SaaS subscription costs more or less over 10 years than an on-premise perpetual license.
* **RFP processes**: A Request for Proposal is a formal document an organization issues to ERP vendors, describing its business requirements and requesting detailed pricing, implementation approach, and reference customer information. Vendor responses are scored against the selection criteria matrix to identify a shortlist for demonstrations.

### 2. Certification Exam Tips

* **SAP Certified Associate focus:** Know SAP's key product lines: SAP S/4HANA (next-generation ERP), SAP SuccessFactors (HCM/HR cloud), SAP Ariba (procurement), and SAP Concur (travel and expense). Exam questions often ask which SAP product covers a specific business function.
* **Salesforce Certified Associate focus:** Salesforce is positioned as a CRM, not a traditional back-office ERP. Exam questions may contrast Salesforce's cloud-native, multi-tenant SaaS model against SAP's on-premise and cloud hybrid options — know the difference in deployment model and maintenance responsibility.
* **TCO trap:** Exam scenarios often present a low-cost on-premise option and a higher-subscription SaaS option. Over a 5–10 year horizon, the SaaS TCO is frequently lower when you factor in eliminated hardware, patching, and upgrade labor costs.
* **Study Resource:** Review SAP's own product overview at [SAP Product Catalog](https://www.sap.com/products.html) — a vendor-maintained reference showing the full landscape of SAP solutions and which business problems each addresses.
