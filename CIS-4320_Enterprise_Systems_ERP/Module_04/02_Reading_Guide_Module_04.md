### Introduction

Welcome to **Module 04 - ERP Implementation Lifecycle**! ERP implementations are among the largest and riskiest technology projects an organization undertakes — and understanding why they succeed or fail is a core competency for both the SAP Certified Associate and anyone working with enterprise platforms professionally.

This module covers the structured phases of an ERP project from initial planning through go-live and stabilization. You will learn SAP's Activate methodology, the role of change management in adoption, and the system testing and cutover activities that determine whether go-live succeeds or fails.

---

### 1. High-Yield Glossary

Review these essential definitions carefully. The certification exam expects you to know these concepts inside and out:

* **ERP implementation phases (Planning, Design, Customization, Testing, Go-live)**: The five sequential stages of a typical ERP project. Planning establishes scope, budget, and team. Design maps business processes to system configuration. Customization builds and configures the system. Testing validates that the configured system meets requirements. Go-live transitions production operations to the new system.
* **Design phase**: The phase in which the project team maps the organization's AS-IS business processes against the ERP system's standard capabilities to produce a TO-BE design document. In SAP Activate, this is called the Fit-to-Standard workshop phase.
* **Customization**: Configuration changes and custom code developed to make the ERP system fit specific business requirements not met by standard functionality. Heavy customization is a leading cause of budget overruns and upgrade complexity.
* **Testing**: The validation phase in which the configured system is put through Unit Testing (individual components), Integration Testing (end-to-end process flows), and User Acceptance Testing (UAT, where business users confirm the system meets their needs before go-live).
* **Go-live**: The date on which the production ERP system replaces legacy systems and all live business transactions begin processing in the new platform. Go-live is preceded by a data migration cutover and typically followed by a hypercare stabilization period.
* **Change management**: The organizational discipline of preparing employees to adopt new systems and workflows. Studies consistently identify poor change management — not technical failures — as the primary cause of ERP project failure.

### 2. Certification Exam Tips

* **SAP Activate methodology:** SAP's official implementation framework has six phases: Discover, Prepare, Explore, Realize, Deploy, Run. Know which activities happen in each phase. Fit-to-Standard workshops occur in Explore. System configuration and testing occur in Realize. Go-live occurs in Deploy.
* **Salesforce implementation:** Salesforce uses a similar lifecycle for CRM deployments. Trailhead's Admin certification prep emphasizes sandbox testing, change sets for deployment, and user training as the pillars of a successful rollout.
* **Common exam trap:** Questions may ask which phase identifies gaps between standard ERP functionality and business requirements. This is always the Design/Explore phase — not the Customization/Realize phase (which builds the solution to close those gaps).
* **Change management stat to know:** Research by Prosci and Gartner consistently shows that ERP projects with excellent change management are 6x more likely to meet objectives than projects with poor change management. Exam scenarios testing "why did this ERP fail?" almost always have a change management root cause.
* **Study Resource:** Review the free Salesforce Trailhead module [Salesforce Rollout Strategy](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/salesforce-rollout-strategy) — it teaches deployment lifecycle thinking applicable to any enterprise system go-live.
