## Lab Overview

This lab develops your ability to analyze financial ERP scenarios, map financial transactions to the correct SAP module, trace journal entry flows, and identify when financial controls like the three-way match have been violated. All work is analytical and document-based.

**Estimated Time:** 90 minutes

**Submission:** Upload to Canvas under "Lab 05 — Financial Management Modules."

---

## Learning Objectives

By completing this lab you will be able to:

- Map a described financial event to the correct SAP FI or CO sub-module
- Trace the journal entries generated by a business transaction through the sub-ledger to the General Ledger
- Identify three-way match failures and their correct resolution
- Distinguish FI (external reporting) from CO (internal management accounting) in scenario-based questions
- Describe the month-end close sequence and identify which steps ERP automates

### B-2: Control Effectiveness Analysis

The Controller tells you that before SAP, Crestview's AP team processed approximately 85 vendor invoices per week. ## Part D: Month-End Close Simulation (20 points)

### D-1: Close Sequence Ordering

The Crestview Controller provides you with a list of month-end close activities in random order. Arrange them in the correct sequence and identify which are automated by SAP (A) and which require manual action (M).

Activities (in random order):

- Lock the closed period in SAP
- Post all remaining vendor invoices received before period end
- Run the depreciation calculation for all 47 active fixed assets
- Reconcile the accounts payable sub-ledger balance to the GL control account
- Execute overhead allocations from IT and HR cost centers to production cost centers
- Generate the draft income statement and balance sheet for Controller review
- Make any required manual adjusting journal entries
- Obtain Controller sign-off on final statements

Provide your reordered sequence and A/M classification in a table.

### D-2: Close Time Improvement

Crestview's Controller tells you that before SAP, the month-end close required 12 business days. After 14 months on SAP, the close takes 4 business days. In 100-150 words, explain which specific SAP automations are responsible for this improvement and why they eliminate the time that manual processes required.
