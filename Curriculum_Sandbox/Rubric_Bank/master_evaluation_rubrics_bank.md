## 1. Graduate Discussion & Colloquium Rubric (100 Points)

| Evaluation Criterion | Exemplary (90–100%) | Proficient (80–89%) | Developing (70–79%) | Unacceptable (<70%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Post Rigor & Scholarly Depth (40 pts)** | Demonstrates deep mastery; grounds arguments in empirical evidence, NIST/ACM standards, and peer-reviewed literature. (36–40 pts) | Substantive and technically sound; addresses all prompt questions with minor gaps in depth. (32–35 pts) | Basic analysis; answers prompt at a surface level with minimal scholarly citation. (28–31 pts) | Superficial, incomplete, or fails to address core technical prompt. (0–27 pts) |
| **Peer Dialogue & Critical Synthesis (30 pts)** | Engages with 2+ peers substantively; challenges assumptions, poses relevant questions, and advances discussion. (27–30 pts) | Responds to 2 peers with meaningful technical commentary. (24–26 pts) | Single peer response or responses that merely express agreement without technical critique. (21–23 pts) | No peer engagement or late, trivial replies. (0–20 pts) |
| **Application of Frameworks (20 pts)** | Accurately integrates relevant enterprise frameworks (NIST CSF, AI RMF, TOGAF, ISO 27001). (18–20 pts) | Correctly cites framework elements with minor omissions. (16–17 pts) | Loose reference to frameworks without specific controls. (14–15 pts) | Frameworks omitted or misapplied. (0–13 pts) |
| **Writing & Scholarly Tone (10 pts)** | Flawless technical communication; formal tone; APA 7th edition formatting. (9–10 pts) | Clear technical writing with minor grammatical or citation formatting flaws. (8 pts) | Multiple writing errors or informal style. (7 pts) | Unacceptable syntax, coherence, or citation failure. (0–6 pts) |

## 2. Applied Laboratory & Engineering Implementation Rubric (100 Points)

| Evaluation Criterion | Exemplary (90–100%) | Proficient (80–89%) | Developing (70–79%) | Unacceptable (<70%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Functional Topology & Execution (40 pts)** | Operational prototype/topology; 100% test case pass rate; flawless routing/security configs. (36–40 pts) | System is functional with minor edge-case anomalies. (32–35 pts) | System partially functional; major configurations incomplete or failing tests. (28–31 pts) | Non-functional deployment, syntax crashes, or missing core files. (0–27 pts) |
| **Diagnostic Verification & Evidence (30 pts)** | Exhaustive verification artifacts (annotated pings, traceroutes, Wireshark PCAPs, CLI output logs). (27–30 pts) | Clear verification logs and screenshots covering primary test scenarios. (24–26 pts) | Sparse verification; screenshots lack context or necessary diagnostic output. (21–23 pts) | Verification evidence missing or unreadable. (0–20 pts) |
| **Architectural Trade-off Analysis (20 pts)** | Thorough technical report analyzing latency, overhead, scalability, and security trade-offs. (18–20 pts) | Sound analysis of primary architectural trade-offs. (16–17 pts) | Brief discussion with minimal quantitative analysis. (14–15 pts) | Analysis omitted. (0–13 pts) |
| **Professional Documentation (10 pts)** | Enterprise-grade engineering documentation, topology schemas, and clean formatting. (9–10 pts) | Well-organized lab report with minor formatting inconsistencies. (8 pts) | Disorganized report structure. (7 pts) | Unacceptable documentation. (0–6 pts) |

## 3. Doctoral Applied Project (ADP) Defense & Manuscript Rubric (100 Points)

| Evaluation Criterion | Outstanding (90–100%) | Commendable (80–89%) | Satisfactory (70–79%) | Unsatisfactory (<70%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Problem Definition & Significance (25 pts)** | Solves high-impact organizational problem; demonstrates empirical gap and practical relevance. (23–25 pts) | Clear problem statement with adequate justification. (20–22 pts) | Problem is broad, generic, or insufficiently scoped. (18–19 pts) | Lacks clear problem framing. (0–17 pts) |
| **Methodological Rigor & Data Integrity (25 pts)** | Sound design science, quantitative, or qualitative methodology; robust data ethics and IRB protocols. (23–25 pts) | Solid methodology appropriate to research questions. (20–22 pts) | Methodology lacks detail or exhibits minor validity threats. (18–19 pts) | Flawed or inappropriate methodology. (0–17 pts) |
| **Empirical Results & Analysis (25 pts)** | Rigorous statistical or qualitative analysis; transparent data triangulation and validation. (23–25 pts) | Solid analytical execution with clear interpretation. (20–22 pts) | Analysis is superficial or conclusions exceed empirical data. (18–19 pts) | Analysis is erroneous or absent. (0–17 pts) |
| **Dissemination & Practical Value (25 pts)** | Publishable executive manuscript, clear implementation roadmap, and actionable enterprise artifacts. (23–25 pts) | Valuable insights with clear organizational relevance. (20–22 pts) | Findings have limited direct utility or generalizability. (18–19 pts) | No clear practical contribution. (0–17 pts) |
