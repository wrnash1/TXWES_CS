## 3. Accreditation & Federal Distance Education Alignment
### SACSCOC Regular and Substantive Interaction (RSI - 34 CFR § 600.2)
Distance education at Texas Wesleyan is defined by active, faculty-initiated interaction:
- **Direct Instruction & Synthesis:** Weekly faculty-authored lecture modules, architectural overviews, and recorded synthesis briefs.
- **Assessing & Providing Feedback:** Individualized, qualitative grading feedback posted within 48–72 hours of submission.
- **Scholarly Dialogues:** Faculty-facilitated weekly colloquia and debate threads.
- **Synchronous Availability:** Weekly virtual office hours and scheduled dissertation/project advising sessions.
