## 1. Institutional Attendance & Census Policy Statement
In compliance with Texas Wesleyan University Academic Policies, Title IV Federal Financial Aid regulations, and SACSCOC substantive reporting standards, attendance in online/hybrid computer science courses is defined by documented **academically-related activity**. Academically-related activity includes:
- Submission of an academic assignment, laboratory report, or Packet Tracer topology.
- Taking an interactive quiz or midterm exam.
- Interactive participation in an assigned online discussion forum.

Logging into the LMS without active submission or participation **does not constitute academic attendance**.
