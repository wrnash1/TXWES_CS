**Question 1**
Which type of statistics uses sample data to make predictions or draw conclusions about a larger population?
*   A) Descriptive Statistics
*   B) Inferential Statistics
*   C) Qualitative Statistics
*   D) Mathematical Statistics
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Inferential statistics uses hypothesis tests and samples to infer traits about larger populations.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Inferential statistics uses hypothesis tests and samples to infer traits about larger populations.
    *   Descriptive statistics only summarizes the data at hand.
