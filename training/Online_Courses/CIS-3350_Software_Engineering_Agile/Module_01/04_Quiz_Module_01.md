**Question 1**
Which SDLC model is characterized by linear, sequential phases where each phase must complete before the next begins?

*   **Correct Answer:** B) Waterfall is the classic linear-sequential model with distinct, non-overlapping development phases.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Waterfall is the classic linear-sequential model with distinct, non-overlapping development phases.
    *   Scrum and Kanban are iterative Agile frameworks. Spiral is risk-driven.
