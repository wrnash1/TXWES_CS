# Quiz: Module 15 - DevOps Principles
## Course: CIS-3350_Software_Engineering_Agile (Professional Scrum Master (PSM I))

---

### Question 1
Which core principle emphasizes breaking down silos between software creators and operational system administrators?

*   A) Waterfall
*   B) Systems Analysis
*   C) DevOps
*   D) Strict Isolation

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
DevOps integrates development workflows with systems operations, aligning development speed with server stability.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Waterfall and isolation reinforce team silos rather than resolving them.
