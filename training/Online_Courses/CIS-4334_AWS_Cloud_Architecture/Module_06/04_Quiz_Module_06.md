---

### Question 1
Which S3 storage class offers the lowest retrieval times and cost for archival data accessed once a year?

*   A) S3 Standard
*   B) S3 Standard-IA
*   C) Amazon S3 Glacier Deep Archive
*   D) S3 One Zone-IA

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Glacier Deep Archive is AWS's lowest-cost archival tier, designed for multi-hour retrieval targets.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Standard is for active data. IA is for monthly access.
