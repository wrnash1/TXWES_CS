### Question 1
What core neural network architecture is the foundation for modern Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT?

*   A) Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)
*   B) Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)
*   C) Transformer
*   D) Support Vector Machine (SVM)

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Transformers utilize self-attention mechanisms to process sequence tokens in parallel, enabling training on large datasets.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    CNN is for images. RNN is sequential but superseded by Transformers for LLMs. SVM is a linear model.
