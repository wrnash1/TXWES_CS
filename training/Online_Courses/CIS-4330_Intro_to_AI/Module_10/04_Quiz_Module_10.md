### Question 1
What is the process of breaking down a continuous stream of text into individual words or punctuation marks called?

*   A) Lemmatization
*   B) Tokenization
*   C) Stemming
*   D) Vectorization

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Tokenization splits a string of text into smaller units (tokens) for lexical analysis.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Lemmatization and stemming reduce words to root forms. Vectorization converts tokens to numbers.
