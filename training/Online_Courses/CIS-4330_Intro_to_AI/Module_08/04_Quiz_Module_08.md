### Question 1
What activation function is typically used in the hidden layers of modern neural networks to prevent vanishing gradients?

*   A) Sigmoid
*   B) Rectified Linear Unit (ReLU)
*   C) Tanh
*   D) Step function

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
ReLU (outputting max(0, x)) is widely used in hidden layers because of its computational simplicity and prevention of vanishing gradients.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Sigmoid and Tanh are prone to vanishing gradients in deep networks.
