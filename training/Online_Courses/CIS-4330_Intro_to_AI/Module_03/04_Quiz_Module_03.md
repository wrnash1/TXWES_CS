### Question 1
Which Pandas DataFrame method displays count, mean, standard deviation, and quartile ranges?

*   A) head()
*   B) info()
*   C) describe()
*   D) summary()

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
The `.describe()` method generates descriptive statistics for numerical columns in a DataFrame.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    head() shows rows. info() shows data types. summary() is not a Pandas method.
