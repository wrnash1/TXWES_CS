### Question 1
Which mathematical derivative rule is utilized to compute gradients of nested layers during the backpropagation step?

*   A) Product Rule
*   B) Quotient Rule
*   C) Chain Rule
*   D) Addition Rule

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Backpropagation computes error gradients starting at the output layer and propagating backward using the Chain Rule.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    The chain rule handles derivatives of composed functions.
