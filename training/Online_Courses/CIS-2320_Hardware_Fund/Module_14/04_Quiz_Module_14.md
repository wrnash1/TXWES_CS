### Question 1
Which port is standard for securing incoming IMAP email traffic?

*   A) Port 25
*   B) Port 110
*   C) Port 993
*   D) Port 443

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
IMAP over SSL/TLS uses TCP port 993.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Port 25 is SMTP. Port 110 is unsecure POP3. Port 443 is HTTPS.
