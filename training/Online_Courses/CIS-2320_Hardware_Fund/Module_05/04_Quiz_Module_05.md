---

### Question 1
Which RAID level provides data striping without parity or redundancy?

*   A) RAID 0
*   B) RAID 1
*   C) RAID 5
*   D) RAID 10

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **A**

---

### Explanation
RAID 0 stripes data for performance but offers zero fault tolerance.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    RAID 1 is mirroring. RAID 5 uses parity. RAID 10 is striped mirrors.
