### Question 1
Which category of copper cable supports 10 Gbps speeds at a maximum distance of 100 meters?

*   A) Cat5
*   B) Cat5e
*   C) Cat6
*   D) Cat6a

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **D**

---

### Explanation
Cat6a supports 10 Gbps up to 100m. Cat6 supports 10 Gbps only up to 55m.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Cat5 is 100 Mbps. Cat5e is 1 Gbps.
