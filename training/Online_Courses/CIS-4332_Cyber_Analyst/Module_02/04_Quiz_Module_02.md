# Quiz: Module 02 - Vulnerability Management Lifecycle
## Course: CIS-4332_Cyber_Analyst (CompTIA CySA+)

---

### Question 1
What is the first phase of the vulnerability management lifecycle?

*   A) Remediation
*   B) Prioritization
*   C) Identification
*   D) Verification

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
You must identify vulnerabilities first (via scanning or assessment) before you can analyze or remediate them.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Identification is the initial step.
