### Question 1
Which DNS record type verifies that an email was actually sent by the authorized domain using public key signatures?

*   A) SPF
*   B) DKIM
*   C) DMARC
*   D) MX

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail) signs emails cryptographically, validating the sender domain.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    SPF lists authorized IPs. DMARC aligns SPF/DKIM.
