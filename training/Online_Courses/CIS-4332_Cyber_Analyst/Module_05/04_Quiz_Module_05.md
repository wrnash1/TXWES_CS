### Question 1
Which protocol is the standard carrier for exchanging structured cyber threat intelligence data over HTTP?

*   A) STIX
*   B) TAXII
*   C) JSON-RPC
*   D) Syslog

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
TAXII (Trusted Automated Exchange of Intelligence Information) is the transport mechanism. STIX is the language format.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    STIX defines the data schema, TAXII carries it.
