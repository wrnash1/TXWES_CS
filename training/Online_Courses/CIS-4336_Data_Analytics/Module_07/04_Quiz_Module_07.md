# Quiz: Module 07 - Basic Statistical Concepts
## Course: CIS-4336_Data_Analytics (CompTIA Data+)

---

### Question 1
Which statistical metric represents the middle value of a sorted list of numbers?

*   A) Mean
*   B) Median
*   C) Mode
*   D) Variance

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
The median divides the sorted dataset in half, representing the exact middle value.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Mean is the average. Mode is the most frequent.
