# Quiz: Module 09 - Data Visualization Principles
## Course: CIS-4336_Data_Analytics (CompTIA Data+)

---

### Question 1
Which chart type is best suited for showing the relationship or correlation between two numerical variables?

*   A) Bar Chart
*   B) Line Chart
*   C) Scatter Plot
*   D) Pie Chart

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Scatter plots map two metrics on x and y axes to show distribution correlations.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Line charts show trends over time. Bar charts compare categorical values.
