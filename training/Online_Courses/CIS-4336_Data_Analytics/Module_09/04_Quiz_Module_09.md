**Question 1**
Which chart type is best suited for showing the relationship or correlation between two numerical variables?
*   A) Bar Chart
*   B) Line Chart
*   C) Scatter Plot
*   D) Pie Chart
*   **Correct Answer:** C) Scatter plots map two metrics on x and y axes to show distribution correlations.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Scatter plots map two metrics on x and y axes to show distribution correlations.
    *   Line charts show trends over time. Bar charts compare categorical values.
