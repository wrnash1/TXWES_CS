### Question 1
Which SQL clause is used to filter group results after aggregation has occurred?

*   A) WHERE
*   B) HAVING
*   C) GROUP BY
*   D) SELECT

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
`HAVING` filters aggregated values. `WHERE` filters individual rows before aggregation.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    WHERE filters rows beforehand.
