# Quiz: Module 08 - Descriptive vs Inferential Statistics
## Course: CIS-4336_Data_Analytics (CompTIA Data+)

---

### Question 1
Which type of statistics uses sample data to make predictions or draw conclusions about a larger population?

*   A) Descriptive Statistics
*   B) Inferential Statistics
*   C) Qualitative Statistics
*   D) Mathematical Statistics

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Inferential statistics uses hypothesis tests and samples to infer traits about larger populations.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Descriptive statistics only summarizes the data at hand.
