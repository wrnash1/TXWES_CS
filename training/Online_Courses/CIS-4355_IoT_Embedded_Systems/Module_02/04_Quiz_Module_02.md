### Question 1
How many data wire lines are used in the I2C communication protocol?

*   A) One wire
*   B) Two wires (SDA and SCL)
*   C) Four wires (MISO, MOSI, SCK, CS)
*   D) Eight wires

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
I2C uses a Serial Data (SDA) line and a Serial Clock (SCL) line, supporting multiple master/slave nodes.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    SPI uses four wire lines (MISO, MOSI, SCK, CS).
