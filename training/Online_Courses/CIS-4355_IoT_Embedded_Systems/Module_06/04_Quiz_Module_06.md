### Question 1
Which wireless protocol is best suited for low-power, long-range sensor networks deployed across agricultural fields?

*   A) Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
*   B) LoRaWAN
*   C) Wi-Fi (802.11)
*   D) Zigbee

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
LoRaWAN offers long-range (kilometers) communications at extremely low power rates, sacrificing bandwidth.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    BLE is restricted to short ranges (meters). Wi-Fi consumes too much power.
