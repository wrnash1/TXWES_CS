### Question 1
What is a phishing attack that specifically targets high-profile corporate executives (such as CEOs or CFOs) called?

*   A) Spear-phishing
*   B) Whaling
*   C) Vishing
*   D) Smishing

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Whaling is a sub-class of phishing designed specifically to target high-ranking executive personnel.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Spear-phishing targets any specific individual. Vishing is voice. Smishing is SMS.
