---

### Question 1
Which wireless security standard is vulnerable to offline dictionary attacks on its 4-way handshake?

*   A) WPA3
*   B) WPA2-Personal (PSK)
*   C) WPA2-Enterprise
*   D) WEP

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
WPA2-Personal uses a 4-way handshake that can be captured and brute-forced offline. WPA3 replaces this with SAE.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    WEP uses RC4 cracking methods, not handshake brute-forcing. Enterprise uses RADIUS.
