### Question 1
What is the maximum height of an AVL tree containing N nodes?

*   A) O(1)
*   B) O(log N)
*   C) O(N)
*   D) O(N^2)

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
AVL trees guarantee a logarithmic height by maintaining a strict balance factor difference of at most 1.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    O(N) is the height of an unbalanced degenerate tree (linked list).
