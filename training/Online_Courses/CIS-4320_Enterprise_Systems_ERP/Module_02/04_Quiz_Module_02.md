### Question 1
In BPMN 2.0, what element is used to categorize activities based on which department or role performs them?

*   A) Task box
*   B) Gateway diamond
*   C) Swimlane (Pool/Lane)
*   D) Event circle

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Swimlanes separate tasks visually, assigning operational ownership to specific departments or users.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Gateways direct logical splits in process routing.
