### Question 1
Which programming language is proprietary to SAP and used to develop custom reports and database integrations?

*   A) Python
*   B) ABAP
*   C) Apex
*   D) SQL Server

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
ABAP (Advanced Business Application Programming) is SAP's primary custom programming language.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Apex is used for customizing Salesforce cloud platforms.
