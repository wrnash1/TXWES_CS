### Question 1
What does the 'A' stand for in a RACI assignment matrix?

*   A) Assigned
*   B) Accountable
*   C) Authorized
*   D) Approved

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Accountable represents the single person answerable for the correct completion of the task (only one 'A' per task).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Responsible executes the work. Accountable owns the outcome.
