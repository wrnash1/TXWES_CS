### Question 1
What is the main focus of Quality Assurance (QA) compared to Quality Control (QC)?

*   A) QA focuses on preventing defects in processes, while QC focuses on identifying defects in final products
*   B) QA runs unit tests
*   C) QC manages project budgets
*   D) QA is done only by project managers

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **A**

---

### Explanation
QA is process-oriented (proactive prevention). QC is product-oriented (reactive inspection of deliverables).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Both teams write tests, but their scope targets process vs product.
