# Quiz: Module 01 - IT Project Framework
## Course: CIS-3310_IT_Project_Management (CompTIA Project+ / PMI CAPM)

---

### Question 1
What are the three pillars of the Project Management Triple Constraint?

*   A) Scope, Time, Cost
*   B) Quality, Speed, Safety
*   C) Staff, Hardware, Software
*   D) Planning, Execution, Closure

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **A**

---

### Explanation
Any change to scope, schedule (time), or budget (cost) impacts the other variables and overall quality.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Staff and planning are resources and phases, not core constraints.
