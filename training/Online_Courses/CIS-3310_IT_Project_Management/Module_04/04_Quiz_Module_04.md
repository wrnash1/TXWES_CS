### Question 1
Which dependency type describes a scenario where Task B cannot start until Task A has completed?

*   A) Start-to-Start (SS)
*   B) Finish-to-Start (FS)
*   C) Finish-to-Finish (FF)
*   D) Start-to-Finish (SF)

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Finish-to-Start is the most common scheduling linkage; preceding activity must end before successor begins.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Start-to-Start requires both tasks to begin concurrently.
