# Quiz: Module 10 - Risk Identification & Management
## Course: CIS-3310_IT_Project_Management (CompTIA Project+ / PMI CAPM)

---

### Question 1
Which risk response strategy involves buying an insurance policy or outsourcing a database migration task to a vendor?

*   A) Avoid
*   B) Transfer
*   C) Mitigate
*   D) Accept

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Transferring shifts the financial ownership or operational threat to a third party (e.g. hosting provider or insurer).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Mitigation reduces probability or impact directly.
