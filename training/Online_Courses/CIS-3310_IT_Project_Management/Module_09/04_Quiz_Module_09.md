### Question 1
How many communication channels exist in a project team containing 8 members?

*   A) 8
*   B) 16
*   C) 28
*   D) 56

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Using the channel formula: 8 * (8 - 1) / 2 = 8 * 7 / 2 = 56 / 2 = 28 channels.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    The formula tracks unique connections between all members.
