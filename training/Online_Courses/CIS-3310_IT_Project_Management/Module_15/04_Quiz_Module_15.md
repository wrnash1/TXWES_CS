### Question 1
What is the primary purpose of conducting a Lessons Learned session during project closure?

*   A) To assign blame for failures
*   B) To identify successes and failures to improve future organizational projects
*   C) To calculate final employee bonuses
*   D) To archive server hardware logs

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Lessons learned capture historical insights to ensure subsequent projects avoid similar pitfalls.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Lessons learned focus on process optimization, not assigning blame.
