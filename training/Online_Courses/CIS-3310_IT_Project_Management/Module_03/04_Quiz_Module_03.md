### Question 1
What is the lowest level of decomposition in a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) called?

*   A) Sub-project
*   B) Task group
*   C) Work Package
*   D) Milestone

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Work packages are the granular units at the bottom of the WBS tree, where costs and schedules can be estimated.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Milestones are points in time with zero duration.
