**Question 1**
Which contract type carries the highest risk for the buyer but low risk for the seller?
*   A) Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP)
*   B) Cost-Reimbursable (CR)
*   C) Time and Materials (T&M)
*   D) Fixed-Price-Incentive-Fee (FPIF)
*   **Correct Answer:** B) In cost-reimbursable contracts, the buyer pays all actual costs plus a fee, meaning cost overruns are paid by the buyer.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* In cost-reimbursable contracts, the buyer pays all actual costs plus a fee, meaning cost overruns are paid by the buyer.
    *   Fixed-price shifts cost overrun risk onto the seller.
