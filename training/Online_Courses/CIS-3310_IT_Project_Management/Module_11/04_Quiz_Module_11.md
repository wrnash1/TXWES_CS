### Question 1
Which contract type carries the highest risk for the buyer but low risk for the seller?

*   A) Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP)
*   B) Cost-Reimbursable (CR)
*   C) Time and Materials (T&M)
*   D) Fixed-Price-Incentive-Fee (FPIF)

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
In cost-reimbursable contracts, the buyer pays all actual costs plus a fee, meaning cost overruns are paid by the buyer.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Fixed-price shifts cost overrun risk onto the seller.
