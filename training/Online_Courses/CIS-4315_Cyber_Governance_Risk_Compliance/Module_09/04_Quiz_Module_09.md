# Quiz: Module 09 - Regulatory Compliance (HIPAA, SOX)
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

---

### Question 1
Which regulatory law mandates strict electronic security and privacy controls to protect patient health records?

*   A) SOX
*   B) HIPAA
*   C) GLBA
*   D) FISMA

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
HIPAA enforces security controls surrounding protected health information (PHI).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    SOX targets financial audit accuracy in public corporations.
