# Quiz: Module 06 - Risk Mitigation Strategies
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

---

### Question 1
Which risk treatment option involves completely eliminating the threat by stopping the business activity associated with the risk?

*   A) Mitigation
*   B) Avoidance
*   C) Acceptance
*   D) Transfer

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Risk avoidance stops the activity (e.g. disabling external website features to prevent SQL attacks completely).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Mitigation implements controls (e.g. firewalls) to reduce risk while keeping the activity active.
