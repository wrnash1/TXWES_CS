**Question 1**
Which risk treatment option involves completely eliminating the threat by stopping the business activity associated with the risk?
*   A) Mitigation
*   B) Avoidance
*   C) Acceptance
*   D) Transfer
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Risk avoidance stops the activity (e.g. disabling external website features to prevent SQL attacks completely).
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Risk avoidance stops the activity (e.g. disabling external website features to prevent SQL attacks completely).
    *   Mitigation implements controls (e.g. firewalls) to reduce risk while keeping the activity active.
