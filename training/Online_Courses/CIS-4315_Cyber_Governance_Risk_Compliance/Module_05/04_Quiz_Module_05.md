### Question 1
An asset worth $100,000 has an exposure factor of 40% if a server room flood occurs. The flood risk occurs once every 5 years. What is the ALE?

*   A) $40,000
*   B) $200,000
*   C) $8,000
*   D) $20,000

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
SLE = $100,000 * 0.40 = $40,000. ARO = 1/5 = 0.2. ALE = SLE * ARO = $40,000 * 0.2 = $8,000.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    40,000 is the SLE. 8,000 is the annualized expected loss.
