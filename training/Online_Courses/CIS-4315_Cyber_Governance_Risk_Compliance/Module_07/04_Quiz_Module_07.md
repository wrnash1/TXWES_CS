**Question 1**
Which metric defines the maximum acceptable age of data that must be recovered from backup storage after a system failure?
*   A) Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
*   B) Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
*   C) Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD)
*   D) Mean Time to Repair (MTTR)
*   **Correct Answer:** B) RPO measures data loss limits (e.g. RPO of 4 hours means backups must run at least every 4 hours).
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* RPO measures data loss limits (e.g. RPO of 4 hours means backups must run at least every 4 hours).
    *   RTO measures recovery duration (how long systems can remain offline).
