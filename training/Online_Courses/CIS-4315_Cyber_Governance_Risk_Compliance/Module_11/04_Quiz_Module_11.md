# Quiz: Module 11 - Industry Standards (PCI-DSS, ISO)
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

---

### Question 1
Which standard is mandatory for any organization processing, storing, or transmitting credit card information?

*   A) ISO 27001
*   B) PCI-DSS
*   C) NIST 800-53
*   D) SOC 2

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
PCI-DSS is established by major card brands to secure cardholder data environments.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    ISO 27001 is a voluntary international security framework.
