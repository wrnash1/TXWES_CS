# Quiz: Module 08 - Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

---

### Question 1
Which recovery site type is fully operational, contains real-time mirrored datasets, and can take over production workflows within minutes?

*   A) Cold Site
*   B) Warm Site
*   C) Hot Site
*   D) Mirror Store

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Hot sites are equipped with matching hardware, power, and synchronized datasets for rapid failovers.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Cold sites have floor space and power but no hardware or data backups pre-loaded.
