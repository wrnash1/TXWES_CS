**Question 1**
Which recovery site type is fully operational, contains real-time mirrored datasets, and can take over production workflows within minutes?
*   A) Cold Site
*   B) Warm Site
*   C) Hot Site
*   D) Mirror Store
*   **Correct Answer:** C) Hot sites are equipped with matching hardware, power, and synchronized datasets for rapid failovers.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Hot sites are equipped with matching hardware, power, and synchronized datasets for rapid failovers.
    *   Cold sites have floor space and power but no hardware or data backups pre-loaded.
