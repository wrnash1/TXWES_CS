# Quiz: Module 01 - Security Governance Frameworks
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

---

### Question 1
What is the primary objective of Information Security Governance?

*   A) Installing host antiviruses
*   B) Aligning the information security strategy with overall business objectives and goals
*   C) Blocking internet traffic
*   D) Encrypting database backups

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Governance ensures security operations support business goals, manage risks, and conform to corporate policies.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Antivirus installations and network blocks are technical operations, not governance planning.
