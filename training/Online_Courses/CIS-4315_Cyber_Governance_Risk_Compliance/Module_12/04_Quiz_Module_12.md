**Question 1**
What is the primary purpose of an IT security audit?
*   A) To write clean code
*   B) To evaluate system operations and verify controls align with regulatory policies and design objectives

*   D) To purchase firewalls
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Auditors independently verify that documented security policies and control systems are actually operating as intended.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Auditors independently verify that documented security policies and control systems are actually operating as intended.
