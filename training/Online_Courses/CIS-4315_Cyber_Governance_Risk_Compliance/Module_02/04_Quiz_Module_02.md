# Quiz: Module 02 - Security Policies & Standards
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

---

### Question 1
Which document type contains mandatory, baseline rules specifying hardware and software requirements across the organization?

*   A) Policy
*   B) Standard
*   C) Guideline
*   D) Procedure

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Standards are compulsory specifications. Policies are high-level goal definitions. Guidelines are recommended options.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Guidelines are non-mandatory suggestions.
