**Question 1**
Which security measure is most effective at reducing the risk of successful phishing attacks against employees?
*   A) Implementing longer passwords
*   B) Continuous security awareness training and phishing simulations
*   C) Turning off email servers
*   D) Changing employee usernames
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Simulated training educates employees to spot warning signs (mismatched domains, urgent requests) before clicking links.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Simulated training educates employees to spot warning signs (mismatched domains, urgent requests) before clicking links.
    *   Lengthy passwords do not stop users from typing credentials into fake sites.
