# Quiz: Module 14 - Security Awareness Programs
## Course: CIS-4315_Cyber_Governance_Risk_Compliance (ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM))

---

### Question 1
Which security measure is most effective at reducing the risk of successful phishing attacks against employees?

*   A) Implementing longer passwords
*   B) Continuous security awareness training and phishing simulations
*   C) Turning off email servers
*   D) Changing employee usernames

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Simulated training educates employees to spot warning signs (mismatched domains, urgent requests) before clicking links.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Lengthy passwords do not stop users from typing credentials into fake sites.
