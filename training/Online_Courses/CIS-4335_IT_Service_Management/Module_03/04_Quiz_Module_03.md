# Quiz: Module 03 - Service Offerings and Relationships
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
What joint activity is performed by service providers and consumers to ensure continual value co-creation?

*   A) Service Provision
*   B) Service Consumption
*   C) Service Relationship Management
*   D) Service Level Agreement creation

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Service Relationship Management coordinates provider and consumer interactions to facilitate value.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Provision is strictly provider. Consumption is strictly consumer.
