**Question 1**
What joint activity is performed by service providers and consumers to ensure continual value co-creation?
*   A) Service Provision
*   B) Service Consumption
*   C) Service Relationship Management
*   D) Service Level Agreement creation
*   **Correct Answer:** C) Service Relationship Management coordinates provider and consumer interactions to facilitate value.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Service Relationship Management coordinates provider and consumer interactions to facilitate value.
