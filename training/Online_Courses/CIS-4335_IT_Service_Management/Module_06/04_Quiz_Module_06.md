# Quiz: Module 06 - Service Value Chain Activities
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
Which value chain activity ensures that service components are available when and where they are needed?

*   A) Design and Transition
*   B) Obtain/Build
*   C) Deliver and Support
*   D) Plan

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Obtain/Build focuses on acquiring or building components, code, and resources.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Design and Transition handles implementation. Deliver and Support handles operation.
