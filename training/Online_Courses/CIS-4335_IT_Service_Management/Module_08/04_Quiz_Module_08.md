# Quiz: Module 08 - Practices: Continual Improvement
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
What database or tool is used to track and prioritize improvement ideas from across an organization?

*   A) Known Error Database
*   B) Service Level Agreement
*   C) Continual Improvement Register (CIR)
*   D) CMDB

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
The CIR records, tracks, and prioritizes improvement opportunities.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Known Error Database is for problems. CMDB holds configuration items.
