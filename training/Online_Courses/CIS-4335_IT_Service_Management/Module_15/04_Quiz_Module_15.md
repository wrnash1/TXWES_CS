## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
What is the correct definition of a service?

*   A) A configuration item that runs code
*   B) A means of enabling value co-creation by facilitating outcomes customers want to achieve without the customer having to manage specific costs and risks
*   C) A set of resources deployed in a cloud network
*   D) A contract between a buyer and seller

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
This is Axelos' official definition of a service under ITIL 4.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    The other options represent components or contracts, not the ITIL definition.
