**Question 1**
Which guiding principle recommends using existing services and processes as a baseline rather than discarding them entirely?
*   A) Focus on Value
*   B) Start Where You Are
*   C) Keep It Simple and Practical
*   D) Optimize and Automate
*   **Correct Answer:** B) 'Start Where You Are' advises assessing current states for reusable elements before building from scratch.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* 'Start Where You Are' advises assessing current states for reusable elements before building from scratch.
    *   Keep It Simple focuses on avoiding complexity.
