# Quiz: Module 07 - The ITIL Guiding Principles
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
Which guiding principle recommends using existing services and processes as a baseline rather than discarding them entirely?

*   A) Focus on Value
*   B) Start Where You Are
*   C) Keep It Simple and Practical
*   D) Optimize and Automate

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
'Start Where You Are' advises assessing current states for reusable elements before building from scratch.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Keep It Simple focuses on avoiding complexity.
