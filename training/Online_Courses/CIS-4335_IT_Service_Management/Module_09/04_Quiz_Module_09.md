# Quiz: Module 09 - Practices: Change Enablement
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
Which type of change is low-risk, pre-authorized, and can be implemented without additional review?

*   A) Normal Change
*   B) Standard Change
*   C) Emergency Change
*   D) Routine Change

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Standard changes are low-risk, repeat procedures that are pre-authorized by management.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Normal changes require authorization cycles. Emergency changes are expedited.
