**Question 1**
Which type of change is low-risk, pre-authorized, and can be implemented without additional review?
*   A) Normal Change
*   B) Standard Change
*   C) Emergency Change
*   D) Routine Change
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Standard changes are low-risk, repeat procedures that are pre-authorized by management.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Standard changes are low-risk, repeat procedures that are pre-authorized by management.
    *   Normal changes require authorization cycles. Emergency changes are expedited.
