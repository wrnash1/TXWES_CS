# Quiz: Module 11 - Practices: Service Desk & Request Management
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
Which of the following is classified as a Service Request rather than an Incident?

*   A) A printer is printing blank pages
*   B) A user forgot their password and needs a reset
*   C) The database server crashed
*   D) A critical application is throwing errors

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Password resets are routine service requests (requests for access/information). The others represent service disruptions (incidents).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Disruptions are incidents. Normal requests are service requests.
