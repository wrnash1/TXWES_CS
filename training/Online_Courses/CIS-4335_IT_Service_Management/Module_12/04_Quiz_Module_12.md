**Question 1**
What is a document that defines agreed-upon service targets between an IT provider and its customer called?
*   A) Service Level Agreement (SLA)
*   B) Service Catalogue
*   C) Service Design Package
*   D) Operational Level Agreement
*   **Correct Answer:** A) The SLA details targets (e.g. 99.9% availability) agreed between provider and client.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* The SLA details targets (e.g. 99.9% availability) agreed between provider and client.
    *   OLAs are agreements between internal departments.
