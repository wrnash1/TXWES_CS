# Quiz: Module 12 - Practices: Service Level Management
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
What is a document that defines agreed-upon service targets between an IT provider and its customer called?

*   A) Service Level Agreement (SLA)
*   B) Service Catalogue
*   C) Service Design Package
*   D) Operational Level Agreement

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **A**

---

### Explanation
The SLA details targets (e.g. 99.9% availability) agreed between provider and client.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    OLAs are agreements between internal departments.
