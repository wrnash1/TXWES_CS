# Quiz: Module 10 - Practices: Incident & Problem Management
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
What is the primary difference between an incident and a problem in ITIL?

*   A) Incidents are larger than problems
*   B) Incidents focus on restoring service; problems focus on identifying root causes
*   C) Problems are reported by users; incidents by software
*   D) None of the above

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Incident management restores service quickly. Problem management investigates the underlying causes of incidents.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    A and C are common misconceptions.
