# Quiz: Module 13 - Practices: Security & Relationships
## Course: CIS-4335_IT_Service_Management (ITIL 4 Foundation)

---

### Question 1
Which practice establishes and nurtures links between the organization and its stakeholders at strategic levels?

*   A) Service Desk
*   B) Relationship Management
*   C) Service Level Management
*   D) Supplier Management

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Relationship Management identifies and maintains partnerships with clients and business units.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Supplier management deals with vendors. Service desk deals with operations.
