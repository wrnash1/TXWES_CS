### Question 1
What state does a JavaScript Promise enter once it has completed successfully?

*   A) Pending
*   B) Fulfilled
*   C) Rejected
*   D) Resolved

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Promises transition from Pending to either Fulfilled (resolved successfully) or Rejected (errored out).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Resolved is the general term for completion, but the explicit state is Fulfilled.
