### Question 1
Which HTML5 tag is considered a semantic element?

*   A) <div>
*   B) <span>
*   C) <article>
*   D) <b>

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
<article> has semantic meaning, telling the browser and search engines about the nature of the enclosed text content.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    div and span are generic container tags with no semantic value.
