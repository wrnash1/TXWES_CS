### Question 1
Which conversion strategy is the lowest risk because both the old and new systems are run simultaneously for a period of time?

*   A) Direct Cutover
*   B) Parallel Conversion
*   C) Phased Conversion
*   D) Pilot Conversion

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Parallel conversion allows verification of new outputs against the old system, fallback is immediate if failures occur.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Direct cutover drops the old system immediately, presenting high risk.
