### Question 1
Which type of software maintenance involves modifying a working system to support new operating system updates or server migrations?

*   A) Corrective Maintenance
*   B) Adaptive Maintenance
*   C) Perfective Maintenance
*   D) Preventive Maintenance

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Adaptive maintenance alters software to operate in changed hardware or software environments.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Corrective maintenance fixes bugs. Perfective adds user-requested features.
