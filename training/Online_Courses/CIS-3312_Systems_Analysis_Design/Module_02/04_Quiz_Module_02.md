# Quiz: Module 02 - Feasibility Analysis
## Course: CIS-3312_Systems_Analysis_Design (IIBA Entry Certificate in Business Analysis (ECBA))

---

### Question 1
Which feasibility aspect determines if the organization has the programming and infrastructure capability to build the proposed system?

*   A) Economic Feasibility
*   B) Operational Feasibility
*   C) Technical Feasibility
*   D) Schedule Feasibility

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Technical feasibility evaluates hardware, software, and development team capability limitations.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    Economic feasibility focuses on project costs and financial payback metrics.
