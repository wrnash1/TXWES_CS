### Question 1
In a Use Case diagram, which relationship is used when a use case requires the mandatory execution of another use case?

*   A) <<extend>>
*   B) <<include>>
*   C) <<generalize>>
*   D) <<dependency>>

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
The <<include>> relationship indicates that the base use case incorporates the behavior of the target use case as a mandatory step.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    <<extend>> marks optional behaviors triggered only under specific conditions.
