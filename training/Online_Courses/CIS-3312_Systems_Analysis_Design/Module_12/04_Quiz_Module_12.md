### Question 1
What software architecture relationship is preferred in modular system design?

*   A) High Coupling and High Cohesion
*   B) Low Coupling and Low Cohesion
*   C) Low Coupling and High Cohesion
*   D) High Coupling and Low Cohesion

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **C**

---

### Explanation
Modular design aims for high cohesion (modules perform single tasks) and low coupling (modules are independent).

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    High coupling creates tight dependencies, making system changes difficult.
