# Quiz: Module 01 - Role of System Analyst & SDLC
## Course: CIS-3312_Systems_Analysis_Design (IIBA Entry Certificate in Business Analysis (ECBA))

---

### Question 1
What is the primary responsibility of a Systems Analyst?

*   A) Writing compiled server assembly code
*   B) Analyzing business requirements and designing information systems solutions to bridge business and IT
*   C) Selling software licenses
*   D) Configuring firewall ports

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
Analysts serve as the interface, translating business needs into detailed technical specifications for programmers.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    They focus on analysis and design, not raw code creation or network security configurations.
