**Question 1**
What is the primary responsibility of a Systems Analyst?
*   A) Writing compiled server assembly code
*   B) Analyzing business requirements and designing information systems solutions to bridge business and IT
*   C) Selling software licenses
*   D) Configuring firewall ports
*   **Correct Answer:** B) Analysts serve as the interface, translating business needs into detailed technical specifications for programmers.
*   **Distractor Analysis:**
    *   *Why correct:* Analysts serve as the interface, translating business needs into detailed technical specifications for programmers.
    *   They focus on analysis and design, not raw code creation or network security configurations.
