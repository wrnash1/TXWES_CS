## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Create VLAN 10 and 20: `vlan 10`**
   * *Instruction:* Execute this step inside your terminal environment. Verify the command completes without errors.
2. **Assign ports to VLAN: `switchport access vlan 10`**
   * *Instruction:* Execute this step inside your terminal environment. Verify the command completes without errors.
3. **Configure trunk link: `switchport mode trunk`**
   * *Instruction:* Execute this step inside your terminal environment. Verify the command completes without errors.
