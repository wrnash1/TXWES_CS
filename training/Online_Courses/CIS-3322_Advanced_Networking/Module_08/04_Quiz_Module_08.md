### Question 1
What wildcard mask corresponds to a standard subnet mask of `255.255.255.252`?

*   A) 0.0.0.3
*   B) 0.0.0.255
*   C) 255.255.255.255
*   D) 0.0.0.7

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **A**

---

### Explanation
Subtracting `255.255.255.252` from `255.255.255.255` yields wildcard `0.0.0.3`.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    0.0.0.255 is for /24. 0.0.0.7 is for /29.
