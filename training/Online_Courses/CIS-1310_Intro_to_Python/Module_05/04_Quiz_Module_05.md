### Question 1
What does the `break` statement do inside a loop?

*   A) Skips the current iteration
*   B) Terminates the loop immediately
*   C) Restarts the loop
*   D) Exits the entire program

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
The `break` statement exits the innermost loop immediately, bypassing any remaining iterations.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    A describes the `continue` statement. D describes `exit()` or `sys.exit()`.
