### Question 1
What is the result of `print(11 // 3)` in Python?

*   A) 3.666
*   B) 3
*   C) 2
*   D) 4

---

### Answer Key
*   **Correct Option:** **B**

---

### Explanation
The double slash `//` operator performs floor division, rounding down to the nearest integer.

---

### Distractor Analysis
*   **Why the incorrect options are wrong:**
    A is the result of single slash `/`. C is the remainder from modulo `%`.
