# CSC-6365: Advanced Database Systems and Data Architectures
**Texas Wesleyan University — School of Business & Computing**  
**Department of Computer Science & Information Technology**  
**Graduate Level:** Master of Science in Computer Science (MCS)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Semester Credit Hours (SCH)  
**Format:** 100% Online (7-Week Accelerated Term)  

---

## 1. Course Description
Theoretical and applied exploration of modern database management systems, query optimization, concurrency control, distributed transactions (2PC, Saga), NoSQL/NewSQL paradigms, and big data lakehouse architectures. This course is engineered to meet SACSCOC Regular and Substantive Interaction (RSI - 34 CFR § 600.2) and Quality Matters (QM) standards.

---

## 2. Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
Upon successful completion, students will be able to:
1. **Analyze and Evaluate** complex computational architectures using rigorous mathematical and algorithmic models.
2. **Formulate and Implement** scalable, fault-tolerant system solutions adhering to modern industry standards.
3. **Appraise and Justify** architectural trade-offs across consistency, availability, performance, and durability.
4. **Author and Defend** graduate-level technical research papers contributing to the computing literature.

## 4. 7-Week Accelerated Schedule
- **Week 1 (Module 01):** Database Engine Internals & Storage Hierarchy — *Focus: B+ Trees, LSM-Trees (Log-Structured Merge), Page Buffering, and Storage Engine Optimization*
- **Week 2 (Module 02):** Advanced Query Execution & Cost-Based Optimization — *Focus: Relational Algebra Optimization, Physical Plan Generation, Join Algorithms (Hash, Merge, Index-Nested)*
- **Week 3 (Module 03):** Transaction Management, Concurrency & Recovery — *Focus: ACID Semantics, Two-Phase Locking (2PL), MVCC (Multi-Version Concurrency Control), WAL & ARIES Recovery*
- **Week 4 (Module 04):** Distributed Transactions and Consistency Models — *Focus: Two-Phase Commit (2PC), Three-Phase Commit, Saga Pattern, Distributed Deadlock Detection*
- **Week 5 (Module 05):** NoSQL Paradigms & Specialized Data Stores — *Focus: Document (MongoDB), Key-Value (Redis), Wide-Column (Cassandra), Graph Databases (Neo4j)*
- **Week 6 (Module 06):** Modern Analytical Architectures & Data Lakehouses — *Focus: OLAP vs. OLTP, Columnar Storage (Parquet), Snowflake, Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg, and Lakehouse Patterns*
- **Week 7 (Module 07):** Data Privacy, Governance, and Capstone Project — *Focus: Database Security, RBAC, Data Masking, Auditing, Enterprise Data Architecture Capstone Project*
