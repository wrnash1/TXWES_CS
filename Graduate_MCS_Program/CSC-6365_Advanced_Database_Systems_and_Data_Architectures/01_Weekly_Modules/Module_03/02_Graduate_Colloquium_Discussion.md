# Module 03 Graduate Colloquium: Transaction Management, Concurrency & Recovery
**Course:** CSC-6365 | Texas Wesleyan University

### Colloquium Prompt
Critically analyze the trade-offs inherent in ACID Semantics, Two-Phase Locking (2PL), MVCC (Multi-Version Concurrency Control), WAL & ARIES Recovery. Consider an enterprise operating under strict latency SLA and five-nines (99.999%) availability mandates.
1. Formulate a technical design proposal defending your chosen architectural pattern.
2. Evaluate potential edge cases and failure domains.
3. Critique the proposals of at least two peers using empirical performance arguments.
