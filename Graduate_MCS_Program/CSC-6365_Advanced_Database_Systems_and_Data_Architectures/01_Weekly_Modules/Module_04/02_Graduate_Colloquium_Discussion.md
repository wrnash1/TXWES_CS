# Module 04 Graduate Colloquium: Distributed Transactions and Consistency Models
**Course:** CSC-6365 | Texas Wesleyan University

### Colloquium Prompt
Critically analyze the trade-offs inherent in Two-Phase Commit (2PC), Three-Phase Commit, Saga Pattern, Distributed Deadlock Detection. Consider an enterprise operating under strict latency SLA and five-nines (99.999%) availability mandates.
1. Formulate a technical design proposal defending your chosen architectural pattern.
2. Evaluate potential edge cases and failure domains.
3. Critique the proposals of at least two peers using empirical performance arguments.
