# CSC-6362: Distributed Systems and Cloud Middleware
**Texas Wesleyan University — School of Business & Computing**  
**Department of Computer Science & Information Technology**  
**Graduate Level:** Master of Science in Computer Science (MCS)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Semester Credit Hours (SCH)  
**Format:** 100% Online (7-Week Accelerated Term)  

---

## 1. Course Description
Advanced study of distributed algorithms, consensus protocols (Raft, Paxos), cloud middleware, microservices architecture, fault tolerance, RPC/gRPC, and distributed storage systems. This course is engineered to meet SACSCOC Regular and Substantive Interaction (RSI - 34 CFR § 600.2) and Quality Matters (QM) standards.

---

## 2. Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
Upon successful completion, students will be able to:
1. **Analyze and Evaluate** complex computational architectures using rigorous mathematical and algorithmic models.
2. **Formulate and Implement** scalable, fault-tolerant system solutions adhering to modern industry standards.
3. **Appraise and Justify** architectural trade-offs across consistency, availability, performance, and durability.
4. **Author and Defend** graduate-level technical research papers contributing to the computing literature.

## 4. 7-Week Accelerated Schedule
- **Week 1 (Module 01):** Distributed Systems Foundations & Architectural Models — *Focus: RPC, gRPC, Client-Server vs. Peer-to-Peer, Microservices, and Event-Driven Architectures*
- **Week 2 (Module 02):** Time, Clocks, and Global State Synchronization — *Focus: Physical vs. Logical Clocks, Lamport Timestamps, Vector Clocks, and Chandy-Lamport Snapshot Algorithm*
- **Week 3 (Module 03):** Distributed Consensus and Coordination — *Focus: State Machine Replication, Paxos, Raft Consensus, Split-Brain Mitigation, and Quorum Systems*
- **Week 4 (Module 04):** Fault Tolerance, Replication, and Consistency — *Focus: Crash vs. Byzantine Failures, CAP Theorem, PACELC, Strong vs. Eventual Consistency, and CRDTs*
- **Week 5 (Module 05):** Distributed Storage and Data Engines — *Focus: Distributed Hash Tables (DHT), Consistent Hashing, Google Bigtable, Spanner, and DynamoDB Architecture*
- **Week 6 (Module 06):** Cloud Middleware, Containers, and Service Meshes — *Focus: Container Orchestration (Kubernetes), Service Mesh (Istio), Envoy Proxy, Distributed Tracing (OpenTelemetry)*
- **Week 7 (Module 07):** Emerging Trends: Edge Computing, Serverless, and Capstone — *Focus: FaaS/Serverless Architectures, Edge Cloud Systems, Fault-Resilient Distributed Architecture Capstone*
