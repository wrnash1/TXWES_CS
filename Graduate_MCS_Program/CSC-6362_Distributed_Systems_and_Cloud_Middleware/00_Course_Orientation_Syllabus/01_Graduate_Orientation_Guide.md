# CSC-6362 Graduate Student Orientation Guide
Welcome to CSC-6362 in the Texas Wesleyan Master of Science in Computer Science (MCS) program.

### SACSCOC RSI Expectations
Distance education at Texas Wesleyan requires active, faculty-guided interaction. In this course:
- Initial colloquium responses are due every Thursday by 11:59 PM CT.
- Two substantive peer critiques are due Sunday by 11:59 PM CT.
- Weekly office hours and personalized code reviews are provided by the instructor.
