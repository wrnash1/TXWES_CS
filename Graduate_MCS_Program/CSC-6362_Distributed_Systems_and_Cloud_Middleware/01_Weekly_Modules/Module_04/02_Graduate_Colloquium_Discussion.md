# Module 04 Graduate Colloquium: Fault Tolerance, Replication, and Consistency
**Course:** CSC-6362 | Texas Wesleyan University

### Colloquium Prompt
Critically analyze the trade-offs inherent in Crash vs. Byzantine Failures, CAP Theorem, PACELC, Strong vs. Eventual Consistency, and CRDTs. Consider an enterprise operating under strict latency SLA and five-nines (99.999%) availability mandates.
1. Formulate a technical design proposal defending your chosen architectural pattern.
2. Evaluate potential edge cases and failure domains.
3. Critique the proposals of at least two peers using empirical performance arguments.
