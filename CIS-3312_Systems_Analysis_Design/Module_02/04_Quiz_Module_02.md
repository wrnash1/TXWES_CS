## Question 1

Which BABOK Guide v3 Knowledge Area is specifically responsible for defining how business analysis activities will be conducted on a project before elicitation begins?

A) Requirements Life Cycle Management

B) Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring

C) Strategy Analysis

D) Solution Evaluation

Correct Answer: B

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Requirements Life Cycle Management (KA 6) covers how requirements are traced, maintained, and approved — it is not the planning knowledge area.
- Why C is incorrect: Strategy Analysis (KA 3) focuses on understanding the current state and defining the future state and solution scope.
- Why D is incorrect: Solution Evaluation (KA 7) focuses on assessing the value delivered by an implemented solution.
- Why B is correct: BABOK KA 2 defines the tasks for planning the BA approach, stakeholder engagement, governance, and information management before other BA work begins.

## Question 2

In the context of business analysis planning, which of the following is the most accurate definition of a stakeholder engagement approach?

A) A formal diagram showing all system interfaces and the data exchanged between external entities and the system under study

B) A document that describes who the stakeholders are, their roles and authority, their attitudes toward the change, and how the BA will collaborate with them

C) A risk register listing technical, financial, and schedule risks with probability and impact scores for each identified risk

D) A test plan that defines the types of testing required before a system is released to production users

Correct Answer: B

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: This describes a context diagram or system interface diagram, which is an analysis artifact, not a stakeholder engagement plan.
- Why C is incorrect: A risk register is a project management artifact; while BAs contribute to risk identification, it is not the definition of a stakeholder engagement approach.
- Why D is incorrect: A test plan is a QA artifact produced in the design or testing phase, unrelated to stakeholder planning.
- Why B is correct: The stakeholder engagement approach in BABOK KA 2 documents stakeholder identification, analysis, collaboration preferences, and the BA's engagement strategy for each stakeholder or group.

## Question 3

A BA is planning an initiative to replace a hospital's patient scheduling system. The project has a strict regulatory compliance requirement, the full scope is well understood, and the go-live date is fixed by a government mandate. Which BA approach is most appropriate?

A) Adaptive (Agile) approach with two-week sprints and an evolving backlog

B) Predictive (Waterfall) approach with fully defined requirements before design begins

C) Exploratory (Lean Startup) approach with minimum viable product releases

D) Ad hoc approach with no formal plan, relying on daily stakeholder conversations

Correct Answer: B

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Adaptive approaches are suited to evolving requirements; this project has stable, regulatory-driven scope not expected to change.
- Why C is incorrect: An exploratory approach is appropriate for highly uncertain, innovative products, not a regulated replacement system.
- Why D is incorrect: An unplanned ad hoc approach introduces unacceptable risk, especially on a project with regulatory and fixed-date constraints.
- Why B is correct: Fixed scope, regulatory requirements, stable needs, and a mandated deadline are all signals that favor a predictive approach where requirements are fully documented before design begins.

## Question 4

During a BA planning meeting, the team places a senior VP who has high organizational power but limited day-to-day interest in the project details into the "Keep Satisfied" quadrant of the stakeholder analysis matrix. Which engagement strategy is most aligned with this classification?

A) Provide comprehensive weekly status reports covering all requirement details and technical decisions

B) Exclude from all project communications to avoid wasting their time with low-priority updates

C) Provide high-level executive summaries at key milestones and escalate only significant decisions or risks

D) Assign as the primary requirements reviewer responsible for approving each individual requirement

Correct Answer: C

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Overwhelming a high-power/low-interest stakeholder with detailed communications is counterproductive and may cause disengagement or interference.
- Why B is incorrect: Excluding a high-power stakeholder risks losing their support and creates a dangerous blind spot when escalation is needed.
- Why D is incorrect: Assigning detailed review responsibility to a low-interest stakeholder is inappropriate and will likely produce poor-quality reviews or bottlenecks.
- Why C is correct: "Keep Satisfied" stakeholders (high power, lower interest) need enough information to remain supportive and to act decisively when escalated to, but should not be burdened with operational details.

## Question 5

A BA notices that three weeks into elicitation, 40% of scheduled stakeholder interviews have not been completed, and the requirements documentation is significantly behind plan. According to BABOK KA 2, which activity should the BA perform?

A) Immediately escalate to the project sponsor and request that all technical work be halted until requirements are complete

B) Skip the remaining interviews and proceed to requirements documentation using only the information collected so far

C) Conduct BA performance analysis to identify the cause of variance and update the BA plan with corrective actions

D) Begin system design using the partial requirements to avoid schedule slippage

Correct Answer: C

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Immediate escalation to halt all work is a disproportionate response before the root cause of the variance is understood.
- Why B is incorrect: Skipping interviews would leave critical requirements gaps, producing a flawed requirements baseline that causes larger problems downstream.
- Why D is incorrect: Beginning design before requirements are established is a classic systems analysis failure; it results in rework when missing requirements surface during testing.
- Why C is correct: BABOK KA 2 includes the task "Identify Business Analysis Performance Improvements," which involves analyzing variances from the plan, identifying root causes, and taking corrective action.

## Question 6

A BA has identified 14 stakeholders. Three of them are front-line customer service representatives who interact with the current system 8 hours a day, have deep knowledge of its pain points, but have no authority to approve or reject requirements. Which Power-Interest Grid quadrant do they belong in?

A) Manage Closely — because their knowledge is critical to the project

B) Keep Satisfied — because they interact with the system so frequently

C) Keep Informed — because they have high interest but low organizational power

D) Monitor — because they are not in a decision-making role

Correct Answer: C

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: "Manage Closely" is for stakeholders with both high power and high interest; these representatives have high interest but low power.
- Why B is incorrect: "Keep Satisfied" is for high-power, low-interest stakeholders; these representatives have low power, eliminating this quadrant.
- Why D is incorrect: "Monitor" is for low-power, low-interest stakeholders; front-line users who interact with the system daily clearly have high interest.
- Why C is correct: Low organizational power combined with high interest and deep domain knowledge places these stakeholders in the "Keep Informed" quadrant. They should be involved in requirements reviews, walkthroughs, and UAT.

## Question 7

Which of the following is the best description of BA governance in the context of BABOK KA 2?

A) The process the project manager uses to track budget expenditures and report variances to the steering committee

B) The set of policies defining who has authority to approve, reject, or change requirements and what process is followed when a change is requested

C) The automated workflow tool used to route requirements documents between team members for review and digital signature

D) The organizational hierarchy chart showing which business units have authority over which technical systems

Correct Answer: B

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Budget tracking and variance reporting is a project management activity, not BA governance.
- Why C is incorrect: A routing or workflow tool may support governance, but governance itself is the policy — who approves and how — not the tool that implements it.
- Why D is incorrect: An organizational hierarchy chart is a stakeholder analysis input, not a governance definition.
- Why B is correct: BA governance in BABOK KA 2 specifically addresses requirements decision authority and change control — who can approve a new requirement, who can approve a change to a baselined requirement, and what steps are followed when stakeholders disagree.

## Question 8

A BA is assigned to a startup building its first mobile application. The product vision is clear but the specific features are expected to evolve significantly as the team tests ideas with early adopters. There is no regulatory constraint. Which BA approach best fits this context?

A) Predictive — because the product vision is clearly defined

B) Adaptive — because requirements are expected to change frequently through user feedback

C) Predictive — because startups need strict documentation to attract investors

D) Neither approach is appropriate; the project should be halted until requirements are fully stable

Correct Answer: B

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: A clear product vision does not mean requirements are stable at the feature level; the scenario explicitly states features will evolve — a key signal favoring adaptive.
- Why C is incorrect: Investor documentation requirements are financial/legal concerns, not systems analysis methodology drivers.
- Why D is incorrect: Waiting for fully stable requirements would be counterproductive in an environment where requirements are expected to emerge through market testing.
- Why B is correct: Evolving requirements, frequent user feedback, no regulatory constraints, and a need for rapid iteration are the defining conditions for an adaptive BA approach.

## Question 9

A BA is updating the Stakeholder Register two months into a project and discovers that a new VP of Operations was hired last week who will be responsible for the department the system will serve. The original register did not include this role. What should the BA do?

A) Leave the register unchanged because the new VP was not a stakeholder when the project started

B) Add the new VP to the register, assess their power and interest level, assign a grid quadrant, and plan engagement accordingly

C) Submit a formal change request to add a new stakeholder, since all changes to planning documents require change control board approval

D) Transfer the previous VP's register entry to the new VP without any further analysis

Correct Answer: B

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: The Stakeholder Register is a living document that must reflect current project reality; a new VP with authority over the system's user base is a significant stakeholder who must be engaged.
- Why C is incorrect: The Stakeholder Register is a BA planning document, not a requirements artifact; it does not require change control board approval to update.
- Why D is incorrect: Transferring an entry without analysis assumes the new VP has identical characteristics to their predecessor, which cannot be assumed and may result in inappropriate engagement.
- Why B is correct: BABOK KA 2 explicitly describes the Stakeholder Register as a living document updated throughout the project. A new influential stakeholder must be added, analyzed, and engaged.

## Question 10

A BA completes a BA approach document recommending a predictive methodology. The BA's manager reviews the document and says: "You chose a methodology but you haven't described how requirements will be stored, who can access them, and how version history will be maintained." Which KA 2 task did the BA fail to complete?

A) Plan Business Analysis Governance — defining who approves requirements

B) Plan Stakeholder Engagement — identifying who needs access to requirements

C) Plan Business Analysis Information Management — deciding how requirements artifacts are stored, versioned, and accessed

D) Identify Business Analysis Performance Improvements — monitoring whether requirements are being completed on time

Correct Answer: C

Distractor Analysis:

- Why A is incorrect: Governance covers decision authority and change control for requirements content, not storage location, access control, and version history.
- Why B is incorrect: Stakeholder engagement covers how the BA collaborates with stakeholders, not how requirements documents are stored and managed.
- Why D is incorrect: Performance improvement monitoring evaluates the quality and progress of BA work; it does not address artifact storage and versioning.
- Why C is correct: BABOK KA 2 task "Plan Business Analysis Information Management" specifically addresses how requirements and other BA work products will be stored, organized, versioned, and made accessible to stakeholders — exactly the gap the manager identified.
