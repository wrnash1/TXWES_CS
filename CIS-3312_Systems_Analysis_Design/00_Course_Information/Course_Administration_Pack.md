# Course Administration Pack: CIS-3312 Systems Analysis & Design
**Target Certification:** IIBA Entry Certificate in Business Analysis (ECBA)

## Course Description
This course introduces the principles, methodologies, and tools used in systems analysis and design, with emphasis on the full Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and the business analyst role throughout it. Topics include requirements elicitation and documentation, process and data modeling, feasibility analysis, system design, testing, and implementation, all aligned with the IIBA ECBA exam blueprint and the BABOK® Guide v3 knowledge areas.
