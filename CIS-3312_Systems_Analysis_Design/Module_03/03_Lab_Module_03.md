## Case Study: Lakewood Community College — Student Services Portal

Lakewood Community College (LCC) is replacing its aging student services portal with a modern, mobile-friendly system. The new portal must support: online enrollment, financial aid status tracking, transcript requests, academic advising scheduling, and student bill payment. The existing portal was built in 2009, its documentation is incomplete, and many staff members use manual workarounds because of known system bugs.

The following stakeholders have been identified:

- Registrar's Office staff (6 people) — process enrollment and transcript requests daily using the current system
- Financial Aid advisors (3 people) — handle student aid queries and are currently using the portal plus email plus spreadsheets
- Student Accounts office (4 people) — manage billing; they find the current portal error-prone and have created a complex manual reconciliation process
- Academic advisors (8 people) — schedule appointments using a separate calendar system that is not integrated with the portal
- Students (approximately 12,000 enrolled) — primary end users; student satisfaction survey data from last year is available
- IT Operations team (2 people) — will build and maintain the new system; they need technical requirements
- The VP of Student Services — project sponsor; approves scope; attends quarterly reviews only

## Part 1: Technique Selection (42 points)

### Instructions

For each of the six elicitation situations below, identify the single most appropriate technique from this list: Interview, Facilitated Workshop, Survey/Questionnaire, Observation, Document Analysis, Prototyping.

For each situation, provide:

- The technique you recommend (1 point)
- A 3–5 sentence justification explaining why this technique is the best fit for the specific situation described (6 points)

### Situations

Situation 1: The BA wants to understand how the Registrar's Office staff actually processes an enrollment request from start to finish. Staff have described their process verbally, but their descriptions are inconsistent with each other. The process is known to include workarounds not reflected in the 2009 system documentation.

Situation 2: The BA needs to collect structured feedback from all 12,000 enrolled students about which portal features they use most, which features are most broken, and what they most want in the new system. Travel budget is limited and the academic semester is active.

Situation 3: The BA needs to align the Registrar's Office, Financial Aid, and Student Accounts teams on a single, agreed-upon definition of "enrollment hold" — a term each department currently uses differently. The three teams have conflicting requirements and the BA needs a resolution before design begins.

Situation 4: The BA has been told the current portal's user interface makes it nearly impossible for students to find the financial aid status page. Students say "the portal is confusing" but cannot articulate specific design improvements. The BA wants stakeholders to point at something specific.

Situation 5: The BA wants to understand all of the integration requirements between the new portal and the existing Banner ERP system before interviewing the IT Operations team. The ERP vendor has provided a 200-page technical specification document.

Situation 6: The BA wants to gather detailed information about the academic advising scheduling workflow from the head academic advisor. This is a complex, judgment-intensive process, and the BA expects the process to be unique to this individual's experience.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Correct technique selected for each situation (1 pt each x 6) | 6 |
| Justification references specific details from the situation and explains why the technique fits (6 pts each x 6) | 36 |

## Part 2: Interview Question Design (28 points)

### Instructions

You will conduct an interview with Maria Chen, the lead Financial Aid advisor at LCC. Maria has worked in the financial aid office for 11 years and is considered the most knowledgeable person on the current portal's financial aid features. She is also known to be skeptical of technology changes.

Write seven interview questions for this session. For each question, provide:

- The question text
- The question type: Open-ended or Closed-ended
- One sentence explaining what information this question is designed to surface

Your questions should collectively cover: current-state pain points, desired future-state capabilities, business rules, user workarounds, and any concerns Maria has about the change.

## Part 3: Workshop Agenda (30 points)

### Instructions

Design a two-hour facilitated workshop agenda to resolve the conflicting definitions of "enrollment hold" (from Situation 3 above). The session will include representatives from the Registrar's Office, Financial Aid, and Student Accounts. A BA will facilitate.

Your agenda must include:

- Session title and objectives (what the session will produce)
- Participant list with roles
- A timed agenda with at least five distinct activities
- The facilitation technique or tool used in each activity (for example: whiteboard exercise, round-robin sharing, dot voting, small group breakout)
- One ground rule for the session and a one-sentence justification for why it matters

### Grading Rubric — Part 3

| Criterion | Points |

| Participant list with roles | 3 |
| At least five timed agenda items | 10 |
| Each activity has a named facilitation technique | 7 |
| One ground rule with justification | 5 |
