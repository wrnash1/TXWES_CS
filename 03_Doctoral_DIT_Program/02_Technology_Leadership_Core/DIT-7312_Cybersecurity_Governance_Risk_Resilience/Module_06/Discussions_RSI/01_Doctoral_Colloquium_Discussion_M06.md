## DIT 7312 | SACSCOC Regular & Substantive Interaction Forum
**Lead Faculty:** Professor William Nash, Ph.D.

### Colloquium Prompt
Scholars, in this doctoral seminar, you are asked to assume the role of Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and Principal Enterprise Security Architect. Critically evaluate the following executive dilemma:

> **Scenario:** An enterprise Board of Directors is evaluating multi-million dollar capital allocations to address emerging regulatory scrutiny and operational vulnerability in the context of cyber resilience, business continuity & cyber insurance. Several board members argue that compliance check-boxes and cyber insurance provide adequate protection, while the audit committee demands quantifiable evidence of cyber resilience.

**Address the following three dimensions in your initial contribution:**
1. **Governance & Legal Analysis:** Analyze the regulatory exposure (e.g., SEC disclosure rules, NIST CSF 2.0 governance metrics) and defend the fiduciary responsibility of executive leadership.
2. **Architectural & Technical Defense:** Detail the core engineering controls and architectural principles required to mitigate this risk, contrasting passive compliance against proactive resilience.
3. **Quantitative Risk Translation:** Using FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk) principles, translate this technical risk into financial loss exposure for board presentation.

### Participation Guidelines
* **Initial Contribution (Thursday 11:59 PM CT):** Minimum 500 words, citing at least two peer-reviewed sources and one federal/international standard.
* **Peer Critique (Sunday 11:59 PM CT):** Engage with at least two colleagues. Your responses should challenge assumptions, introduce counter-examples, or probe edge-case architectural failure modes.
