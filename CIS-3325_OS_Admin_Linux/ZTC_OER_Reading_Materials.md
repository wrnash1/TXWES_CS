# Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) & Open Educational Resources (OER) Guide
## Course: CIS-3325_OS_Admin_Linux

This course is designed as a Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) curriculum. All core lecture notes, video scripts, lab activities, and practice quizzes are integrated directly within the Canvas LMS course shell.

---

## Recommended Free Study Resources & Links

To help you study and prepare for your examinations, we recommend the following free open-education resources:

1.  **Primary Open Textbook / OER Book:**
    *   [The Linux Command Line by William Shotts](https://linuxcommand.org/tlcl.php)
    *   *Study Guide:* This freely available, comprehensive textbook covers the Linux command line from fundamentals through advanced scripting. Refer to this resource for weekly reading assignments aligned with each module's topic area.

2.  **Video Study Stream Lectures:**
    *   [Linux Essentials Course by LearnLinuxTV](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLT98CRl2KxEG0QLjR-8t7k3S4I15Z1A78)
    *   *Study Guide:* Follow this playlist for weekly video lectures. LearnLinuxTV provides professional-quality Linux training aligned with real-world administration and certification objectives.

3.  **Official Vendor Documentation:**
    *   [CompTIA Linux+ Official Certification Page](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/linux)
    *   *Study Guide:* Refer to the official CompTIA Linux+ (XK0-005) exam objectives document to understand the domains, sub-domains, and performance-based question formats used on the certification exam.

4.  **Supplemental Standards-Body Portal:**
    *   [Linux Professional Institute (LPI) Learning Portal](https://learning.lpi.org/)
    *   *Study Guide:* The LPI learning portal provides free study guides, open courseware, and practice exercises that complement the CompTIA Linux+ objectives and reinforce foundational Linux administration knowledge.
