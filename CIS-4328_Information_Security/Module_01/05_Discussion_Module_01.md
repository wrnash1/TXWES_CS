## Scenario A — The Unpatched Server

A regional hospital network operates a medical imaging system that has been running the same operating system version for seven years. The vendor no longer provides security patches for that OS version, and the imaging software is not compatible with any currently supported operating system. The hospital's IT security team has identified three publicly known CVEs affecting this system, at least one of which has been actively exploited at other healthcare organizations in the past six months.

The hospital cannot take the system offline because it is used in patient care. They cannot upgrade the OS because the imaging software would break. They cannot replace the system because budget approval for the replacement is 18 months away.

In 175–225 words, respond to all three of the following:

1. Identify the vulnerability class present in this scenario and explain why this situation represents a risk rather than simply a vulnerability.
2. Recommend two specific security controls — using correct category and function labels — that the hospital should implement immediately to reduce risk while the replacement is pending. Explain how each control addresses the threat.
3. From a CompTIA Security+ perspective, what term describes the controls you recommended in point 2, given that they are substitutes for the primary control that cannot be implemented?

## Scenario B — The Suspicious Outbound Transfer

A financial services firm's SOC analyst notices an alert from the SIEM: a workstation in the accounting department has transmitted 4.2 GB of data to an IP address in an Eastern European country over the past 72 hours. The transfers occurred between 2 AM and 4 AM each night. The workstation belongs to a senior accountant who has been with the firm for 11 years. There are no other anomalies on the workstation — no malware detected, no failed logins, no privilege escalations.

In 175–225 words, respond to all three of the following:

1. Identify the most likely threat actor type and attack category consistent with this evidence. Justify your classification using the specific attributes of that threat actor type.
2. Identify which CIA Triad property has most likely been violated and explain your reasoning.
3. Describe two specific indicators of compromise from the scenario and explain what each one suggests to the investigating analyst.

## Scenario C — The Controls Gap

A startup technology company has grown from 5 to 85 employees in two years. Their current security posture consists of: a consumer-grade router with default credentials, no written security policies, no security awareness training, antivirus software on 40 percent of laptops, and a shared administrator password used by all three IT staff members.

In 175–225 words, respond to all three of the following:

1. Using the security controls framework, identify two control categories that are almost entirely absent from this organization's current posture and explain the risk created by each gap.
2. If you were their first dedicated security hire and could implement only three controls in your first 30 days, which three would you choose? For each, state the category, function, and a brief justification based on the risk it addresses.
3. The shared administrator password violates a fundamental security principle. Name that principle and explain how violating it increases both the likelihood and the impact of a security incident.

## Peer Response Guidelines

After posting your initial response, read at least two of your classmates' posts and write a substantive reply to each. Your replies should:

- Add a specific technical point, alternative perspective, or real-world example that extends the conversation.
- Respectfully challenge a classification or recommendation if you believe a different answer is more accurate, with reasoning.
- Draw a connection between their scenario analysis and a concept from the Module 01 Reading Guide.

Replies of "Great post!" or "I agree with everything you said" without substantive technical content will receive zero points for that response.
