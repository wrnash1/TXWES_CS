## 🎓 3. `03_Doctoral_DIT_Program/` — Doctor of Information Technology
Contains the complete **16-course doctoral curriculum (48 SCH)** aligned with the official proposal from Interim Dean Eddy J. Lynton, Ph.D. (CIP 11.0103):

### A. `01_Research_Core/` (9 SCH)
- **`DIT-7301_Doctoral_Inquiry_and_Evidence/`**: Quantitative and applied research methodologies for executive decision making.
- **`DIT-7302_Quantitative_Analytics_Technology_Leaders/`**: Statistical modeling, regression, and data-driven technological forecasting.
- **`DIT-7303_Qualitative_and_Design_Based_Inquiry/`**: Qualitative case study research, design science, and grounded theory in computing.

### B. `02_Technology_Leadership_Core/` (21 SCH)
- **`DIT-7310_Technology_Strategy_Governance_Ethics/`**: IT executive strategy, ethical AI principles, and corporate governance.
- **`DIT-7311_Enterprise_Architecture_Digital_Transformation/`**: TOGAF frameworks, legacy modernizations, and cloud transition architecture.
- **`DIT-7312_Cybersecurity_Governance_Risk_Resilience/`**: **Lead Course for Dr. Bill Nash.** Grounded in NIST CSF 2.0, Zero Trust (SP 800-207), C-SCRM, and SEC cyber disclosure compliance.
- **`DIT-7320_Data_AI_Responsible_Innovation/`**: NIST AI RMF 1.0, algorithmic accountability, and generative system deployments.
- **`DIT-7321_Cloud_Distributed_Systems_Infrastructure/`**: Multi-cloud reliability engineering, distributed consensus, and zero-trust infrastructure.
- **`DIT-7330_IT_Portfolio_Program_Change_Leadership/`**: Large-scale portfolio governance, change orchestration, and stakeholder management.
- **`DIT-7331_Policy_Law_Privacy_Technology/`**: Global privacy law (GDPR, CCPA), regulatory compliance, and cross-border data transfer legalities.

### C. `03_Applied_Doctoral_Project/` (18 SCH ADP Milestone Sequence)
- **`DIT-8301`**: Milestone 1 — Problem Framing & Literature Synthesis
- **`DIT-8302`**: Milestone 2 — Research Design, Ethics & Proposal Defense
- **`DIT-8303`**: Milestone 3 — Implementation & Empirical Data Collection
- **`DIT-8304`**: Milestone 4 — Analysis, Evaluation & Model Verification
- **`DIT-8305`**: Milestone 5 — Final Doctoral Manuscript & Oral Defense
- **`DIT-8306`**: Milestone 6 — Technology Impact & Industry Dissemination Practicum
