## Overview
This guide supports the instructor in managing and facilitating CSC-6361. It covers weekly rhythms, grading timelines, Canvas setup notes, and pedagogical guidance for teaching CCNP-level content to graduate students in an asynchronous online format.

---

## Weekly Instructor Rhythm

### Monday (Module Opens)
- Unlock the new module in Canvas at 12:00 AM Monday.
- Post a brief text-based "Week Kickoff" announcement in Canvas (2–3 sentences summarizing what's covered this week and any critical reminders).
- Review and respond to any unresolved previous-week discussion posts.

### Wednesday (Mid-Week Check-In)
- Monitor Canvas analytics: identify students who have not yet viewed the week's video scripts.
- Send a Canvas Inbox message to any student who has not logged in since the module opened.
- Post a mid-week "Pro Tip" announcement to the class — a brief real-world anecdote or additional context for the week's topic.

### Sunday (Deadline Day + Grading Opens)
- All Module assignments are due at 11:59 PM CST.
- Begin grading on Monday morning; aim to return all grades within 5 business days.
- Post detailed grading feedback in the Canvas SpeedGrader comment area for each student.

## Graduate-Level Teaching Notes

### Discussion Board Management
Graduate discussion boards require substantive engagement. Watch for students who are "checking the box" with short, unsupported posts. A good way to elevate the discussion: post one instructor reply per week that either challenges a claim made in the discussion or introduces a complicating real-world scenario (e.g., "One student mentioned OSPF is preferred over EIGRP for multi-vendor environments. What would you do if you inherited a legacy EIGRP network from an acquisition?").

### Lab Complexity Scaling
Labs are designed to scale from guided (Module 01) to open-ended (Module 07). In the early modules, the lab guide provides step-by-step commands. By Module 05–06, students are expected to determine the correct configuration approach from a requirements brief with minimal scaffolding. This mirrors CCNP exam lab expectations.

### Research Paper (Module 06)
Accept topic proposals via Canvas submission by the end of Module 04 (Friday, November 13). Approved topics include (but are not limited to):
- SD-WAN deployment vs. traditional MPLS: a TCO analysis
- RPKI and BGP route origin validation
- IPv6 migration strategies for enterprise networks
- Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) architecture
- Network automation with Ansible: use cases and limitations

## Grading Turnaround Expectations
*   **Quizzes:** Auto-graded by Canvas immediately upon submission.
*   **Lab Reports:** Return within 5 business days with detailed SpeedGrader comments.
*   **Discussion Boards:** Assign a grade and brief feedback note within 5 business days of module close.
*   **Research Paper:** Return within 7 business days of December 1 deadline.
*   **Capstone Final:** Return by December 16 (grades due date).

---

## Academic Integrity Monitoring
*   Use Canvas's built-in submission metadata (time stamps, IP logging) to flag anomalous submissions.
*   For lab reports, verify that Packet Tracer `.pkt` files submitted are consistent with the screenshots provided (students cannot easily fake a working topology file).
*   For discussion posts, run through a quick AI-detection heuristic: if the post reads as generic and does not reference specific module content or personal lab observations, follow up with the student.
