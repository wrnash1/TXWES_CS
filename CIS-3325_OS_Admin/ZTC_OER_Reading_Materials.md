# Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) & Open Educational Resources (OER) Guide
## Course: CIS-3325_OS_Admin

This course is designed as a Zero Textbook Cost (ZTC) curriculum. All core lecture notes, video scripts, lab activities, and practice quizzes are integrated directly within the Canvas LMS course shell.

## Recommended Free Study Resources & Links

To help you study and prepare for your examinations, we recommend the following free open-education resources:

1.  **Primary Open Textbook / OER Book:**
    *   [The Linux Command Line by William Shotts](https://linuxcommand.org/tlcl.php)
    *   *Study Guide:* Refer to this open resource for weekly reading assignments.

2.  **Video Study Stream Lectures:**
    *   [Linux Essentials Course by LearnLinuxTV](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLT98CRl2KxEG0QLjR-8t7k3S4I15Z1A78)
    *   *Study Guide:* Follow this playlist for weekly video lectures.

3.  **Official Vendor Documentation:**
    *   [Linux Professional Institute (LPI) Reference Portal](https://learning.lpi.org/)
    *   *Study Guide:* Refer to this official documentation for standard-body guidelines and specifications.
