**Audio:** "Historically, we used `ifconfig` to check IP addresses in Linux. Today, `ifconfig` is deprecated. You must learn the `ip` command suite. `ip addr show` reveals your IP, `ip route show` reveals your default gateway, and `ss` or `netstat` shows you what ports are open and listening."
